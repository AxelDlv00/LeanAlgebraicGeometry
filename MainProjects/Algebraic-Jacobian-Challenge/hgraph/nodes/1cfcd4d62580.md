The class \(\mathrm{ExistsNonconstantMapToProjInt}\, C\) asserts the
  existence of a scheme morphism \(f \colon C \to \operatorname{Proj}
  \mathbb{Z}[X_0, X_1]\) into the integral projective-line model with
  two distinct point-images \(f(x_1) \ne f(x_2)\) (no over-\(k\)
  structure required). This is the genuine one-dimensional curve
  content of \(\mathrm{ExistsNonconstantMapToP1}\): a nonconstant
  rational function of \(K\) spreads out to a rational map
  \(C \dashrightarrow \operatorname{Proj} \mathbb{Z}[X_0, X_1]\) which
  extends to an everywhere-defined morphism by the valuative criterion
  applied at the codimension-one (DVR) stalks of \(C\). Discharging it
  is currently blocked on (i) the codimension-\(\ge 2\)
  indeterminacy-locus extension over an \emph{arbitrary} base field
  \(k\) (the present smooth \(\Rightarrow\) regular \(\Rightarrow\) DVR
  pipeline only covers algebraically closed \(k\)) and (ii) the
  chart-ring identification \(\mathrm{Away}\,\mathcal A\,X_0 \cong
  \mathbb{Z}[X_1/X_0]\) needed to write down the resulting \(K\)-point
  of \(\operatorname{Proj}\mathbb{Z}[X_0,X_1]\) concretely
  (\Cref{thm:adelic_span_p1CoordAway_pow_top} supplies the
  \(\mathbb{Z}\)-span core of this identification; the base-change to
  \(k\) remains open). No instance is supplied here.
