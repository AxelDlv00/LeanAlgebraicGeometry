In the setting of \cref{lem:coverinter_rhs_iso_tilde}, the raw base-changed corner module
  \(N' = \Gamma\bigl(V', (j'_\sigma)^*((g')^*\mathcal{F})\bigr)\) satisfies
  \[
    N' \;\cong\; N \otimes_R R'
  \]
  naturally in \(\mathcal{F}\), so the right-hand side of
  \cref{lem:coverinter_rhs_iso_tilde} may equivalently be written
  \((\operatorname{Spec}\psi)_*\,\widetilde{N \otimes_R R'}\).
