Let \(x\) raise degree by one and let \(N \subseteq M\) be homogeneous. Then the
  degree-\((n+1)\) part of the image \(x \cdot N\) is exactly \(x\) of the
  degree-\(n\) part of \(N\):
  \[
    (x \cdot N) \cap \mathcal{M}_{n+1} \;=\; x \cdot (N \cap \mathcal{M}_n) .
  \]
