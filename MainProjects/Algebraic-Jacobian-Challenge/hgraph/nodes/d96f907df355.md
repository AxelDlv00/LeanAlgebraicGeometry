docstring: '**The residue-weighted degree homomorphism `deg_k : Div(X) →+ ℤ`.**

  `deg_k D = Σ_P D(P)·[κ(P):k]`, the degree the χ-ledger telescopes to

  (`chi_telescope_list`).  Bundled as an `AddMonoidHom` through

  `Finsupp.liftAddHom`, exactly as the unweighted `Scheme.WeilDivisor.degree_hom`

  is, so additivity, `deg_k 0 = 0` and `deg_k (-D) = -deg_k D` come for free.


  Over an algebraically closed base field every closed point of a smooth curve has

  `[κ(P):k] = 1` and this is the geometric degree; over a general `k` the weighting

  is what makes the degree a linear-equivalence invariant.'

noncomputable def degKHom : X.WeilDivisor →+ ℤ :=
  Finsupp.liftAddHom (fun P : X.PrimeDivisor =>
    (AddMonoidHom.mulRight ((residueDeg k P : ℤ)) : ℤ →+ ℤ))
