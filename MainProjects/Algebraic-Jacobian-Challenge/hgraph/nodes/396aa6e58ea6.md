If \(\mathfrak U\) is \v{C}ech-acyclic for \(\mathcal F\), has comparison data, and satisfies \(\bigsqcup_i\mathfrak U_i=U\), then \(H^{\prime n}(U,\mathcal F)=0\) for \(n\geq1\).
