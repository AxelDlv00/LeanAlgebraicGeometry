Let \(S\) be a two-affine cover of \(X\) and \(\mathcal F\) a sheaf of \(k\)-modules. If \(H^{\prime n}(S.\toMayerVietorisSquare.X_4, \mathcal F)\) is finite-dimensional over \(k\), then so is \(H^{n}(X, \mathcal F)\).
