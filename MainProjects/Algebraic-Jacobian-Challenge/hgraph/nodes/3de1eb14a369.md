Let \(P_1,\ldots,P_r\) be pairwise distinct points in a common affine
  Dedekind chart.  Given \(a_i\in K\) and integers \(m_i\), there exists
  \(f\in K\) such that
  \[
    v_{P_i}(f-a_i)\geq m_i\qquad(1\leq i\leq r).
  \]
