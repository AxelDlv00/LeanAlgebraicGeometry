If \((\kappa, f)\) admits a \(\mathrm{GradedHilbert}\) datum of order \(r\)
  (\Cref{def:gradedHilbert}), then \(f\) is a rational Hilbert function of order
  \(r\) (\Cref{def:ratHilb}): there are \(p \in \mathbb{Q}[X]\) and \(N\) with
  \(f(n) = [X^n]\bigl(p \cdot (1-X)^{-r}\bigr)\) for all \(n > N\).
