For every nonzero \(\eta\in\Omega_K\) there is a unique divisor
  \(\operatorname{div}(\eta)\) maximal among the divisors \(D\) for which
  \(\eta\in\Omega_K(D)\).  Consequently
  \[
    \Omega_K(D)=\{0\}\cup
      \{\eta\ne0:\operatorname{div}(\eta)\ge D\}.
  \]
