Let \(k\) be a field, \(C\) a scheme over \(\Spec k\), and \(F\) a sheaf of \(k\)-modules on \(C\). We say that \(F\) has global \(H^0\) Hom-finiteness if \(\Hom(\text{constant sheaf at }k, F)\) is finite-dimensional over \(k\).
