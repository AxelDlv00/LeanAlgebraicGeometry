For an affine open \(U \subseteq T\), the preimage \(q^{-1}(U)\) is affine and is
  canonically isomorphic to \(\Spec\) of the sections of \(\mathcal{A}_g\) over \(U\).
  This per-piece isomorphism feeds the global base-change
  iso construction.
