Let \(p : X \to S\) be a morphism of schemes, \(U' \subseteq U \subseteq S\)
  opens, \(W \subseteq p^{-1}(U')\) an open of \(X\), and
  \(u \in \Gamma(S, U)\). Then
  \(p^\sharp_{U',W}\bigl(u|_{U'}\bigr) = p^\sharp_{U,W}(u)\).
