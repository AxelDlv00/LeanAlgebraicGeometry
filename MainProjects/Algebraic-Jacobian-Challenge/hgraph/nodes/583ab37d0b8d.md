Let \(C\) be a smooth curve over an algebraically closed field \(k\), and
  suppose the \(\chi\)-ledger
  \(\chi(D)=\chi(0)+\deg_k(D)\) holds at every Weil divisor \(D\). Then for
  every \(g\in K^\times\),
  \[
    \deg\bigl(\operatorname{div}(g)\bigr)=0 .
  \]
