For a commutative ring \(R\), an ideal \(I\), a finite index type \(\iota\), and an
  \(R\)-module \(M\), the submodule \(I \cdot \top_{\iota \to M}\) equals the product
  submodule \(\prod_{\iota} (I \cdot \top_M)\) of fibrewise ideal actions.
