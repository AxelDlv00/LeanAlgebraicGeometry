Given the full four-term ledger exact sequence of
  \Cref{thm:adelic_twist_exact_sequence} (with the connecting map and
  the surjectivity at \(\check{H}^1(D')\) supplied as an explicit
  hypothesis, as noted there) between finite-dimensional \(k\)-vector
  spaces,
  \[
    \dim_k\bigl(L(D')/L(D)\bigr) - \dim_k\bigl(\Gamma(V,\struct{C}(D'))/
      \Gamma(V,\struct{C}(D))\bigr) + h^1(D) - h^1(D') = 0.
  \]
