Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and \(M, N\) two \(R\)-modules. Then
  \[
    \mathrm{Ann}_R(M) \;\subseteq\; \mathrm{Ann}_R(M \otimes_R N),
    \qquad
    \mathrm{Ann}_R(N) \;\subseteq\; \mathrm{Ann}_R(M \otimes_R N).
  \]
