For a unit \(u \in (R[\varepsilon])^{\times}\), the first components
  of \(u^{-1}\) and of \(u\) multiply to \(1\); that is, reduction mod
  \(\varepsilon\) carries \(u^{-1}\) to the inverse of the reduction of
  \(u\).
