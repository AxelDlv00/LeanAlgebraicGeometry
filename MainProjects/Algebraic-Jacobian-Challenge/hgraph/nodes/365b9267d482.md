\textit{Source: [Mumford], \emph{Abelian Varieties}, II \S 5.}
  Let \(C\) be a smooth proper geometrically integral curve over a field \(k\),
  let \(A\) be a finitely generated \(k\)-algebra and \(q : C_A \to \Spec A\) the
  constant family. Let \(\mathcal{L}\) be an invertible sheaf on \(C_A\) with
  \(H^1(C_t, \mathcal{L}_t) = 0\) at every point \(t\) of \(\Spec A\). Then every
  point of \(\Spec A\) has an open neighbourhood on which \(q_* \mathcal{L}\) is
  free of rank \(h^0(C_t, \mathcal{L}_t)\).
