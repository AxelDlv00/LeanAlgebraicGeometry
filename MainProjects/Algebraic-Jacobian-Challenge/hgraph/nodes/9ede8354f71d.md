For a vector \(v : \mathrm{Fin}\,d \to R\) and a row index \(p\), the determinant of the identity
  matrix with its \(p\)-th column replaced by \(v\) is the entry \(v_p\), with \emph{no} sign:
  \(\det\bigl((1).\mathrm{updateCol}\,p\,v\bigr) = v_p\).
