Lemma~\ref{lem:pullback_preserves_monos}, which quantifies over arbitrary
  \(\mathcal{O}_S\)-modules, is not needed for flat base change of the \v{C}ech higher direct
  images. The higher direct images are the homology of a complex whose terms are quasi-coherent,
  and by Lemma~\ref{lem:homology_iso_of_preserves_kernel} the comparison of homology with \(g^*\)
  requires only that \(g^*\) preserve one kernel in each degree --- which
  Lemma~\ref{lem:flat_pullback_kernels_qcoh} supplies. The general statement remains open, and
  Remark~\ref{rem:pullback_mono_missing_stalk_model} records why; the point here is that it is not
  on the path to Lemma~\ref{lem:cech_flat_base_change}.
