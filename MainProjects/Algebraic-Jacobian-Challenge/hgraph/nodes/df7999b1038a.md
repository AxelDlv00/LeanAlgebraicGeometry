\textit{Source: [Stacks Project], tag 090V (proposition-Auslander-Buchsbaum),
  induction-on-\(\text{pd}\) re-organisation; cf.\ Matsumura, Theorem 19.1.}

  Inductive-step variant pinning the project-internal helper used by
  \cref{thm:auslander_buchsbaum}: for a Noetherian local ring
  \((R, \mathfrak m)\), a nonzero finite \(R\)-module \(M\) of projective
  dimension \(k+1\), and the AB formula known for projective dimension
  \(\leq k\), one has
  \(\text{pd}_R(M) + \text{depth}(M) = \text{depth}(R)\).
