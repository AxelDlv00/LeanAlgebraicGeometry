Reduction modulo the maximal ideal induces a \(k\)-linear isomorphism
  \[
    \mathfrak G_P^{\ge0}/\mathfrak G_P^{\ge1}\simeq_k\kappa(P).
  \]
  In particular \(\deg_P^{\mathrm{val}}=[\kappa(P):k]=\deg P\).
