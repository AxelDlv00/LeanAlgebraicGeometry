Applying the pre-localisation hom \(\tilde\theta_{I,J}\)
  (\cref{def:gr_transition_pre}) entrywise to the universal matrix \(X^J\) yields
  the image matrix:
  \[
    (\tilde\theta_{I,J})_\ast\, X^J \;=\; M \;=\; (X^I_J)^{-1} X^I .
  \]
