For homogeneous submodules \(P, Q \subseteq M\) and any degree \(k\), intersecting
  the sum with a graded piece distributes:
  \[
    (P + Q) \cap \mathcal{M}_k \;=\; (P \cap \mathcal{M}_k) + (Q \cap \mathcal{M}_k) .
  \]
