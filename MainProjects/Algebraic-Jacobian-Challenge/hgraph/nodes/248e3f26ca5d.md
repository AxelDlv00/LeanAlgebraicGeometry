The endomorphism \(x\) carries \(N\) into the cokernel pair's upper module:
  \(x \cdot N \subseteq N' + x \cdot N\), trivially (the right summand). Thus \(x\)
  acts as zero on the cokernel subquotient \(N/(N' + x \cdot N)\).
