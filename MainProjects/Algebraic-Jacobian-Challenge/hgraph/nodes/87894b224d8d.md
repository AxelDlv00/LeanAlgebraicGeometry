docstring: '**The Galois splitting of the self-tensor.** For a finite Galois extension

  `L/K`, the `L`-algebra map `a ⊗ b ↦ (γ ↦ a * γ b)` is an isomorphism


  `L ⊗[K] L ≃ₐ[L] (Gal(L/K) → L)`.


  Scheme-theoretically: `Spec L ×_{Spec K} Spec L ≅ ⨿_{γ ∈ Gal(L/K)} Spec L`. This

  is the computation of the two pullback legs of a one-element Galois cover, i.e.

  the algebra input of the invariance half of Galois descent.'
