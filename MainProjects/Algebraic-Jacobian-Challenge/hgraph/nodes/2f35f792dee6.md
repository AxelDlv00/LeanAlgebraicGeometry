\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  For adjunctions \(\mathrm{adj}_1 : F \dashv G\) and \(\mathrm{adj}_2 : F' \dashv G\) sharing the
  right adjoint, applying \(\mathrm{adj}_1.\mathrm{homEquiv}\) to the component of the left-adjoint
  uniqueness comparison \(\mathtt{leftAdjointUniq}\,\mathrm{adj}_1\,\mathrm{adj}_2\) at an object
  \(x\) returns the unit of \(\mathrm{adj}_2\) at \(x\):
  \[
    \mathrm{adj}_1.\mathrm{homEquiv}\bigl(
      (\mathtt{leftAdjointUniq}\,\mathrm{adj}_1\,\mathrm{adj}_2).\mathrm{hom}.\mathrm{app}\,x\bigr)
      \;=\; \mathrm{adj}_2.\mathrm{unit}.\mathrm{app}\,x.
  \]
