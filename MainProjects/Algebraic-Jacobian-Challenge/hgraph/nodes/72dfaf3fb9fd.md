Let \(\varphi_{\mathrm{hom}}\) be the ring-sheaf hom underlying a flat morphism
  \(g\). Then \(\texttt{PresheafOfModules.pullback}\,\varphi_{\mathrm{hom}}\) preserves
  finite limits.
