The addition on the relative Picard quotient
  \(\Pic^\sharp_{C/k}(T) = \Pic(C \times_S T)/\pi_T^*\Pic(T)\) is the descent of
  the tensor product \cref{def:rel_tensor_obj} to isomorphism classes:
  \[
    [L] + [L'] \;:=\; [\,L \otimes L'\,].
  \]
