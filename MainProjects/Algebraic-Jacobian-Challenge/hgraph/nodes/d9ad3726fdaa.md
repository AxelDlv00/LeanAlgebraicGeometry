Given \(\chi(D) = \chi(0) + \deg D\) (the telescoped Euler
  characteristic of \Cref{thm:adelic_chi_eq_chi_zero_add_degree}),
  \[
    \deg D + \chi(0) \;\le\; \ell(D).
