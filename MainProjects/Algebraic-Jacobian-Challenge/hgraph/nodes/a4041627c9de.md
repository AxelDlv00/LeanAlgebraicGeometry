\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, \(x \in R\), and \(S\) a localisation of \(R\)
  away from \(x\) (i.e.\ \(S = R[1/x]\)). Then the image of \(x\) under the
  structure map \(R \to S\) is a unit of \(S\).
