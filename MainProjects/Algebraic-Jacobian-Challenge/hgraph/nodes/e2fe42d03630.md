In the polynomial-module structure (\cref{def:graded_polyModule}), \(X_i \cdot m =
  t_i(m)\) for all \(m \in M\). Immediate from
  \(\mathrm{polyEndHom}(t)(X_i) = t_i\) (\cref{lem:graded_polyEndHom_X}).
