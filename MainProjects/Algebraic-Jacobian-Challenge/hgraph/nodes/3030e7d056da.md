Let \(g' : X' \to X\) and \(q : E \to F\) be as in
  \cref{def:pullback_kernel_comparison}, and suppose the comparison map
  \(\kappa_{g',q}\) is an isomorphism. If \(\ker q\) is locally trivial of
  rank one, then so is \(\ker(g'^{*} q)\).
