Let \(C\) be a scheme over \(\Spec k\) and \(\mathfrak U\) a \v{C}ech-acyclic cover for \(\mathcal O_C\) with comparison data and \(\bigsqcup_i\mathfrak U_i=\top\). Then \(H^n(C,\mathcal O_C)=0\) for \(n\geq1\).
