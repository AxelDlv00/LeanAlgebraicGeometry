Under the gate \(\mathrm{ExistsNonconstantMapToP1}\, C\)
  (\Cref{def:adelic_nonconstant_map_gate}: a nonconstant \(k\)-morphism
  \(\pi \colon C \to \mathbb{P}^1_k\) exists), \(\pi\) is automatically
  a \emph{finite} morphism; equivalently, the finite-map gate
  \(\mathrm{HasFiniteMapToP1}\, C\) holds.
