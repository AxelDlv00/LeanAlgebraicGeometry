For \(a, b \in \Gamma(X, \mathcal{O}_X)\) the scalar endomorphisms compose by
  multiplication: \(\mathrm{scalarEnd}(a) \circ \mathrm{scalarEnd}(b) =
  \mathrm{scalarEnd}(a\,b)\).
