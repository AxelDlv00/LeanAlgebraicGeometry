If \(\mathfrak U\) is \v{C}ech-acyclic for \(\mathcal F\), has \v{C}ech-to-derived comparison data, and satisfies \(\bigsqcup_i\mathfrak U_i=\top\), then \(H^n(X,\mathcal F)=0\) for \(n\geq1\).
