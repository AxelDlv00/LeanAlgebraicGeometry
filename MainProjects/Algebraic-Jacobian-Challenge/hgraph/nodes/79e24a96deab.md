Let \(\F\) be a quasi-coherent \(\OO_S\)-module.  The restriction of
  \(\F\) to the open subscheme \(W_e\) --- its pullback along the open
  immersion \(W_e \hookrightarrow S\) --- admits \(e\)-presentation charts.
