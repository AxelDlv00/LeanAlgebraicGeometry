The canonical morphism \(\iota_e : S_e \to S\) is the composite of the
  closed immersion \(S_e \hookrightarrow W_e\)
  (\cref{def:stratum_immersion}) with the open immersion
  \(W_e \hookrightarrow S\); it is an immersion.
