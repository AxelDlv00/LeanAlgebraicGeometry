Let \(W\) be a reduced Jacobson scheme and let \(Z\) be separated.  If two
  morphisms \(g_1,g_2\colon W\to Z\) agree after restriction along
  \(\Spec\kappa(x)\to W\) for every closed point \(x\in W\), then \(g_1=g_2\).
