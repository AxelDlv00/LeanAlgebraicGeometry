For \(a, b \in \mathbb{N}\), the degree-\((a+b)\) monomial fraction
  \(X_0^a X_1^b / X_0^{a+b}\) in \((\mathbb{Z}[X_0,X_1]_{X_0})_0\) equals
  \((X_1/X_0)^b\).
