If \(\varphi\colon C\to D\) is a finite morphism of complete nonsingular
  curves over an algebraically closed field and
  \(E\in\operatorname{Div}(D)\), then
  \[
    \deg(\varphi^*E)=\deg(\varphi)\deg(E).
  \]
