\textit{Source: [Nitsure], \S 1 (FGA Explained Ch.~5); Serre's theorem, as in
  \Cref{lem:sectionGradedModule_fg}.}
  Let \(\pi : X \to S\) be projective carrying the very ample line bundle \(L\),
  let \(F\) be finitely presented, and let \(s \in S\).  Then the graded Hilbert
  function \(m \mapsto \dim_{\kappa(s)} \Gamma(X_s, F_s \otimes L_s^{\otimes m})\)
  (\Cref{def:hilbert_polynomial}) admits a \(\mathrm{GradedHilbert}\) datum over
  the residue field \(\kappa(s)\) (\Cref{def:gradedHilbert}).

  \emph{Status.} This is \Cref{lem:sectionGradedModule_fg} (Serre) restated in
  engine-ready form; its Lean statement is a named leaf.  Taking \(M\) to be the
  fibre section module \(M(X_s, F_s, L_s)\) and the \(d+1\) generators to be the
  homogeneous coordinate sections of the very ample embedding
  \(X_s \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}^d_{\kappa(s)}\), the content is the finite
  generation of \(M(X_s, F_s, L_s)\) over the homogeneous coordinate ring
  \(\kappa(s)[x_0, \dots, x_d]\) --- the finiteness of
  \Cref{lem:sectionGradedModule_fg} together with the degree-one coordinate
  action.
