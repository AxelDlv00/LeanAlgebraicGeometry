The object map of the push--pull functor \(G\): to an \(X\)-scheme
  \(p : Y \to X\) it assigns
  \[
    G(Y, p) \;=\; p_*\, p^*\mathcal{F},
  \]
  the pushforward along \(p\) of the pullback \(p^*\mathcal{F}\).
