The matrix endomorphism of the identity matrix is the identity morphism of
  \(\mathcal{O}_S^{\,d}\): \(\mathrm{matrixEnd}(1_{d\times d}) =
  \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{O}_S^{\,d}}\).
