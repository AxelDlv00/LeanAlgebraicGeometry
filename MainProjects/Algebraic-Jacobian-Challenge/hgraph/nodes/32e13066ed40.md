Let \(A\) be an abelian variety and let
  \(\varphi\colon C\to A\) be pointed at \(P_0\).  There is a unique
  regular morphism \(\psi\colon J\to A\) such that
  \[
    \varphi^{(g)}=\psi\circ f^{(g)}.
  \]
