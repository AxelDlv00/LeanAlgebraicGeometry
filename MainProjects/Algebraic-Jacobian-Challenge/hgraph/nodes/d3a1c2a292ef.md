Let \(p : X \to S\), \(\mathcal F\) a module sheaf on \(X\),
  \(U_0 \subseteq S\) open, and \(O_1 = O_2 \subseteq p^{-1}(U_0)\) equal
  opens of \(X\). If \(\Gamma(\mathcal F, O_1)\) is flat over
  \(\Gamma(S, U_0)\) (via \(p^\sharp\)), then so is
  \(\Gamma(\mathcal F, O_2)\).
