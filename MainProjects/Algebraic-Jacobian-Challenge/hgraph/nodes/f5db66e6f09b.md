Let \(A\) be an abelian variety and let \(\varphi\colon C\to A\) be a
  morphism.  There is a unique morphism
  \(\varphi^{(r)}\colon C^{(r)}\to A\) satisfying
  \[
    \varphi^{(r)}(P_1+\cdots+P_r)
      =\varphi(P_1)+\cdots+\varphi(P_r).
  \]
