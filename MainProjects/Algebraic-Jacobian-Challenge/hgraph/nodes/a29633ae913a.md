Let \(\bar k\) be an algebraically closed field and let \(C\) be a
  \(\bar k\)-scheme, smooth and geometrically irreducible over \(\bar k\).
  Then \(C\) has a \(\bar k\)-rational point (\cref{def:has_rational_point}).
