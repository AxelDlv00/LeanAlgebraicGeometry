\textit{Source: internal categorical construction; dual of the proven tensor base-change \cref{lem:pullback_tensor_map_basechange} (statement anchor: internal-hom base-change, Stacks Modules ch.~17).}
  Let \(f : Y \hookrightarrow X\) and \(h : Z \hookrightarrow Y\) be composable open immersions, so that
  \(h \mathbin{;} f : Z \hookrightarrow X\), let \(M \in \Scheme.\mathtt{Modules}\,X\), and let
  \(V \subseteq Z\) be open. This is the structure-ring half of the pseudofunctoriality (composition)
  law for the forward slice dual transport (\cref{lem:slice_dual_transport}): evaluated sectionwise, the
  composite unit ring-swap \(\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,(h\mathbin{;}f)\) splits into the two
  single-immersion swaps of \(f\) and of \(h\). Concretely, on a slice object \(W\) of
  \((\mathtt{Over}\,V)^{\mathrm{op}}\), writing \(W' = W.\mathrm{left}\), the value of
  \(\mathtt{sliceDualTransport}\,(h\mathbin{;}f)\,M\,V\,\varphi\) at \(W\) and a section \(z\) is
  \[
    \bigl(\mathtt{sliceDualTransport}\,(h\mathbin{;}f)\,M\,V\,\varphi\bigr).\mathtt{app}\,W\,(z)
      \;=\;
    (\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,h\,W').\mathrm{hom}\Bigl(
      (\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,f\,(h.\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,W')).\mathrm{hom}\bigl(
        \rho\bigl(\varphi.\mathtt{app}(\mathtt{Over.mk}\,((h\mathbin{;}f).\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,W.\mathrm{hom}))\,(z)\bigr)\bigr)\Bigr),
  \]
  where \(\rho = X.\mathrm{presheaf}.\mathrm{map}\bigl(\mathtt{eqToHom}\,
  (\mathtt{comp\_image}\,h\,f\,W')^{-1}\bigr)^{\mathrm{op}}\) is the reindex along the direct-image
  composition equality \((h\mathbin{;}f).\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,W' =
  f.\mathtt{opensFunctor}(h.\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,W')\) (an equality of \(\mathrm{Opens}\,X\),
  \cref{lem:scheme_modules_comp_appiso}). In words: the reindexed component \(\varphi.\mathtt{app}\) at
  the \((h\mathbin{;}f)\)-image of \(W\), retracted across \(\mathtt{comp\_image}\) and then conjugated
  successively by the codomain unit ring-swaps of \(f\) and of \(h\). There is no separate
  \(\mathtt{dualIsoOfIso}\) correction factor: the whole splitting is carried by the structure-ring
  cocycle \(\mathtt{comp\_appIso}\) (\cref{lem:scheme_modules_comp_appiso}) reindexed by
  \(\mathtt{comp\_image}\).
