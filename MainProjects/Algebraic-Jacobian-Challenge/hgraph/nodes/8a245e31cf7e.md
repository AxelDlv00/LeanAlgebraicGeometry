The degree-zero away ring \((\mathbb{Z}[X_0,X_1]_{X_0})_0\) is spanned,
  as a module over its constant subring \((\mathbb{Z}[X_0,X_1])_0\), by
  the powers of the coordinate fraction \(X_1/X_0\).
