\Cref{lem:pic0_tangent_dimension_genus} is not
  \cref{lem:pic0_relative_dimension_genus}. Two further ingredients separate them:
  smoothness of \(\Pic^0_{C/k}\) over \(k\), and the implication that a smooth
  morphism whose tangent space at a rational point has dimension \(n\) is smooth
  \emph{of relative dimension} \(n\). The second is the statement that, for a smooth
  group scheme over a field, the relative dimension is constant and computed by the
  tangent space at the identity; translation by a rational point makes the dimension
  independent of the point, and the local structure theory of smooth morphisms then
  converts the tangent-space dimension into the relative dimension of a standard
  smooth presentation.
