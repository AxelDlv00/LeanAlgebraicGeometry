For \(D \le D'\) the skyscraper section space is finite-dimensional
  with
  \[
    \dim_k \mathcal{Q}(D, D')
      \;=\; \deg(D' - D)
      \;=\; \sum_P \bigl(D'(P) - D(P)\bigr)\,\deg P .
  \]
  \emph{Status:} the one-point reduction and the upper bound \(\le \deg
  P\) at each step are \Cref{thm:adelic_local_step_dim_le_residue_degree};
  the matching lower bound (surjectivity of the residue evaluation onto
  \(\kappa(P)\), giving equality rather than \(\le\)) and the
  finite-support summation are not yet assembled at the sheaf-theoretic
  level of this proposition.
