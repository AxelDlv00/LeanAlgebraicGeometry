For \(D \le D'\) the skyscraper section space is finite-dimensional
  with
  \[
    \dim_k \mathcal{Q}(D, D')
      \;=\; \deg_k(D' - D)
      \;=\; \sum_P \bigl(D'(P) - D(P)\bigr)\,\deg P .
  \]
  \emph{Status:} the one-point reduction and the generic injected-target
  upper bound are \Cref{thm:adelic_local_step_dim_le_residue_degree}.  The
  identification of that target with \(\kappa(P)\), the matching lower bound
  (surjectivity of residue evaluation, giving equality rather than \(\le\)),
  and the finite-support summation are not yet assembled at the
  sheaf-theoretic level of this proposition.
