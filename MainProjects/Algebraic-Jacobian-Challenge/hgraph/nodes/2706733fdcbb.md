Suppose every element of \(\mathfrak G_P^{\ge-D(P)-1}\) is congruent,
  modulo \(\mathfrak G_P^{\ge-D(P)}\), to a member of
  \(\Gamma(U,\struct C(D+P))\).  Then
  \[
    \dim_k\bigl(\Gamma(U,\struct C(D+P))/\Gamma(U,\struct C(D))\bigr)
      =\deg_P^{\mathrm{val}}.
  \]
