Let \(p : X \to S\) be a morphism of schemes, \(U \subseteq S\) open,
  \(W' \subseteq W \subseteq p^{-1}(U)\) opens of \(X\), and
  \(u \in \Gamma(S, U)\). Then restricting
  \(p^\sharp_{U,W}(u) \in \Gamma(X, W)\) to \(W'\) yields
  \(p^\sharp_{U,W'}(u)\).
