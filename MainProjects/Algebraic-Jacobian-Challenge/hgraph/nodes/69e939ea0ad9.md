The chart matrix \(M^I\) of \(x\) over the chart locus \(T_I\) is the presented matrix of
  \(x\) along the locus inclusion: \(\mathrm{chartMatrix}(x, I) =
  \mathrm{presentedMatrix}(x, \iota_{T_I}, I)\).
