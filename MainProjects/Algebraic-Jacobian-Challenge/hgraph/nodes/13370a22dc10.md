Let \(k\) be an arbitrary field and \(C/k\) a smooth proper geometrically
  irreducible curve --- with \emph{no} hypothesis on \(C(k)\). Then the identity
  component \(\Pic^0_{C/k}\) carries a Jacobian witness for \(C\).
