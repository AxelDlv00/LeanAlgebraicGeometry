The \emph{Jacobian} \(\Jac(C)\) is the underlying \(k\)-scheme of the
  chosen Jacobian witness.  Equivalently, \(\Jac(C)=\Pic^0_{C/k}\) up to the
  unique isomorphism of Albanese objects.
