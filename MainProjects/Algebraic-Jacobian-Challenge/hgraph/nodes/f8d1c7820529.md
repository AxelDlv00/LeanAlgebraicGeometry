\textit{Source: [Kleiman], ``The Picard scheme'', \S 2, Def.~df:aPf
  (the absolute Picard functor).}
  The \emph{absolute Picard functor} of \(C\) over \(k\): the group-valued
  presheaf
  \[
    (\Sch/k)^{op} \longrightarrow \Ab, \qquad
    T \longmapsto \Pic(C \times_k T),
  \]
  on the iso-class carrier, with the tensor-product group law of
  \cref{lem:rel_pic_sharp_groupoid} and the pullback structure maps of
  \cref{thm:pullback_natural}. In Kleiman's notation this is \(\Pic_X\)
  of df:aPf (with \(X = C \times_k -\) over the test scheme). The Lean
  declaration bundles the object/morphism data directly; the helpers
  \texttt{PicSharp.functorial} (group-hom form of the structure maps) and
  \texttt{PicSharp.presheaf} (a re-export of the bundle) belong to this
  node. It compares onto the relative functor of \cref{def:rel_pic_sharp}
  via \cref{lem:relpic_quotient_comparison}.
