Let \(C\) be a smooth proper curve over an algebraically closed field
  \(\bar k\). The degree map of \ref{def:divisor_degree} is a group
  homomorphism
  \[
    \deg \colon \mathrm{Div}(C) \longrightarrow \mathbb Z,
    \qquad
    \deg(D_1 + D_2) \;=\; \deg(D_1) + \deg(D_2),
    \qquad
    \deg(-D) \;=\; -\deg(D).
  \]
