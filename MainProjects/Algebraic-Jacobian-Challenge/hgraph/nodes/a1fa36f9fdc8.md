\textit{Source: Stacks Project, Tag 01BG, \texttt{lemma-pullback-quasi-coherent}.}
  Let \(g : Y \to X\) be a morphism of schemes and \(\mathcal{F}\) a quasi-coherent
  \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-module. Then \(g^*\mathcal{F}\) is a quasi-coherent
  \(\mathcal{O}_Y\)-module.
