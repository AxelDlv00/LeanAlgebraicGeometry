The Euler characteristic \(\chi(D) = \ell(D) - i(D)\) is additive in the
  degree:
  \[
    \chi(D') - \chi(D) = \deg(D' - D)
    \quad\text{for } D \le D',
    \qquad\text{hence}\qquad
    \chi(D) = \deg D + 1 - g .
  \]
