The separated case above suffices for the curve/Jacobian application, where the
  structure morphisms in play are separated. The general (quasi-compact,
  quasi-separated) case of Stacks Tag 02KH is obtained from the separated case by base
  change functoriality of the spectral sequence
  \cref{lem:cech_to_derived_pushforward_ss}: the flat morphism \(g\) induces, by
  \(g^*\)-exactness, a comparison of the entire \(E_2\) page
  \(g^*\check{H}^p(\mathfrak{U}, R^q f_*\mathcal{F}) \to
  \check{H}^p(\mathfrak{U}', R^q f'_*\mathcal{F}')\), whose termwise input is the affine
  base change of \cref{def:cech_complex_base_change_iso}; an isomorphism on \(E_2\)
  yields an isomorphism on the abutment \(R^{p+q} f_*\), promoting the separated
  conclusion to the general one. This downstream lift is recorded as a remark only; it
  is not needed for the present development.
