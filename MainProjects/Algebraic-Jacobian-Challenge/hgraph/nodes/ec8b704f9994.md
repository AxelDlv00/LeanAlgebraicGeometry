  \nThe ambient hypotheses of `Jacobian C` are `SmoothOfRelativeDimension 1`, `IsProper`\n\

  \ follow: a smooth proper geometrically\n  integral curve over a field need not\
  \ have a `k`-rational point (a genus-`0`\n  conic without rational points, or the\
  \ standard genus-`2` example over `ℚ`). It is\n  available over an algebraically\
  \ closed field only\n  (`Albanese.hasRationalPoint_of_isAlgClosed`).\n\nSo this\
