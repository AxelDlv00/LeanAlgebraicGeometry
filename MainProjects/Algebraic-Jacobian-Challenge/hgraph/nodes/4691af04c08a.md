\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  Sheafification of presheaves of modules is a left-exact reflector, hence preserves
  finite limits. On the genuine scheme site, where the requisite
  \(\operatorname{HasSheafify}\)/weak-sheafification instances hold, the
  \(\operatorname{PreservesFiniteLimits}\) instance is supplied by typeclass
  inference.
