Let \(M\) be a sheaf of \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-modules and \(V \subseteq X\)
  an open with a trivialisation \(M|_V \cong \mathcal{O}_V\). Then
  \(\Gamma(M, V)\) is an invertible \(\Gamma(X, V)\)-module.
