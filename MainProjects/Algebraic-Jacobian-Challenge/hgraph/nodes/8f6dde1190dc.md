\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, \(x, y \in R\), and \(R[1/x]\) the localisation
  away from \(x\). Then \(R[1/x] \otimes_R R[1/y]\) is a localisation of \(R\)
  away from \(x y\); equivalently it realises \(R[1/(xy)]\).
