Let \(P\) be a closed point of a scheme \(C\).  Define
  \[
    [P]=
    \begin{cases}
      1\cdot\langle P,h\rangle,&
        \text{if }h:\operatorname{coheight}(P)=1,\\
      0,&\text{otherwise.}
    \end{cases}
  \]
