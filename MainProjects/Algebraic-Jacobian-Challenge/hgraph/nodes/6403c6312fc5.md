For a prime divisor \(Y\) and \(n \in \mathbb Z\), the positive part of the
  one-point-supported divisor \(n \cdot Y\) is \(\max(n, 0) \cdot Y\):
  \((n \cdot Y)_0 = \max(n, 0) \cdot Y\).
