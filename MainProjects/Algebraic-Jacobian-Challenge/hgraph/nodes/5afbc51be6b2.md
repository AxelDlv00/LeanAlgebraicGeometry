For any morphism of schemes \(\varphi : T' \to T\), the inverse-image functor on opens
  \(\mathrm{Opens.map}\,\varphi : \mathrm{Opens}(T) \to \mathrm{Opens}(T')\),
  \(U \mapsto \varphi^{-1}U\), is \emph{final}.
