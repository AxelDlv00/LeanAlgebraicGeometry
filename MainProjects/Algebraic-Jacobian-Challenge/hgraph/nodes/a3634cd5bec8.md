Let \(U \subseteq C\) be a nonempty affine open with coordinate ring
  \(A_U = \Gamma(U, \struct{C})\). Then \(A_U\) is a Dedekind domain and
  the canonical map \(A_U \hookrightarrow K\) exhibits \(K\) as the field
  of fractions of \(A_U\).
