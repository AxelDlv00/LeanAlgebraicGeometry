Let \(k\) be a field, \(C\) a scheme over \(\Spec k\), and \(F\) a sheaf of \(k\)-modules. Assume wholespace \(H^0\) Hom-finiteness holds for \(F\). Then \(H^0_{\Module}(F)\) is finite-dimensional over \(k\).
