Suppose \(D \le D'\) pointwise and \(D' - D\) is supported on the
  overlap \(V = U_0 \cap U_1\) (off \(V\), \(D\) and \(D'\) agree). Then
  \[
    L(D') \cap \Gamma(V, \struct{C}(D)) \;=\; L(D).
  \]
