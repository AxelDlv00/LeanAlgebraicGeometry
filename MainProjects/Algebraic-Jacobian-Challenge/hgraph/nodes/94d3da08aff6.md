For a prime divisor \(P\) and \(m \in \mathbb{Z}\),
  \(\mathfrak G_P^{\ge m} = \{ f \in K : f = 0 \text{ or } v_P(f) \ge m
  \}\), an additive subgroup of \(K\) (for \(m \le 0\) the fractional
  ideal \(\mathfrak m_P^{-m} \subset \struct{C,P}\) viewed inside \(K\)).
