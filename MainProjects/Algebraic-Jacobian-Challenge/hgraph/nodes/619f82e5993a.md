With the notation of \cref{def:presentation_basic_open}, the entry ideal of
  the restricted presentation is the image of \(I(\psi)\) under restriction:
  \(I(\psi|_{D(f)}) = I(\psi) \cdot \Gamma(X, D(f))\).
