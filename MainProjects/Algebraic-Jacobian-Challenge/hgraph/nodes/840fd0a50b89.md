For \(i, j \in \{0, 1\}\), the coordinate fraction \(X_j/X_i\) is the
  element of the degree-zero away ring \((\mathbb{Z}[X_0,X_1]_{X_i})_0\)
  with numerator \(X_j\) and denominator \(X_i\) (both homogeneous of
  degree \(1\)).
