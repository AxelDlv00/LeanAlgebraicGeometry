Let \(R\) be a commutative ring. The inclusion
  \(a \mapsto a + 0 \cdot \varepsilon\) (a ring homomorphism
  \(R \to R[\varepsilon]\)) induces a group homomorphism
  \(R^{\times} \to (R[\varepsilon])^{\times}\) on unit groups.
