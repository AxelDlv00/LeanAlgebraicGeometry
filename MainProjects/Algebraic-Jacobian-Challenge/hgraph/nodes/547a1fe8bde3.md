\textit{Source: internal categorical construction; no external reference.}
  Let \(\mathrm{adj} : L \dashv R\) be an adjunction between categories \(C\) and \(D\), let
  \(P \in C\), \(B \in D\), and let \(g : L.\mathrm{obj}\,P \to B\). Then transporting \(g\) to its
  unit form and collapsing through the counit recovers \(g\):
  \[
    L.\mathrm{map}\bigl(\mathrm{adj}.\mathrm{unit}.\mathrm{app}\,P \mathbin{;} R.\mathrm{map}\,g\bigr)
      \mathbin{;} \mathrm{adj}.\mathrm{counit}.\mathrm{app}\,B \;=\; g.
  \]
  This is the round-trip identity of the hom-set adjunction equivalence
  (\(\mathrm{adj}.\mathrm{homEquiv}\)) read at the morphism \(g\): the left factor is the
  unit-presentation \(\mathrm{adj}.\mathrm{homEquiv}\,g\) of \(g\)'s transpose, and post-composing the
  counit is the inverse transpose, so the composite is the identity round trip. Stated abstractly over
  arbitrary \([\mathtt{Category}]\) so that its applications cross the \(\mathtt{SheafOfModules}\)
  sheafification seam by definitional unification rather than a keyed rewrite.
