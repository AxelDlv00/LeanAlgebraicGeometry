Let \(A, B \in \operatorname{Mat}_{d\times d}(R)\) and
  \(M \in \operatorname{Mat}_{d\times e}(R)\) over a commutative ring, with
  \(\det A\) a unit. Then
  \[
    \bigl(B^{-1} A\bigr)\,\bigl(A^{-1} M\bigr) \;=\; B^{-1} M.
  \]
