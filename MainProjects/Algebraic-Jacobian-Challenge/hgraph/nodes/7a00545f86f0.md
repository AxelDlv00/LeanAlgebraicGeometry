docstring: 'Finiteness over `k` of the global Hom group `((constantSheaf _).obj (ModuleCat.of
  k k) ⟶ F)`,

  that is, of the global sections `Γ(C.left, F)`. For `F = O_C` on a proper geometrically
  integral

  `k`-curve this holds because `Γ(C, O_C) ≃ k` by Stein factorization on a proper
  geometrically

  connected curve; see `instIsHModuleHomFinite_toModuleKSheaf`.


  The condition is imposed on global sections only, and deliberately not open by open.
  By the Yoneda,

  free-module and sheafification adjunctions, the per-open Hom group

  `((presheafToSheaf _ _).obj ((yoneda ⋙ ModuleCat.free k).obj U) ⟶ F)` is `≃ₗ[k]
  Γ(U, F)`, and on a

  proper smooth `k`-curve `Γ(U, O_C)` is *not* finite over `k` for a proper affine
  open `U`: for the

  standard cover of `P¹_k` by two copies of `A¹_k` one has `Γ(Uᵢ, O_{P¹}) = k[t]`,
  which is infinite

  dimensional over `k`.'
