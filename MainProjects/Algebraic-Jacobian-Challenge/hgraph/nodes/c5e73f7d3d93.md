Let \(g' : X' \to X\) and \(q : E \twoheadrightarrow F\) be as in
  \cref{def:pullback_kernel_comparison}, with \(q\) an epimorphism. Then the
  comparison map \(\kappa_{g',q} : g'^{*}(\ker q) \to \ker(g'^{*} q)\) is an
  epimorphism.
