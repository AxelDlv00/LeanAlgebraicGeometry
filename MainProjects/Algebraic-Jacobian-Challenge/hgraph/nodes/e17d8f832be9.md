docstring: '**Augmentation of a representable free presheaf onto the structure presheaf.**


  The canonical map `freeYoneda V ⟶ unit` (= `O_X`) corresponding under the free–Yoneda
  hom

  bijection to the unit section `1 ∈ O_X(V)`. Concretely it sends the free generator
  over a

  `W ⊆ V` to `1 ∈ O_X(W)`. This is the per-summand component of the Čech augmentation

  `K(𝒰)_0 ⟶ O_𝒰`.


  Project-local: Mathlib has neither `freeYoneda` nor its augmentation.'
