Let \((\psi, \pi)\) be a matrix presentation of \(M\) by \(e\) generators
  and \(\mathfrak p \subseteq R\) a prime.  Then
  \(I(\psi) \subseteq \mathfrak p\) if and only if the fiber rank of \(M\)
  at \(\mathfrak p\) equals \(e\).
