\Cref{lem:curve_hypothesis_gap} and \cref{lem:curve_rational_point_algClosed} state the
  same conclusion under different hypotheses, and the pair delimits what is open. Over an
  arbitrary base field the statement is false, so it admits no proof and the witness built
  on it rests on an inconsistent hypothesis --- its consequences are vacuously true. Over
  an algebraically closed field the same statement is a theorem. So the open question is
  not the existence of a rational point in general, but which of two formulations is
  claimed over an arbitrary \(k\): representability of \(\Pic^\sharp_{C/k}\) with
  \(C(k) \neq \emptyset\) as a hypothesis, or representability of the \'etale
  sheafification \(\Pic_{(C/k)\et}\), which needs no section
  (\cref{sec:fga_pic_setup}). The remaining obligations of the witness ---
  \cref{thm:pic0_smooth}, \cref{thm:pic0_proper},
  \cref{lem:pic0_relative_dimension_genus} and \cref{lem:pic0_isAlbanese_all_points} ---
  are of the other kind: true statements awaiting proofs.
