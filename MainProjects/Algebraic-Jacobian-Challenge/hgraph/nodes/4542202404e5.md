The structural morphism \(\mathbb{P}(\{0,\dots,d\}; S) \to S\) is projective
  carrying the Serre twist \(\mathcal{O}(1)\); the witnessing closed immersion
  is the identity.  In particular the projective-with predicate is
  non-vacuous.
