Let \(\varphi\colon C\to A\) satisfy \(\varphi(P_0)=0_A\).  For every
  regular morphism \(\psi\colon J\to A\),
  \[
    \varphi=\psi\circ\iota_{P_0}
      \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
    \varphi^{(g)}=\psi\circ f^{(g)}.
  \]
