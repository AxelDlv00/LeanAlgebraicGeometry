Right-composition of a rational map \(F \colon X \dashrightarrow Y\) with a morphism
  \(g \colon Y \to Z\) enlarges the domain of definition:
  \(\mathrm{Dom}(F) \subseteq \mathrm{Dom}(F \circ g)\).
