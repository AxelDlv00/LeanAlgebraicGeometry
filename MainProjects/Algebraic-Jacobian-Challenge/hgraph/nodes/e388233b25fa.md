\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  For composable open immersions \(f : X \hookrightarrow Y\) and \(g : Y \hookrightarrow Z\) and an open
  \(U \subseteq X\), the structure-ring isomorphism of the composite factors as the
  \(\mathtt{eqToIso}\)-twisted product of the two factor isomorphisms,
  \[
    (f \mathbin{;} g).\mathtt{appIso}\,U
      \;=\; Z.\mathrm{presheaf}.\mathrm{mapIso}\bigl(\mathtt{eqToIso}\,
        (\mathtt{comp\_image}\,f\,g\,U)\bigr)^{\mathrm{op}}
        \mathbin{;} g.\mathtt{appIso}\bigl(f.\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,U\bigr) \mathbin{;} f.\mathtt{appIso}\,U,
  \]
  the cocycle reconciling the composite direct image \((f\mathbin{;}g)\,''\,U\) with the iterated one
  \(g\,''\,(f\,''\,U)\). This is the structure-ring half of the open-immersion pseudofunctor coherence.
