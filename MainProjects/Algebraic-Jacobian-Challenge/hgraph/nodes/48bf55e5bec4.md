Let \(I\) be an ideal of a commutative ring \(R\), let \(M\) be an
  \(R\)-module and let \(N \subseteq M\) be a submodule with
  \(N + IM = M\). Then \(N + I^{n}M = M\) for every \(n \ge 0\).
