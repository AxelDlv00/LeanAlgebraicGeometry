The class \(\mathrm{P1HasLaurentChartData}\, k\) asserts that the
  concrete projective line \(\mathbb{P}(\mathrm{ULift}(\mathrm{Fin}\, 2);
  \operatorname{Spec} k)\) carries Laurent chart data on its standard
  two-chart affine cover (the preimages of \(D_+(X_0), D_+(X_1)\) under
  the affine projection to the integral model, formalised
  unconditionally as the cover square \(\mathrm{p1CoverSquare}\)); the
  outstanding content is the chart-ring computation
  \(\Gamma(V_0) = k \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} (\mathrm{Away}\, X_0)_0 = k[x]\)
  for the pullback model. The \(\mathbb{Z}\)-linear span core of this
  computation is \Cref{thm:adelic_span_p1CoordAway_pow_top}; what
  remains is the characteristic-free base change
  \(\Gamma(V_0) \cong k \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Away}\,\mathcal
  A\,X_0\) (reading \(V_0\) as the affine pullback
  \(\operatorname{Spec} k \times_{\top} D_+(X_0)\)) together with the
  overlap identities \(V_0 \sqcap V_1 = D(x) = D(y)\), \(xy = 1\). No
  instance of the class is supplied here.
