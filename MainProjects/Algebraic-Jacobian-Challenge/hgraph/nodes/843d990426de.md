title: The opens-preimage functor of a continuous map is final

For any continuous map \(\varphi : T \to T'\), the preimage functor
  \(U \mapsto \varphi^{-1}(U)\), from open subsets of \(T'\) to open subsets of \(T\), is a
  final functor.
