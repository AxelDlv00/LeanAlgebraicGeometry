For a field \(k\), a finite index set \(\iota\), and any localisation \(A\) of
  \(\mathrm{MvPolynomial}\,\iota\,k\) at a maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m\), the Krull
  dimension of \(A\) equals \(\#\iota\).
