For \(1\le r\le g\), the morphism
  \[
    f^{(r)}\colon C^{(r)}\longrightarrow W^r
  \]
  is birational.  In particular, \(W^g=J\), and
  \(f^{(g)}\colon C^{(g)}\to J\) is a birational surjective morphism.
