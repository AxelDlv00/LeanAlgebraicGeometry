The class \(\mathrm{ExistsNonconstantMapToP1}\, C\) asserts the
  existence of a \(k\)-morphism \(\pi \colon C \to \mathbb{P}^1_k\)
  taking two distinct values \(\pi(x_1) \ne \pi(x_2)\). This is the
  honest remaining kernel of the finite-map gate: any nonconstant
  rational function on \(C\) (which exists since \(K/k\) has
  transcendence degree one) extends to such a \(\pi\) by the valuative
  property of the dimension-one regular local rings of \(C\)
  (\Cref{lem:adelic_curve_stalk_dim_le_one}); no instance is supplied
  here. This gate is further reduced, decoupling it from all over-\(k\)
  and relative-projective-space bookkeeping, to the strictly cleaner
  gate \(\mathrm{ExistsNonconstantMapToProjInt}\, C\)
  (\Cref{def:adelic_nonconstant_projint_gate} and
  \cref{lem:adelic_nonconstant_projint_bridge}).
