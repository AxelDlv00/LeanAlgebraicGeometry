Every \(R\)-linear map \(A : R^m \to R^n\) decomposes as
  \(A = \sum_{i,j} A_{i,j} \cdot E_{i,j}\), where \(A_{i,j} = (A\,(\text{Pi.single}\,j\,1))_i\)
  is the corresponding matrix entry.
