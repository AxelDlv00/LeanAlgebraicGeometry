\textit{Source: internal categorical construction; no external reference.}
  The \(\mathtt{app}\) component of \(\mathtt{sliceDualTransport}\,f\,M\,V\) --- the forward per-slice
  dual-transport family of \cref{lem:slice_dual_transport} --- evaluated at a dual section
  \(\varphi\) equals its explicit forward-transport formula. On a slice object \(W\) of
  \((\mathtt{Over}\,V)^{\mathrm{op}}\), writing \(W' = W.\mathrm{left}\), the value at a section
  \(z\) is
  \[
    \bigl(\mathtt{sliceDualTransport}\,f\,M\,V\,\varphi\bigr).\mathtt{app}\,W\,(z)
      \;=\;
    (\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,f\,W').\mathrm{hom}\Bigl(
      \varphi.\mathtt{app}\bigl(\mathtt{Over.mk}\,(f.\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,W.\mathrm{hom})\bigr)\,(z)\Bigr),
  \]
  i.e. the reindexed component \(\varphi.\mathtt{app}\) at the \(f\)-image of \(W\) followed by the
  codomain unit ring-swap \(\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,f\,W'\) (\cref{def:dual_unit_ring_swap}, the
  \((f.\mathtt{appIso}\,W').\mathrm{hom}\) conjugation); the intervening \(\mathtt{restrictScalars}\)
  leg is a definitional identity on carriers. This is the forward \(\mathtt{rfl}\) bridge exposing
  the forward leg in clean form --- the mirror of \cref{lem:slice_dual_transport_inv_app_apply} ---
  and is the sectionwise tool that closes the pushforward-flank residual of
  \cref{lem:presheafdual_pullback_comparison_restrict}.
