Fix \(P\in C(k)\), put \(J=\Pic^0_{C/k}\), and let
  \(f^P(Q)=[\mathcal O_C(Q-P)]\).  For every pointed morphism
  \(\varphi\colon C\to A\) to an abelian variety there is a unique
  group-scheme homomorphism \(\psi\colon J\to A\) such that
  \(\varphi=f^P\circ\psi\).
