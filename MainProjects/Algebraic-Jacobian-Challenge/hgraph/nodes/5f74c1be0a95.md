Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(M\) an \(A\)-module.  We say \(M\) is
  \emph{generically free over \(A\)} if there exists \(f \in A\), \(f \ne 0\),
  such that the localisation \(M_f\) is a free \(A_f\)-module.
