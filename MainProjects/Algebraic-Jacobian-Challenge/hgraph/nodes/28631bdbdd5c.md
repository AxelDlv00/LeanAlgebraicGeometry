Let \(\mathcal G\) be a quasi-coherent \(\OO_X\)-module admitting
  \(e\)-presentation charts, and \(\iota : Z_e \to X\) the closed immersion
  of the rank-\(e\) stratum.  Then \(\iota^*\mathcal G\) is flat over
  \(Z_e\).
