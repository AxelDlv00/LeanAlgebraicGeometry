\textit{Source: [Stacks Project], tag 00OD (lemma-regular-ring-CM).}
  Every regular Noetherian local ring is Cohen--Macaulay: if \((R, \mathfrak{m})\) is
  regular local of Krull dimension \(d\), then \(\text{depth}(R) = d = \dim(R)\).
