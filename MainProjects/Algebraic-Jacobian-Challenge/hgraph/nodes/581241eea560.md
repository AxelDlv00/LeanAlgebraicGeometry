In the setting of
  \cref{lem:rigidity_eqOn_saturated_open_to_affine}, let \(x\) be a closed point of
  \(U\).  Then \(f\) and the map \((x',y)\mapsto f(x_0,y)\) agree after restriction
  along the residue-field point \(\Spec\kappa(x)\to U\).
