For a sheaf \(F\) of \(k\)-modules and an affine Mayer--Vietoris square,
  define
  \[
    \operatorname{H1Cok}_S(F)
      =\Gamma(U_0\cap U_1,F)/
        \operatorname{im}\bigl(\Gamma(U_0,F)\oplus\Gamma(U_1,F)
          \xrightarrow{\,f_0-f_1\,}\Gamma(U_0\cap U_1,F)\bigr).
  \]
