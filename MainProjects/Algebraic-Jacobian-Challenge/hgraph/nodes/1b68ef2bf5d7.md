The inverse-image functor \(g^{-1}\) on presheaves of modules is exact, hence
  preserves finite limits. \emph{Proof sketch.} On presheaves the inverse image is
  computed by a filtered colimit over the comma category of the structure map; filtered
  colimits are exact in an abelian category, so \(g^{-1}\) preserves both finite limits
  and finite colimits.
