For a \(\mathbb{N}\)-graded ring \(A\) of finite type over its degree-zero
  part \(A_0\), the structural morphism
  \(\operatorname{Proj} A \to \operatorname{Spec} A_0\) is proper (separated,
  universally closed, and of finite type).
