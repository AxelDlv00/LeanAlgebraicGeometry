Let \(D \le D'\) agree except at a prime divisor \(P \in U\) with
  \(D'(P) = D(P) + 1\). Then
  \[
    \Gamma(U, \struct{C}(D)) \;=\; \Gamma(U, \struct{C}(D')) \cap
      \mathfrak G_P^{\ge -D(P)}.
  \]
