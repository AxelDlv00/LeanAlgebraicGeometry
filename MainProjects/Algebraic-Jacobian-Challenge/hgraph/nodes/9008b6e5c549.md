The \emph{Serre twist} \(\mathcal{O}(m)\) on \(\mathbb{P}(n; S)\) is the
  pullback of the twisting sheaf of \Cref{def:serre_twist} along the
  projection \(\mathbb{P}(n; S) \to \operatorname{Proj} \mathbb{Z}[X_i]\).
