Let \(k\) be a field, \(C\) a Grothendieck site, \(X\) an object of \(C\), and \(F\) a sheaf of \(k\)-modules. Then there is a canonical \(k\)-linear equivalence
  \[
    H^{\prime 0}(X, F) \;\simeq_k\; \Hom(\text{sheafified representable at }X,\; F).
  \]
