Let \(X\) be a scheme. A two-affine Mayer--Vietoris square on \(X\) consists of two open subschemes \(U_1, U_2\) of \(X\) such that \(U_1\), \(U_2\), and \(U_1 \cap U_2\) are affine, together with the hypothesis \(U_1 \cup U_2 = X\).
