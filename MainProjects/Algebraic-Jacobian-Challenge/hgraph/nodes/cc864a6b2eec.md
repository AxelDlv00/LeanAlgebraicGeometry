The group of Weil divisors of \(X\) is the free abelian group on its prime
  divisors:
  \[
    \operatorname{Div}(X)
      =\bigoplus_{Y\in X^{(1)}}\mathbb Z\cdot Y.
  \]
  Thus a divisor is a finite formal sum \(D=\sum_Y n_Y Y\).  It is
  \emph{effective} when every \(n_Y\geq 0\).
