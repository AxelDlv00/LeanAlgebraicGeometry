For every abelian variety \(A\) and every morphism
  \(\varphi\colon C\to A\) with \(\varphi(P_0)=0_A\), there exists a unique
  regular morphism \(\psi\colon J\to A\) such that
  \[
    \varphi=\psi\circ\iota_{P_0}.
  \]
