  dimension** — `dim R ≤ dim_{κ(R)} (m/m²)`, with no regularity hypothesis and no\n\
  condition on the residue field.\n\nTwo mathlib facts, composed:\n\n* `ringKrullDim_le_spanFinrank_maximalIdeal`\
  \ (Krull's height theorem) bounds the\n  dimension by the minimal number of generators\
  \ of the maximal ideal;\n* `IsLocalRing.spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace`\
  \ (Nakayama)\n  identifies that count with the `κ(R)`-dimension of the cotangent\
  \ space.\n\nRegularity is exactly the case of *equality*\n(`IsRegularLocalRing.iff_finrank_cotangentSpace`),\
  \ which is the only form this\nproject had; the inequality holds always, and it\
  \ is the direction an upper bound on\ndimension needs. The converse inequality is\
  \ **false** without regularity — a cusp\n`k[x,y]/(y²-x³)` localised at the origin\
  \ has embedding dimension `2` and dimension\n`1` — which is why the `≥` half of\
  \ a dimension computation still needs a regular\npoint and this one does not."
