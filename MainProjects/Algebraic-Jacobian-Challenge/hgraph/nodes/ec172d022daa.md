Let \(f : R \to A\), \(g : A \to D\) and \(h : R \to D\) be ring
  homomorphisms with \(g \circ f = h\). Then for any matrix \(M\) over \(R\),
  applying \(f\) then \(g\) entrywise equals applying \(h\) entrywise:
  \(g_\ast(f_\ast M) = h_\ast M\).
