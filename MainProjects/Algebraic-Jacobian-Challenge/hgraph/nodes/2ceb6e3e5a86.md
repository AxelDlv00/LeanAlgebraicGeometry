For sheaves of \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-modules \(A, B, C\) there is a canonical
  associativity isomorphism
  \[
    (A \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} B) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} C
      \;\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\;
      A \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} (B \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} C)
  \]
  of sheaves of \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-modules.
