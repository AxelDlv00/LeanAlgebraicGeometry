Let \(\mathcal{F}\) be an \(\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spec} R}\)-module and \(g \in R\); write
  \(\iota : \widehat{D(g)} \hookrightarrow \operatorname{Spec} R\) for the open immersion of the basic
  open and \(\operatorname{e} = \operatorname{modulesOverBasicOpenEquivalence} g\)
  (Definition~\ref{def:modules_over_basicOpen_equivalence}) for the engine equivalence. Then the
  inverse of the engine carries the over-picture object \(\mathcal{F}.\operatorname{over} D(g)\) — a
  sheaf of modules over the localized site \((\operatorname{Opens} \operatorname{Spec}
  R).\operatorname{over} D(g)\), where the quasi-coherence presentation lives — to the honest subspace
  restriction \(\mathcal{F}.\operatorname{restrict} \iota\):
  \[
    \operatorname{e}.\operatorname{inverse}.\operatorname{obj}
      \bigl(\mathcal{F}.\operatorname{over} D(g)\bigr)
      \;\cong\; \mathcal{F}.\operatorname{restrict} \iota
    \qquad \text{in } \widehat{D(g)}.\mathrm{Modules}.
  \]
  This is the object-level corollary of the engine equivalence: it identifies the over-picture
  restriction with the subscheme restriction \(\mathcal{F}.\operatorname{restrict} \iota\) over the open
  \(\widehat{D(g)} \subseteq \operatorname{Spec} R\). The further transport to the affine
  \(\operatorname{Spec} R_g\) along the identification \(D(g) \cong \operatorname{Spec} R_g\) — landing on
  \(\mathcal{F}_{(g)} = \operatorname{modulesRestrictBasicOpen} g\, \mathcal{F}\)
  — is performed downstream inside
  step B4 (Lemma~\ref{lem:presentation_modulesRestrictBasicOpen}), not here.
