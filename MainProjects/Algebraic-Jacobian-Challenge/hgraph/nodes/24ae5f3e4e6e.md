\textit{Source: [Kleiman], ``The Picard scheme'', \S 2, Def.~df:Pfs
  (the quotient defining \(\Pic_{X/S}\)).}
  The componentwise coset-quotient maps
  \(\Pic(C \times_k T) \to \Pic(C \times_k T)/H_T\) assemble into a natural
  transformation from the absolute functor of \cref{def:abs_pic_functor} to
  the relative functor of \cref{def:rel_pic_sharp}, and each
  component is a surjective group homomorphism.
