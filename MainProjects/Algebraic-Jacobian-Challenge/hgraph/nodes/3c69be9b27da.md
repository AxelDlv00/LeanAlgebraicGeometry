\textit{Source: internal categorical construction; no external reference.}
  The underlying map of the codomain unit ring-swap \(\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,f\,W'\)
  (\cref{def:dual_unit_ring_swap}) is the \(\mathrm{hom}\)-direction of the open-immersion structure-ring
  isomorphism. For a section \(x \in X.\mathrm{presheaf}\bigl(f.\mathtt{opensFunctor}\,W'\bigr)\),
  \[
    (\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\,f\,W').\mathrm{hom}\,(x)
      \;=\; (f.\mathtt{appIso}\,W').\mathrm{hom}.\mathrm{hom}\,(x).
  \]
  This is the workhorse pointwise action exposing \(\mathtt{dualUnitRingSwap}\) as a plain
  change-of-rings ring map; it is the rewrite that drives the forward-transport sectionwise reductions.
