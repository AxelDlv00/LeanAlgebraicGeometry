A \emph{line bundle on $P=C\times_S T$} is a locally trivial module of rank
  one on $P$.  Thus the type of line bundles on the product is
  \[
    \mathrm{OnProduct}(C\to S,T\to S)
    :=\{\mathcal M\in P.\mathrm{Modules}\mid
          \mathcal M\text{ is locally trivial of rank one}\}.
  \]
  Isomorphism classes of these objects are the usual elements of
  $\Pic(P)$; the tensor-product group structure is not needed for the
  constructions below.
