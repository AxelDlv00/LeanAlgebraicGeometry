\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  For adjunctions \(\mathrm{adj}_1 : F \dashv G\) and \(\mathrm{adj}_2 : F' \dashv G\) sharing the
  right adjoint, the left-adjoint uniqueness comparison satisfies the counit triangle
  \[
    (\mathtt{leftAdjointUniq}\,\mathrm{adj}_1\,\mathrm{adj}_2).\mathrm{hom}.\mathrm{app}\,(G.\mathrm{obj}\,x)
      \mathbin{;} \mathrm{adj}_2.\mathrm{counit}.\mathrm{app}\,x
      \;=\; \mathrm{adj}_1.\mathrm{counit}.\mathrm{app}\,x.
  \]
