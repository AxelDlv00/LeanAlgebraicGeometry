For a standard-smooth \(R\)-algebra \(S\), the module of K\"ahler differentials
  \(\Omega_{S/R}\) is free as an \(S\)-module (Stacks 00T7(2)), with basis the family
  \(\{ds_i\}\) of the standard-smooth presentation.
