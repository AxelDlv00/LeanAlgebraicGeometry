Let \(C\) be a scheme over \(\Spec k\) and \(\mathfrak U\) a \v{C}ech-acyclic cover for \(\mathcal O_C\) with comparison data. Then \(H^{\prime n}(\bigsqcup_i\mathfrak U_i,\mathcal O_C)=0\) for \(n\geq1\).
