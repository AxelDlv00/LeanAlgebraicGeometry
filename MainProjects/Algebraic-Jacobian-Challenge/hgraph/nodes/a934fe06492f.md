The divisor class group is
  \[
    \operatorname{Cl}(X)
      =\operatorname{Div}(X)/
        \operatorname{im}\bigl(K(X)^\times
          \xrightarrow{\operatorname{div}}\operatorname{Div}(X)\bigr).
  \]
