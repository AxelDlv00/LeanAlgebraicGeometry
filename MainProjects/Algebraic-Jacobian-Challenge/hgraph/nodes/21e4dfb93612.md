\textit{Source: internal categorical construction; no external reference.}
  Let \(f : Z \hookrightarrow Y\) and \(g : Y \hookrightarrow X\) be composable open immersions
  (\emph{general} --- not tied to the chart \(j\) nor to any equality of opens) and
  \(A \in \Scheme.\mathtt{Modules}\,X\). The restriction-to-pullback comparison
  \(\mathtt{restrictFunctorIsoPullback}\) (\cref{def:restrictfunctorisopullback}) is pseudonatural
  across the \emph{literal} composite \(f \mathbin{;} g\): it carries the restriction-world
  composition isomorphism \(\mathtt{restrictFunctorComp}\,f\,g\)
  (\cref{lem:scheme_modules_restrictfunctorcomp}) onto the pullback-world composition isomorphism
  \(\mathtt{pullbackComp}\,f\,g\). Concretely, the leading
  \(\mathtt{restrictFunctorIsoPullback}\) factors of the two flanks intertwine
  \(\mathtt{restrictFunctorComp}\,f\,g\) with \(\mathtt{pullbackComp}\,f\,g\), the head being the
  \emph{bare} \(\mathtt{restrictFunctorComp}\,f\,g\) (not the chart reindexing
  \(\mathtt{restrictCompReindex}\) of \cref{def:restrictcompreindex}), so that \emph{no}
  \(\mathtt{pullbackCongr}\)/\(\mathtt{restrictFunctorCongr}\) congruence legs occur. The
  \(\mathrm{hom}\)-component is recorded separately as
  \(\mathtt{restrictFunctorIsoPullback\_comp\_general\_hom}\) --- the \(.\mathrm{hom}\) of this iso
  identity.
