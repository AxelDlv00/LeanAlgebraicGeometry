title: Restriction of a quasi-coherent module to an open is quasi-coherent

Let \(\iota_V : V \hookrightarrow X\) be the open immersion of an open subscheme \(V\) of a
  scheme \(X\), and let \(\mathcal{F}\) be a quasi-coherent \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-module. Then the
  pullback \(\iota_V^*\mathcal{F}\) is quasi-coherent on \(V\).
