Under the finite-map gate \(\mathrm{HasFiniteMapToP1}\, C\)
  (\Cref{thm:adelic_exists_finiteMorphismToP1}), the chart-data gate
  \(\mathrm{P1HasLaurentChartData}\, k\)
  (\Cref{def:adelic_P1HasLaurentChartData}), the ambient \(\mathrm{Ext}\)
  hypotheses, and the Čech-to-derived comparison gate
  \(\HasCechToHModuleIso\, (\toModuleKSheaf C)\, S.\cover\) supplied
  \emph{for every} affine Mayer--Vietoris square \(S\) of \(C\) (rather
  than only for one fixed square, because the finite-map keystone
  produces an \emph{existential} pulled-back cover that cannot be named
  in advance), the first cohomology of the structure sheaf is finite
  over \(k\):
  \[
    \Module.\Finite\ k\ \bigl(\HModule\, k\, (\toModuleKSheaf C)\, 1\bigr).
  \]
  Consequently \(\genus C = \finrank_k
  \bigl(\HModule\, k\, (\toModuleKSheaf C)\, 1\bigr)\) is a genuine
  natural number.
