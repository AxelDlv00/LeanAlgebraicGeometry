If \(C\) is smooth and proper with geometrically integral fibres, then
  \[
    \Module.\Finite\ k\ \bigl(\HModule\, k\, (\toModuleKSheaf C)\, 1\bigr).
  \]
  Thus \(\genus C\) is the dimension of this finite-dimensional vector
  space.
