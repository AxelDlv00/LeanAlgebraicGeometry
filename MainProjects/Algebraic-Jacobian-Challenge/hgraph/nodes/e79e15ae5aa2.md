Under the identification of Weil differentials with rational Kähler
  differentials, the evaluation pairing is the finite sum
  \[
    (\alpha,\eta)\longmapsto
      \sum_P\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(P)/k}
        \operatorname{res}_P(\alpha_P\eta).
  \]
  In particular the sum vanishes on principal adeles.
