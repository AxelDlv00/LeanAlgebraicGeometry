For \(y_2 \in H^{\prime n}(X_2, \mathcal F)\) and \(y_3 \in H^{\prime n}(X_3, \mathcal F)\),
  \[
    \fromBiprod(y_2, y_3) \;=\; \mathrm{restriction}_{X_2 \to X_1}(y_2) \;-\; \mathrm{restriction}_{X_3 \to X_1}(y_3).
  \]
