Let $g:T'\to T$ be a morphism over $S$, so that
  $\pi_{T'}=\pi_T\circ g$.  Let
  \[
    g_C:C\times_S T'\longrightarrow C\times_S T
  \]
  be the induced base-change morphism.  For every $\mathcal O_T$-module
  $\mathcal N$ there exists an isomorphism
  \[
    g_C^*\pi_T^*\mathcal N
      \;\cong\;\pi_{T'}^*g^*\mathcal N.
  \]
