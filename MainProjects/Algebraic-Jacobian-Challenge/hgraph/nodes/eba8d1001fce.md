Fix \(x_0\in X\), and let \(s_{x_0}\colon Y\to X\times Y\) be the section
  \(y\mapsto(x_0,y)\) of \(p_2\).  Then \(s_{x_0}\circ p_2\) is the endomorphism
  \((x,y)\mapsto(x_0,y)\) of \(X\times Y\).
