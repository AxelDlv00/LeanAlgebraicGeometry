For any scheme morphism \(\varphi : T' \to T\) and index type \(I\), there is a canonical
  isomorphism of sheaves of \(\mathcal{O}_{T'}\)-modules
  \[
    \varphi^{*}\bigl(\mathcal{O}_T^{\,\oplus I}\bigr)
      \;\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\;
    \mathcal{O}_{T'}^{\,\oplus I}.
  \]
