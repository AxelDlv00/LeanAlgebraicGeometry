  of the object.**

  If `α.app Z''` is invertible and `Z ≅ Z''`, then `α.app Z` is invertible.


  **This is mathlib''s `CategoryTheory.NatTrans.isIso_app_iff_of_iso`, in its `.mpr`
  direction**, and

  it is kept only as a named one-directional abbreviation because it reads better
  at the single use

  site below.  An earlier revision of this docstring called it "the bridge that lets…"
  and gave it a

  four-line proof of its own; a fresh-context reviewer found the mathlib lemma, so
  the proof is now

  that lemma rather than a duplicate of it.  Do not treat this as project infrastructure.


  What it is *for*: the per-σ decomposition `pushPull_sigma_iso` is an isomorphism
  of *objects*, and

  the obligation is `IsIso` of a *map*, so the decomposition cannot be substituted
  directly — that

  gap is the "groups agree ≠ maps agree" error, and this lemma is what bridges it.'
