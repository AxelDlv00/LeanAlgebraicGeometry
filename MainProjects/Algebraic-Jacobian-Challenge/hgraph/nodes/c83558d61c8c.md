Fix a field of constants \(k\). Write \(\ell(D) = \dim_k L(D)\),
  \(h^1(D) = \dim_k \check{H}^1_{\mathrm{ff}}(D)\), and
  \(\chi(D) = \ell(D) - h^1(D)\)
  for the Euler characteristic.
