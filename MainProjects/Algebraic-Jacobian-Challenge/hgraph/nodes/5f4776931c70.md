Suppose the structure morphism \(\pi : \mathrm{Gr}(r,d) \to \Spec \mathbb{Z}\)
  (\cref{def:gr_to_specZ}) satisfies the \emph{existence} part of the valuative
  criterion. Then \(\pi\) is proper.
