Project-local construction of the \(R_x\)-balanced additive bilinear map underlying the
  reverse map \(\mathtt{stalkTensorRev}\) of \cref{def:stalk_tensor_desc}. For a fixed
  \(A\)-section \(a\) over \(U\), the inner leg \(\mathtt{revInnerLeg}\) sends a
  \(B\)-section \(b\) over \(V\) to \(\mathrm{germ}_x\bigl(a|_{U\cap V} \otimes
  b|_{U\cap V}\bigr)\); descending it through the \(B\)-stalk colimit gives
  \(\mathtt{revInner}\), the outer leg \(\mathtt{revOuterLeg}\) packages this additively in
  \(a\), and descending through the \(A\)-stalk colimit gives the additive bilinear map
  \(\mathtt{revBihom}\colon A_x \times B_x \to (A \otimes^{p} B)_x\).
