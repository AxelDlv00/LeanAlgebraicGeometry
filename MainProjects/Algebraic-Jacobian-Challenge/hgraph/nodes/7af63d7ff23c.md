Let \(S\) be standard-smooth of relative dimension \(1\) over a field
  \(k\), and let \(q\) be a prime of \(S\). Every localisation \(S_q\)
  of \(S\) at \(q\) satisfies \(\operatorname{ringKrullDim} S_q \le 1\).
