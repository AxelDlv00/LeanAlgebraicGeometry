Let \(Y\in X^{(1)}\) have generic point in a nonempty integral open
  \(U\).  The diagram
  \[
  \begin{tikzcd}
    \mathcal O_{U,Y|_U} \arrow[r] \arrow[d, "\sim"']
      & K(U) \arrow[d, "\sim"] \\
    \mathcal O_{X,Y} \arrow[r] & K(X)
  \end{tikzcd}
  \]
  commutes.
