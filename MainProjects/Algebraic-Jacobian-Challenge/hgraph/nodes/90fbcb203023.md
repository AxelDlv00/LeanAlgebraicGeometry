With \((C, \mathcal U)\) as in \cref{def:Scheme_cechCochain_OC}, define the \(n\)-th \v{C}ech cohomology to be the \(n\)-th homology of the \v{C}ech cochain complex. The result lives in the category of \(k\)-modules.
