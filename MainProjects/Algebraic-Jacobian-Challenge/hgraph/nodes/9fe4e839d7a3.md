The candidate inverse \cref{def:gr_glueRestrictionInv} followed by the restricted
  \(j\)-th projection \(\iota_i^{*}(\pi_j)\) recovers the \(j\)-th component
  \cref{def:gr_glueChartComponent}:
  \[
    \mathrm{s}_i\ \circ\ \iota_i^{*}(\pi_j)
      \;=\; \mathrm{s}_i^{(j)} .
  \]
