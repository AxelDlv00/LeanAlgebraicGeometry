\textit{Project-local.}
  Fix an open \(V \subseteq X\) and a one-element multi-index \(\sigma : \mathrm{Fin}\,1 \to I\).
  The \(\sigma\)-coordinate of the canonical augmentation
  \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{can}} = \operatorname{sectionCechAugV}\mathcal{U}\,\mathcal{F}\,V\) of
  Definition~\ref{def:sectionCechAugV}, namely the composite with the projection
  \(\operatorname{Pi.\pi}(\cdots)\,\sigma\) onto the \(\sigma\)-factor of
  \(\prod_\tau \Gamma\bigl(\bigcap_l(U_{\tau l}\cap V),\mathcal{F}\bigr)\), is the plain presheaf
  restriction of \(\mathcal{F}\) along the inclusion of intersection opens
  \(\bigcap_l(U_{\sigma l}\cap V) \subseteq V\):
  \[
    \varepsilon_{\mathrm{can}} \cdot \operatorname{Pi.\pi}(\cdots)\,\sigma
      \;=\;
    \mathcal{F}\bigl(\,\textstyle\bigcap_l(U_{\sigma l}\cap V)\subseteq V\,\bigr)
      \;:\; \Gamma(V,\mathcal{F}) \longrightarrow
      \Gamma\bigl(\textstyle\bigcap_l(U_{\sigma l}\cap V),\mathcal{F}\bigr).
  \]
  This is the degree-\(0\) sibling of the per-leg naturality of
  Lemma~\ref{lem:coreIso_comm_leg}, and is the augmentation compatibility used by
  Lemma~\ref{lem:cechSection_contractible} for its degree-\(0\) contracting identities.
