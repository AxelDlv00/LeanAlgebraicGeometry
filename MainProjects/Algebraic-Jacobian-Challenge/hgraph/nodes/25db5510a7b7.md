Let \(S\) be a locally noetherian scheme and \(\F\) a finitely presented
  \(\OO_S\)-module.  Every point \(s \in S\) lies in an \(e\)-presentation
  chart for \(\F\) with \(e\) the point rank of \(\F\) at \(s\).
