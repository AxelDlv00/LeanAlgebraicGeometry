A \emph{Jacobian witness} for \(C\) consists of a single \(k\)-scheme \(J\),
  an abelian-variety structure on \(J\), a proof that \(J\) is smooth of
  relative dimension \(g(C)\), and, for every \(P\in C(k)\), an Albanese
  structure on \((C,P,J)\).
