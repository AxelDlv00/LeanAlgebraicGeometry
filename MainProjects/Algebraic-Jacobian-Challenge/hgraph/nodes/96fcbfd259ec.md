For \(b \in \mathbb{Z}[X^J]\), the comorphism \(\delta_{I,J}\)
  (\cref{def:gr_diagonalRingMap}) acts on the right factor by the pre-localisation
  transition hom:
  \[
    \delta_{I,J}(1 \otimes b) \;=\; \tilde\theta_{I,J}(b)
  \]
  (\cref{def:gr_transition_pre}).
