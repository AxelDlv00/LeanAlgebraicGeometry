\textit{Source: [Stacks Project], Modules on Ringed Spaces, Lemma~0B8K.}
  The unit object \(\mathcal{O}_X = \mathtt{SheafOfModules.unit}\,X.\mathtt{ringCatSheaf}\)
  is \(\otimes\)-invertible: taking \(N = \mathcal{O}_X\), the substrate unitor gives
  \(\mathcal{O}_X \otimes_X \mathcal{O}_X \cong \mathcal{O}_X\), so the \(\exists N\)
  criterion \cref{def:scheme_modules_isinvertible} is met. This is the invertibility of
  the identity element of the relative Picard group law.
