\textit{Project-local: enlargement of Definition~\ref{def:affine_cover_system}.}
  The \emph{enlarged affine cover system} for \(\operatorname{Spec} R\) is the instance of
  Definition~\ref{def:basis_cov_system} whose basis \(\mathcal{B}\) is \emph{all} affine opens of
  \(\operatorname{Spec} R\) and whose admissible coverings \(\mathrm{Cov}\) are the standard finite covers
  \(i \mapsto D(g_i)\) whose union \(\bigcup_i D(g_i)\) is affine. It generalises
  Definition~\ref{def:affine_cover_system}, which uses only the distinguished opens \(D(f)\) as its basis.
  Its three proof fields are discharged by: \(\mathrm{faces\_mem}\) --- each face
  \(\bigcap_k D(g_{\sigma k}) = D(\prod_k g_{\sigma k})\) is distinguished, hence affine
  (Lemma~\ref{lem:isAffineOpen_specBasicOpen}), so lies in the enlarged basis
  (Lemma~\ref{lem:affine_faces_mem}); \(\mathrm{surj\_of\_vanishing}\) by
  Lemma~\ref{lem:affine_surj_of_vanishing_affine}; and \(\mathrm{injective\_acyclic}\) by the cover-agnostic
  family-form injective {\v C}ech-acyclicity (Lemma~\ref{lem:injective_cech_acyclic}).
