Let \(S\) be a two-affine cover of \(X\) and \(\mathcal F\) a sheaf of \(k\)-modules. Then
  \[
    \dim_k H^{n}(X, \mathcal F) \;=\; \dim_k H^{\prime n}(S.\toMayerVietorisSquare.X_4, \mathcal F).
  \]
