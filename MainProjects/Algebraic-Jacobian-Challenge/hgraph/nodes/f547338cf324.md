For \(g : S' \to S\), the pullback of \(\mathcal{O}(m)\) on
  \(\mathbb{P}(n; S)\) along the base-change morphism
  \(\mathbb{P}(n; S') \to \mathbb{P}(n; S)\) is canonically isomorphic to
  \(\mathcal{O}(m)\) on \(\mathbb{P}(n; S')\).
