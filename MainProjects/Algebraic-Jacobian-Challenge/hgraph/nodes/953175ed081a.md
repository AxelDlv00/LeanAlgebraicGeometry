For a smooth curve \(C\) over an algebraically closed field \(k\) and every
  Weil divisor \(D\) on \(C\),
  \[
    \deg_k(D) = \deg(D),
  \]
  the right-hand side being the unweighted degree of \Cref{def:divisor_degree}.
