Let \(B\) be an algebra over a commutative ring \(R\), let \(N\) be a
  \(B\)-module which is flat as an \(R\)-module, and let
  \(g : N \to N'\) be a localization of \(B\)-modules at a submonoid
  \(p \subseteq B\). Then \(N'\) is flat as an \(R\)-module.
