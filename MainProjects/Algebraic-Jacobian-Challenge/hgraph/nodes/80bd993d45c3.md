The composite
  \(R^{\times} \to (R[\varepsilon])^{\times} \to R^{\times}\)
  of \cref{def:dual_number_units_inl} followed by
  \cref{def:dual_number_units_fst} is the identity. In particular
  reduction mod \(\varepsilon\) on unit groups is surjective.
