title: Flatness over the base is stable under pullback
type: tex
updated: '2026-07-16T21:14:30'

Let \(\mathcal G\) be a quasi-coherent \(\OO_T\)-module flat over \(T\),
  and \(g : T' \to T\) a morphism of schemes.  Then \(g^*\mathcal G\) is
  flat over \(T'\).
