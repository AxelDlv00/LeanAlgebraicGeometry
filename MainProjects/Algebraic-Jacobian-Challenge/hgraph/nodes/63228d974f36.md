For a prime \(\mathfrak p \subseteq R\) with residue field
  \(\kappa(\mathfrak p)\), the \emph{fiber rank} of \(M\) at
  \(\mathfrak p\) is
  \(\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)} \bigl(\kappa(\mathfrak p) \otimes_R M\bigr)\).
