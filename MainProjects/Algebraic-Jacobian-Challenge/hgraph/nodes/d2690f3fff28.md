For a submersive presentation \(P\) of an \(R\)-algebra \(S\), the family of
  cotangent classes of the relations \(i \mapsto [\,P.\mathrm{relation}\,i\,]\) in the
  cotangent module of \(P\) is \(S\)-linearly independent.
