For a Weil divisor \(D \in \mathrm{Div}(X)\), the degree of the positive part
  equals the sum of the capped coefficients:
  \(\deg\bigl((D)_0\bigr) = \sum_Y \max(n_Y, 0)\), where \(D = \sum_Y n_Y \cdot Y\).
