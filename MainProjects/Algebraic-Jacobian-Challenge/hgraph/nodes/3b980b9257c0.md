\textbf{The theorem of the cube is not needed for these corollaries.} The Milne \S I.1--I.3
  corollaries used here --- additivity over a product (Corollary~1.5,
  \cref{lem:hom_additivity_over_product}) and ``a pointed regular map of abelian varieties is a
  homomorphism'' (Corollary~1.2, \cref{lem:av_regular_map_is_hom}) --- follow directly from the
  Rigidity Theorem~1.1 (our \cref{thm:rigidity_lemma}), with no appeal to the theorem of the
  cube. The theorem of the cube (Milne \S I.5) enters Milne's theory only later, for
  projectivity, the seesaw/square principle, dual abelian varieties, polarizations, and the
  Poincar\'e sheaf. For Route~A: the Albanese universal property that the positive-genus arm
  needs (Milne Proposition~6.1/6.4, \S III.6) is \emph{also} cube-free, resting on Theorem~3.2
  (\cref{thm:rational_map_to_av_extends}) together with Corollaries~1.2 and~1.5 --- which is
  precisely why Theorem~3.2 and Corollary~1.2 are retained in this chapter as Route-A inputs.
