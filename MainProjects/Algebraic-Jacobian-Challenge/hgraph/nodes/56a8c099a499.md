Let \(A\) be a domain and \(M\) a finite \(A\)-module every element of which
  is annihilated by some non-zero element of \(A\).  Then \(M\) is generically
  free over \(A\): indeed some localisation \(M_f\) with \(f \ne 0\) vanishes.
