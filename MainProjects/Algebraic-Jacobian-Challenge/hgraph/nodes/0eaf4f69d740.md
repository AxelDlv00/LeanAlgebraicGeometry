Let \(L\) be an \emph{invertible} sheaf of \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-modules. Then the
  external direct sum \(\bigoplus_{m \ge 0} \Gamma(X, L^{\otimes m})\) admits a
  commutative semiring structure.
