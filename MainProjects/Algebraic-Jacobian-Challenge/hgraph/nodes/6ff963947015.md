For \(f \in K^\times\) with \(v_P(f) \ge 0\), \(f\) lifts to the
  discrete valuation ring \(\struct{C, P}\), i.e.\ \(f = a\) for some
  \(a \in \struct{C, P}\) (viewed in \(K\)).
