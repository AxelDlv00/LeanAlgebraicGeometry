For each smooth proper geometrically irreducible curve \(C\) over \(\bar k\)
  there is a Pic\(^0\) bundle (\cref{def:pic0_bundle}), supplied by the A.3
  row (\cref{chap:Picard_Pic0AbelianVariety}): the identity component
  \(\Pic^0_{C/k}\) of the representable Picard scheme, equipped with its
  group-object, proper, smooth, and geometrically irreducible structure.
