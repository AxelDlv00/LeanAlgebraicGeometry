Let \(V\) and \(W\) be complete varieties with points \(v_0\) and \(w_0\), and let
  \(A\) be an abelian variety.  Every morphism \(h\colon V\times W\to A\) with
  \(h(v_0,w_0)=0\) has a unique expression
  \[
    h=f\circ p+g\circ q,
  \]
  where \(f(v_0)=0\) and \(g(w_0)=0\).
