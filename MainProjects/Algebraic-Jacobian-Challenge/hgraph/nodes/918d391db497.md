In terms of the coface maps of \(Y_{\mathcal{U}, F}\), the Čech
  differentials in degrees \(0\) and \(1\) are
  \[
    d^0 = \delta_0 - \delta_1, \qquad\qquad
    d^1 = \delta_0 - \delta_1 + \delta_2.
  \]
