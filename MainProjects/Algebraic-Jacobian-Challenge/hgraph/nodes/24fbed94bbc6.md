For any scheme \(X\), define the coefficient degree of
  \(D=\sum_Y n_YY\) by
  \[
    \deg(D)=\sum_Y n_Y.
  \]
  If \(X\) is a nonsingular curve over an algebraically closed field, this
  is the usual degree because every closed point has residue degree one.
