Let \(A\) be an abelian variety over \(k\).  For every morphism
  \(f \colon C \to A\) satisfying \(P \circ f = \eta_A\), there exists a
  unique homomorphism of group schemes \(g \colon \Jac(C) \to A\) such that
  \[
    f = \alpha_P \circ g.
  \]
