For a Noetherian local ring \(R\) with \(\text{spanFinrank}(\mathfrak m) = 0\), the maximal
  ideal collapses to \(0\), so \(R\) is a field and hence an integral domain. This is the
  base case of \cref{lem:isDomain_of_regularLocal}.
