Let \(A\) be a Noetherian localisation of \(\mathrm{MvPolynomial}\,\iota\,k\) at a
  maximal ideal, and let \(rs\) be a regular sequence in \(A\) of length \(b\). Then
  \(\mathrm{ringKrullDim}\,(A/(rs)) + b = \#\iota\).
