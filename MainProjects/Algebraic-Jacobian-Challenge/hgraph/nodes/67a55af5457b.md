Let \(C\) be a complete nonsingular curve over an algebraically closed field
  \(k\), and let
  \(f\in K(C)\setminus k\).  There is a finite morphism
  \(\varphi_f\colon C\to\mathbb P^1_k\) inducing the inclusion
  \(k(f)\subseteq K(C)\), and
  \[
    \operatorname{div}(f)
      =\varphi_f^*([0]-[\infty]).
  \]
