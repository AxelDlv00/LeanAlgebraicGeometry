Let \(C\) be a scheme over \(\Spec k\) and \(S\) a two-affine cover of \(C\). Then
  \[
    \dim_k H^{n}(C, \mathcal O_C) \;=\; \dim_k H^{\prime n}(S.\toMayerVietorisSquare.X_4, \mathcal O_C).
  \]
