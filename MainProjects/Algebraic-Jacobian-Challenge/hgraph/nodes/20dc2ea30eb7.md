\textit{Source: Stacks Project, Schemes, Tag 01I8, \texttt{lemma-quasi-coherent-affine}
  (proof).}
  The unconditional quasi-coherent statement
  \([{\,}\mathrm{IsQuasicoherent}\,\mathcal{F}{\,}] \Rightarrow \mathcal{F} \cong
  \widetilde{\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec} R, \mathcal{F})}\) reduces, through
  Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_iso_tilde_sections}, to the single instance
  \([{\,}\mathrm{IsQuasicoherent}\,\mathcal{F}{\,}] \Rightarrow
  \operatorname{IsIso}(\operatorname{fromTilde\Gamma})\) on the affine base \(\operatorname{Spec} R\).
  This is the content of Stacks Tag 01I8, and it is discharged here by the
  \emph{section-localization} route (Route B):
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Keystone (P1).} For a quasi-coherent \(\mathcal{F}\) and \(f \in R\), the
    section-restriction map \(\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F}) \to \Gamma(D(f), \mathcal{F})\) is a
    localization at the powers of \(f\): Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_section_isLocalizedModule}. This is
    proved by the sheaf-axiom equalizer route in degrees \(0/1\). Refine to a finite presentation
    cover \(\{D(g_j)\}\) (Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_finite_presentation_cover}); present
    \(\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F})\) and \(\Gamma(D(f), \mathcal{F})\) as the kernels of the {\v C}ech
    differentials on \(\{D(g_j)\}\) and \(\{D(g_j f)\}\) (the degree-\(0/1\) sheaf axiom,
    Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_section_equalizer}); localize the global equalizer at \(f\) using exactness of
    localization (Lemma~\ref{lem:localized_module_map_exact_mathlib}); and match it term-by-term with
    the \(D(f)\)-cover equalizer through the per-tile localizations
    (Lemma~\ref{lem:tile_section_localization}), giving the kernel comparison
    (Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_section_kernel_comparison}). The route is non-circular because every
    ``sections-localize'' input sits on a presented tile, never on the global object. It is the only
    load-bearing input.
  \item \emph{Assembly.} The distinguished-open component of
    \(\operatorname{fromTilde\Gamma}\) is the localization lift
    (Lemma~\ref{lem:fromTildeGamma_mathlib}) of that very section-restriction map along the
    structure-sheaf localization; with the keystone in hand both sides are localizations of
    \(\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F})\) at the powers of \(f\), so the component is an isomorphism
    (Lemma~\ref{lem:isLocalizedModule_linearEquiv_mathlib}). Since the forgetful functor reflects
    isomorphisms and \(\{D(f)\}\) is a basis (Lemma~\ref{lem:forget_reflectsIso_mathlib}), the counit
    is an isomorphism: Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_isIso_fromTildeGamma} (equivalently, the essential-image
    criterion Lemma~\ref{lem:isIso_fromTildeGamma_iff_mathlib}). This is the unconditional instance
    that upgrades Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_iso_tilde_sections} to its quasi-coherent form.
  \end{enumerate}
  Route B drops the global-generation step, the kernel-quasi-coherence step, the finite-limit
  preservation of \(\widetilde{(-)}\), and the restriction-to-basic-open base-change chain from the
  critical path; the presentation-form discharge
  Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_iso_tilde_sections_of_presentation} (built on the Mathlib counit-iso
  Lemma~\ref{lem:isIso_fromTildeGamma_of_presentation}) remains available for any \(\mathcal{F}\)
  carrying a global presentation.

  \emph{Route A (dormant fallback).} The same instance can alternatively be reached through the
  \emph{global-generation} route (Hartshorne~II.5.14--17, Stacks Tag 01I8): globally generate
  \(\mathcal{F}\) (Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_global_generation}, fed by the localized-sections core
  Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_localized_sections}); observe its kernel is again quasi-coherent using the
  exactness of \(\widetilde{(-)}\) (Lemma~\ref{lem:tilde_preserves_kernels},
  Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_kernel_qcoh}); and feed the two generating families to
  Lemma~\ref{lem:isIso_fromTildeGamma_of_genSections}, packaged as
  Lemma~\ref{lem:isIso_fromTildeGamma_of_quasicoherent} and
  Lemma~\ref{lem:qcoh_iso_tilde_sections_of_genSections}. This route is strictly longer and relies on
  the finite-limit preservation of \(\widetilde{(-)}\) and a base-change chain that are not available
  in the ambient library; its blocks below are kept as dormant axiom-clean assets but are off the
  live 01I8 critical path. It is also distinct from the
  \emph{module-gluing} route suggested by the Stacks proof above (gluing the local modules \(M_i\)
  along the cocycle \(\psi_{ij}\), Algebra \texttt{lemma-glue-modules}), which is not pursued, as it
  would require effective faithfully-flat descent of modules.
