docstring: '**Uniform vanishing from a uniform chart count.**  If above some weighted-degree

  threshold `b` the Čech count is exact at every divisor, then `h¹` vanishes uniformly
  above

  `b`.  The quantifier structure is the one cluster P''s consumers take, and the hypothesis
  is

  now a statement about chart dimensions rather than about a connecting homomorphism.


  This is deliberately stated with the finiteness binders as an instance-quantified

  hypothesis, matching `ResidueField.UniformlyBoundedVanishing`''s shape, so that
  a consumer

  proving the chart count over a family of divisors gets uniform vanishing directly.


  **Is `hcount` refutable by the §5–§6 tower argument?  No — and the reason is the
  useful part.**

  This hypothesis equates the chart count with `ℓ(D)`, and `ell_le_finrank_chart_along_tower`
  says

  `ℓ` is *also* bounded along the tower under these binders.  Bounded = bounded is
  no

  contradiction, so unlike `hledger` — which equates χ with the **unbounded** `deg_k
  D` — `hcount`

  survives the tower.


  That contrast is the sharpest available statement of what went wrong with the lane''s
  ledger:

  `hcount` and `hledger` both look like "the Čech count is exact", but only the ledger
  asserts

  *growth*.  The vanishing content is safe from the *tower*; the Riemann-growth content
  is what

  these binders forbid.


  **BUT THIS THEOREM IS STILL EMPTY AT A CURVE, for the other reason, and the distinction
  is

  worth keeping straight.**  Surviving the tower argument is not the same as being
  instantiable.

  The three `∀ D` chart-finiteness binders it carries are unsatisfiable on a curve
  outright

  (`ChartFinitenessRefuted.not_chart_finite_of_primeDivisor`), so no curve supplies
  them and this

  theorem has no instances there either.  The difference from `hledger` is real but
  narrower than

  it looks: `hledger` is *inconsistent with* the binders, while `hcount` is merely
  *unavailable

  because of* them.  Neither reaches a curve.


  **A DEAD END worth recording, because it is the obvious escape and it does not work.**

  `h1dim_eq_zero_iff_charts` and `charts_sub_overlap_le_ell` assume finiteness at
  a *single*

  divisor rather than over the family, so they look salvageable by instantiating at

  `U₀ = U₁ = ⊤`, where `Module.Finite` is the honest finiteness of `L(D)`.  They are
  not: at that

  cover `𝒜(D) = Γ(⊤,𝒪(D))` too, the count `dim + dim − dim` collapses to `ℓ(D)`, and
  the

  criterion''s right-hand side becomes `ℓ(D) = ℓ(D)`.  So `h1dim k ⊤ ⊤ D = 0` follows

  *unconditionally* — which is correct (the Čech `H¹` of a one-set cover vanishes)
  and useless.

  Machine-checked, not argued: the proof is `rw [h1dim_eq_zero_iff_charts, ell, inf_idem];
  ring`.


  So the §3 criteria face a genuine squeeze rather than a fixable defect: at a two-chart
  cover

  their binders are unsatisfiable, and at the degenerate cover their conclusion is
  vacuous.  What

  they need is finiteness of the three chart section spaces at *some* cover that is
  neither — which

  is exactly what `ChartFinitenessRefuted` shows no curve provides at an affine chart.  Anyone

  picking this up should treat the two-chart adelic count as the thing to replace,
  not the

  hypothesis to weaken.'

theorem exists_bound_h1dim_eq_zero_of_charts (hcov : U₀ ⊔ U₁ = ⊤) (b : ℤ)
    [∀ D : X.WeilDivisor, Module.Finite k (sectionSub k U₀ D)]
    [∀ D : X.WeilDivisor, Module.Finite k (sectionSub k U₁ D)]
    [∀ D : X.WeilDivisor, Module.Finite k (sectionSub k (U₀ ⊓ U₁) D)]
    (hcount : ∀ D : X.WeilDivisor, b ≤ degK k D →
      (Module.finrank k (sectionSub k U₀ D) : ℤ)

    ∀ D : X.WeilDivisor, b ≤ degK k D → h1dim k U₀ U₁ D = 0 :=
