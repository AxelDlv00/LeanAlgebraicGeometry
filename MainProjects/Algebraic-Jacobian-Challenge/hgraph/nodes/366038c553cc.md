\textit{Source: [Stacks Project], Sheaves on Spaces, Lemma~\texttt{lemma-points-exactness}
  (Tag 007T), part (1); local injectivity is the sheaf-theoretic monomorphism condition
  spelled out at the level of the covering (canonical) topology.}
  A morphism \(g\colon M \to N\) of presheaves of \(R\)-modules on \(\mathtt{Opens}\,X\)
  is locally injective (for the canonical topology) if and only if the induced stalk map
  \(g_x\colon M_x \to N_x\) is injective at every point \(x\).
