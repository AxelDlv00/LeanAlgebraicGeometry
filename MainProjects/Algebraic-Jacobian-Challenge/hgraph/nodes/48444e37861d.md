The structure map induces a \(k\)-algebra equivalence
  \[
    \Gamma(C,\struct C)\simeq_k k.
  \]
  Equivalently, the zeroth cohomology of the structure sheaf has dimension
  one on every affine Mayer--Vietoris square.
