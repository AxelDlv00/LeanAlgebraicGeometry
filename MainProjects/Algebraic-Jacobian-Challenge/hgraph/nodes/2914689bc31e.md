  four-fold composite

  `(pushforwardId _).inv ≫ pushforwardNatTrans (𝟙 _) adj.counit ≫ (pushforwardCongr
  _).hom ≫

  (pushforwardComp _ _).inv`.  Both `pushforwardId` and `pushforwardComp` are `Iso.refl`

  carriers), and `pushforwardNatTrans`/`pushforwardCongr` act on carriers by the presheaf
  restriction

  map of `M` along `adj.counit` (the `restrictScalars` base change is the identity
  on the underlying

  abelian group).  Hence on a section at `U`, the unit is just `M.map (adj.counit.app
  U.unop).op`.

  This is the presheaf-level value lemma the K1 η-bridge needs (no sheaf-level twin
  exists).'
