Let \(R' = S^{-1}R\) be a localization, \(N = S^{-1}M\) the corresponding
  localized module, and \(\mathfrak q\) a prime of \(R'\).  Then the fibre
  rank of \(N\) at \(\mathfrak q\) equals the fibre rank of \(M\) at the
  contraction \(\mathfrak q \cap R\).
