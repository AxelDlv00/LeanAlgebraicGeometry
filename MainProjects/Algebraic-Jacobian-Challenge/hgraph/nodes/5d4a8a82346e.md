The untwisted cokernel of the standard cover of \(\mathbb{P}^1_k\)
  vanishes:
  \[
    k[x, x^{-1}] \big/ \bigl(k[x] + k[x^{-1}]\bigr) \;=\; 0,
    \qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad H^1(\mathbb{P}^1_k, \struct{\mathbb{P}^1}) = 0.
  \]
