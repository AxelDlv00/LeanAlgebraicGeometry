Let \(\kappa \to \kappa'\) be a field extension, \(V\) a \(\kappa\)-vector
  space, and \(W\) a \(\kappa'\)-vector space. Every \(\kappa'\)-linear
  equivalence \(\kappa' \otimes_\kappa V \simeq_{\kappa'} W\) forces
  \(\dim_{\kappa'} W = \dim_\kappa V\).
