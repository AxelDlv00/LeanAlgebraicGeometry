docstring: '**Dimension of `Pic⁰_{C/k}` equals the genus of `C`.**


  Milne~§III.1, Rmk.~1.4(e): "The dimension of `J` is the genus of `C`".

  For a smooth proper geometrically integral curve `C/k` of genus

  `g = g(C)`, the topological Krull dimension of the underlying scheme of

  `Pic⁰_{C/k}` equals `g(C)`. By Kleiman~§5 Cor.~`cor:sm`, the inequality

  `dim Pic_{C/k} ≤ dim_k H¹(C, O_C)` is always an equality at points where

  `Pic_{C/k}` is smooth, and for smooth proper curves

  (`SmoothOfRelativeDimension 1 C.hom`) the identity component is smooth

  (Kleiman~§5 Ex.~`ex:jac`), so the dimension equals `dim_k H¹(C, O_C) = g(C)`

  by `def:genus`.


  This argument is not yet formalised: the proof is `sorry`.


  RETRACTED (run 0067 r5, commit `5dca5e7cc`) — this node used to carry the

  paragraph that priced the blocker as "mathlib has almost no `topologicalKrullDim`

  API, so the route must run through

  `Algebra.IsStandardSmoothOfRelativeDimension` plus an affine-local

  presentation". That measured the API *surface*, never the *definition*.

  `topologicalKrullDim` IS `krullDim (IrreducibleCloseds X)`; a scheme is sober and

  `T0`, so `irreducibleSetEquivPoints` is an order iso onto the carrier,

  `Order.krullDim_eq_iSup_coheight` applies, and the project's own

  `Scheme.ringKrullDim_stalk_eq_coheight` finishes:

  `dim X = ⨆ z, dim 𝒪_{X,z}` for ANY scheme

  (`Scheme.topologicalKrullDim_eq_iSup_ringKrullDim_stalk`,

  `Picard/SchemeKrullDimStalk.lean`).


  STATE OF THE TWO DIRECTIONS (`Picard/Pic0Dimension.lean`):

  * `≥` is PROVED, from data at the identity alone —

    `Pic0.genus_le_topologicalKrullDim_of_smooth`, whose only side condition is

    regularity of the stalk at `e`, and over a PERFECT base field that follows

    from smoothness of `Pic⁰`

    (`Scheme.isRegularLocalRing_stalk_of_smooth_of_perfectField`).

  * `≤` is the whole open content, and it is one UNIFORM bound

    `∀ z, ringKrullDim (stalk z) ≤ genus C` — it cannot come from one point,

    since the dimension is a supremum.

    `Pic0.topologicalKrullDim_eq_genus_of_forall_ringKrullDim_stalk_le` states

    this theorem against exactly that hypothesis.


  Scope, checked rather than assumed: the retraction concerns

  `topologicalKrullDim` only. `Jacobian.lean`'s leaf-B docstrings price a

  DIFFERENT translation (tangent dimension to the presentation-based

  `SmoothOfRelativeDimension` via `Module.rank S Ω[S⁄R]`) and remain open and

  untouched.


  Consumers wanting only a dimension *index* (e.g. the Albanese lane) may find

  `SmoothOfRelativeDimension (genus C) (Pic0Scheme C).hom` a cheaper target than

  this Krull-dimension form.'
