Fix a field of constants \(k\) (\Cref{def:adelic_constant_field_gate}),
  under which the section subgroups of
  \Cref{thm:adelic_local_step_injective} are \(k\)-subspaces. Given a
  \(k\)-linear embedding \(\iota\) of the single-point quotient
  \(\mathfrak G_P^{\ge -D(P)-1}/\mathfrak G_P^{\ge -D(P)}\) into a
  finite-dimensional \(k\)-vector space \(V\) (at the use site,
  \(V = \kappa(P)\) with \(\dim_k V = \deg P\), via
  \Cref{def:adelic_pointValuation}), the local step satisfies
  \[
    \dim_k \bigl(\Gamma(U, \struct{C}(D'))/\Gamma(U, \struct{C}(D))\bigr)
      \;\le\; \dim_k V.
  \]
