Under the hypotheses of \Cref{thm:adelic_ledger_alternating} for
  \(D \le D'\),
  \[
    \chi(D') \;=\; \chi(D) + \dim_k\bigl(\Gamma(V,\struct{C}(D'))/
      \Gamma(V,\struct{C}(D))\bigr).
  \]
  For a one-point bump \(D'=D+P\), the approximation hypothesis of
  \Cref{thm:adelic_local_step_dim_eq_residue_degree} gives
  \(\chi(D+P)=\chi(D)+\deg_P^{\mathrm{val}}\).
