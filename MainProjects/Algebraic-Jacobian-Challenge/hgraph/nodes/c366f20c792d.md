Under the hypotheses of \Cref{thm:adelic_ledger_alternating} for
  \(D \le D'\),
  \[
    \chi(D') \;=\; \chi(D) + \dim_k\bigl(\Gamma(V,\struct{C}(D'))/
      \Gamma(V,\struct{C}(D))\bigr).
  \]
  Combined with \Cref{thm:adelic_dim_adele_quotient_eq_degree}, for a
  one-point bump \(D' = D + P\) this is \(\chi(D+P) = \chi(D) + \deg P\).
