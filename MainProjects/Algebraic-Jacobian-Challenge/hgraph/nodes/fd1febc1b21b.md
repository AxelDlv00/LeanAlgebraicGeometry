Let \(P_1,\ldots,P_r\) be prime divisors and suppose the one-point
  identity
  \(\chi(E+P)=\chi(E)+\deg_P^{\mathrm{val}}\) holds at every step.  Then
  \[
    \chi(P_1+\cdots+P_r)
      =\chi(0)+\sum_{j=1}^r\deg_{P_j}^{\mathrm{val}}.
  \]
