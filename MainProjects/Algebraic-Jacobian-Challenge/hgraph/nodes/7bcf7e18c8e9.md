On the intersection \(P = T_I \times_T T_J\) of two chart loci, the two composite chart
  morphisms agree into the glued scheme:
  \(\mathrm{pr}_1 \circ (\varphi_I \circ \iota_I) = \mathrm{pr}_2 \circ (\varphi_J \circ
  \iota_J)\).
