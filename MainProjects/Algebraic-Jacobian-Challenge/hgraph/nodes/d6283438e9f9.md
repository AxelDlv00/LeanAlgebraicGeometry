A field \(k \hookrightarrow K\) is a \emph{field of constants} of
  \(C\) if every nonzero \(c \in k\) is a unit at every prime divisor,
  \(v_P(c) = 0\). On a smooth proper curve over an algebraically closed
  \(\bar k\), with \(k = \bar k\), this holds because a nonzero constant
  is a unit in every local ring; no instance is supplied here.
