\textit{Provided by Mathlib.}
  If \(F : (\mathcal{C}, J) \to (\mathcal{D}, K)\) and \(G : (\mathcal{D}, K) \to (\mathcal{E}, L)\)
  are continuous functors of sites, then their composite \(F \circ G\) is continuous for \(J\) and
  \(L\).
