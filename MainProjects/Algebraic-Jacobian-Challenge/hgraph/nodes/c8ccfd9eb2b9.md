For every morphism \(g : S' \to S\) the canonical square
  \[
    \begin{array}{ccc}
      \mathbb{P}(n; S') & \longrightarrow & \mathbb{P}(n; S) \\
      \downarrow & & \downarrow \\
      S' & \xrightarrow{\;g\;} & S
    \end{array}
  \]
  is cartesian.
