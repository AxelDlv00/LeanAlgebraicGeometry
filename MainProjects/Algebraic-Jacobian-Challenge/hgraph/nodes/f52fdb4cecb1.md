In the polynomial-module structure (\cref{def:graded_polyModule}), \((C\,c) \cdot m =
  c \cdot m\) for all \(c \in \kappa\), \(m \in M\). Immediate from
  \(\mathrm{polyEndHom}(t)(C\,c) = c \cdot \mathrm{id}_M\)
  (\cref{lem:graded_polyEndHom_C}).
