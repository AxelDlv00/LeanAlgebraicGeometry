In the degree-zero part of the localization
  \(\mathbb{Z}[X_i : i \in n]_{X_i X_j}\), the fraction
  \(X_i/X_j = X_i^2/(X_i X_j)\) is a unit, with inverse
  \(X_j/X_i = X_j^2/(X_i X_j)\).
