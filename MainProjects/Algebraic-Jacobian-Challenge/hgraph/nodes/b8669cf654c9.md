Let \(X\) be a scheme and \(M, N \in \Scheme.\mathtt{Modules}\,X\)
  \emph{arbitrary} \(\mathcal{O}_X\)-modules. There is a natural isomorphism
  \[
    M \otimes_X N \;\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\; N \otimes_X M .
  \]
