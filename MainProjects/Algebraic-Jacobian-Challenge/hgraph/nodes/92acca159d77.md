The \(J\)-minor of the image matrix \(M = (X^I_J)^{-1} X^I\)
  (\cref{def:gr_image_matrix}) is the identity:
  \[
    M_J \;=\; (X^I_J)^{-1}\, X^I_J \;=\; 1_{d\times d}.
  \]
