For a degree-\(m\) homogeneous form \(F\), the chart family
  \(\bigl(F/X_i^m\bigr)_i\) --- the away-fraction of \(F\) at each chart,
  through \Cref{def:chart_sections_away} --- is a compatible family for the
  Serre-twist descent datum (\Cref{lem:serre_twist_compatible_condition}).
