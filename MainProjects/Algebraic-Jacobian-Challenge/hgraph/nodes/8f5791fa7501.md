For a nonzero \(f\in K(X)\), its principal divisor is
  \[
    \operatorname{div}(f)
      =\sum_{Y\in X^{(1)}}\operatorname{ord}_Y(f)\,Y.
  \]
  The sum is finite by \cref{lem:rationalMap_order_finite_support}.
