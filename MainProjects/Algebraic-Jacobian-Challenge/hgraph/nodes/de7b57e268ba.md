If \(D'=D+P\) and
  \(\mathcal T=\struct C(D')/\struct C(D)\), then
  \[
    \mathcal T_P\simeq_k
      \mathfrak G_P^{\ge-D(P)-1}/\mathfrak G_P^{\ge-D(P)}
      \simeq_k\kappa(P),
  \]
  while \(\mathcal T_Q=0\) for \(Q\ne P\).
