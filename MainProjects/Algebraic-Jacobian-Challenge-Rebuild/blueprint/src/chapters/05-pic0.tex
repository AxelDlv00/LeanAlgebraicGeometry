\chapter{Universal class and the finite-stage representability gap}
\label{route-ch-universal}

Chapter~\ref{route-ch-finite-stage} proves the carrier comparison for any
legacy \(P_0:\operatorname{Pic0FiniteStageGluePackage}(C_\Omega,K)\).  The
route does not freeze that arbitrary stage.  Instead, the first missing
producer must choose a final legacy package only after all carrier, class, and
cocycle coefficients have been enlarged to one finite Galois subextension.
This joint choice is what prevents a silent change of carrier.

\section{What is pinned over the separably closed field}

\begin{theorem}[The universal class and all atlas restrictions over \(\Omega\)]
  \label{route-thm-sepclosed-universal-class}
  \lean{AlgebraicGeometry.pic0SepClosedUniversalClass,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalChartClass,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalOverlapClass,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalChartClass_restrict_left,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalChartClass_restrict_right,
    AlgebraicGeometry.Pic0FiniteStageUniversalAtlasClass,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalAtlasClass}
  \leanok
  \dcref{custom: Yoneda universal element and its restrictions}
  \uses{route-thm-sepclosed-representability, route-thm-finite-atlas}

  Let
  \[
    \xi_\Omega:=r_\Omega(\mathrm{id}_{J_\Omega})
       \in\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_\Omega}(J_\Omega).
  \]
  For every selected affine chart \(U_i\subset J_\Omega\) and every exact
  ordered pair-overlap \(U_{ij}=U_i\cap U_j\), the checked classes are exactly
  the pullbacks of this one \(\xi_\Omega\).  If
  \(q^i_{ij}:U_{ij}\to U_i\) and \(q^j_{ij}:U_{ij}\to U_j\), then
  \[
    (q^i_{ij})^*(\xi_\Omega|_{U_i})
      =\xi_\Omega|_{U_{ij}}
      =(q^j_{ij})^*(\xi_\Omega|_{U_j}).
  \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  Apply the representing equivalence to the identity of \(J_\Omega\).  Its
  naturality says that precomposition by a chart or overlap morphism is
  pullback of the resulting Picard class, proving both restriction equations.
  Every class in this theorem still lives over \(\Omega\).
\end{proof}

The source tree additionally declares \texttt{pic0ClassYoneda} and
\texttt{pic0SepClosedClassYoneda\_app}, which express the expected
class-to-natural-transformation formula.  Their defining module is not
reachable from the public \texttt{AlgebraicJacobian} root and has no current
verified artifact.  They are therefore unverified implementation aids, not
attachments or proof credit in this specification.

\section{The first unmet producer}

\begin{theorem}[Joint Galois-stage package and descended universal class]
  \label{route-thm-descended-universal-class}
  \notready
  \source{stacks-project}
  \dcref{Tags 01ZM, 0BMG, and 0EXM; Tag 0B9K as a source boundary}
  \uses{route-thm-sepclosed-universal-class, route-thm-global-comparison}

  There must exist, simultaneously:
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item a legacy package
    \(P:\operatorname{Pic0FiniteStageGluePackage}(C_\Omega,K)\);
  \item for \(L:=P.N.1\subset\Omega\), certificates that \(L/K\) is finite
    Galois, with the displayed inclusion and scalar-tower maps;
  \item on the literal carrier \(J_L:=P.\mathrm{gluedOver}\), a class
    \[
      \xi_L\in\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_L}(J_L)
        =\operatorname{pic0Subgroup}
          ((\operatorname{baseChange}_{K,L})C,P.\mathrm{gluedOver}),
      \qquad C_L=C\times_KL.
    \]
  \end{enumerate}
  The symbol \(P\) in this theorem is a new, jointly selected package; it is
  not the arbitrary comparison witness \(P_0\) from
  Theorem~\ref{route-thm-global-comparison}.  If the construction starts from
  \(P_0\), it must additionally provide an explicit refinement map from
  \(P_0\) to \(P\) together with the induced \(\operatorname{Over}\)-map and
  its compatibility with every chart and overlap comparison.  Without those
  fields, \(P_0\) is discarded after the comparison theorem and no field
  enlargement is allowed to silently replace its carrier.  All subsequent
  occurrences of \(J_L\), \(\xi_L\), (FS-Rep), (Proj), and (OA) quantify over
  this single final \(P\).
  For the checked comparison
  \(\beta_P:J_L\times_L\Omega\xrightarrow{\sim}J_\Omega\), this class must
  satisfy
  \[
    \operatorname{bc}_{L,\Omega}(\xi_L)=\beta_P^*(\xi_\Omega)
      \quad\text{in}\quad
    \operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_\Omega}(J_L\times_L\Omega).
    \tag{UC}
  \]
  Equivalently, its restriction to every descended chart pulls back to
  \(\xi_\Omega|_{U_i}\), and its two restrictions to every descended overlap
  agree before gluing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  The field-theoretic part has no route choice.  Every finite coefficient set
  lies in a finite separable subextension because \(\Omega/K\) is algebraic
  and separable.  Normality of \(\Omega/K\) and Stacks Tag 0BMG place a finite
  normal enlargement \(L/K\), containing that coefficient field, inside
  \(\Omega\).  Separability inherited from \(\Omega/K\), equivalently Tag
  0EXM applied to the normal closure of the finite separable input, makes
  \(L/K\) finite Galois.  Only then re-run the finite-presentation descent of all chart
  rings, restriction maps, inverse maps, and triple equations at \(L\), and
  assemble the final legacy package with \(P.N.1=L\).  One must not retain an
  earlier package while changing its field.

  The unresolved mathematical step is to descend finite presentations of
  representatives of the finitely many chart classes, all transition
  isomorphisms and their cocycle equations at that same \(L\), glue them using
  the actual etale-sheafified relative Picard equivalence relation, and prove
  degree zero after every field-valued pullback.  Comparison through
  \(\beta_P\) must then prove (UC).  Tag 01ZM supplies finite-presentation,
  morphism, and equality descent.  Section 44.4 (Tag 0B9K) describes the fppf
  Picard sheaf and invertible-sheaf descent, but is only orientation: it does
  not identify that object with AJCR's big-etale degree-zero carrier or
  construct \(\xi_L\).  The planned Lean handoff is one dependent package
  containing exactly \(P\), the finite-Galois certificates, \(\xi_L\), and
  equation (UC).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Yoneda equivalence on the same finite Galois carrier]
  \label{route-thm-finite-stage-yoneda}
  \notready
  \source{stacks-project}
  \dcref{Tag 040L, descent of morphisms; exact RepresentableBy target}
  \uses{route-thm-descended-universal-class}

  For the same \((P,L,\xi_L)\), every \(L\)-scheme \(T\) must admit the
  bijection
  \[
    \Phi_T:\Hom_L(T,J_L)\xrightarrow{\sim}
       \operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_L}(T),
    \qquad f\longmapsto f^*\xi_L.
    \tag{Y}
  \]
  For every \(q:T'\to T\) and \(f:T\to J_L\), it must satisfy the correctly
  typed naturality equation
  \[
    \Phi_{T'}(f\circ q)=q^*\Phi_T(f).
  \]
  The exact public Lean handoff is
  \[
    \operatorname{RepresentableBy}
      (\operatorname{pic0TypeFunctor}(C_L),P.\mathrm{gluedOver}).
    \tag{FS-Rep}
  \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  Pullback of \(\xi_L\) defines the transformation in (Y).  To prove every
  component bijective, compare it after the faithfully flat extension
  \(L\to\Omega\).  Equation (UC), \(\beta_P\), and \(r_\Omega\) identify the
  result with a bijection.  Existence and equality of morphisms then descend;
  Stacks Remark 35.35.12 (Tag 040L) supplies the abstract fpqc morphism-descent
  principle.  The specialization must be performed uniformly for arbitrary
  \(T\), including the curve scalar-tower and big-etale degree-zero transports.

  No public-root declaration proves (Y) or (FS-Rep).  In particular,
  \texttt{pic0RepresentableBy\_finiteStageBaseChange} has target over
  \(\Omega\), not over \(L\), and its own module is unrooted.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Frontier accounting]
  \label{route-rem-first-frontier}
  \dcref{custom}

  The first unmet mathematical producer is the class \(\xi_L\) and equation
  (UC) in Theorem~\ref{route-thm-descended-universal-class}.  Choosing the
  finite normal coefficient field and rebuilding the finite diagram there is
  a fixed imported/planned prelude inside that same package, not a competing
  route.  The frontier moves only when a class on the displayed literal
  carrier exists; further transport or comparison wrappers do not move it.
\end{remark}
