\chapter{Finite-Galois descent to the original field}
\label{route-ch-galois}

Fix the single witness \((P,L,\xi_L)\) from
Theorem~\ref{route-thm-descended-universal-class}, where
\(L=P.N.1\) is finite Galois over \(K\), and assume
Theorem~\ref{route-thm-finite-stage-yoneda} has supplied
\[
  r_L:h_{J_L}\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_L},
  \qquad J_L=P.\mathrm{gluedOver}.
\]
No object is base-changed or replaced in this chapter: the Galois action,
projectivity input, orbit condition, and quotient all use this literal carrier.

\section{The one selected exact-carrier geometry route}

The equivalence \(r_L\) canonically equips \(J_L\) with a commutative group
object and with the semilinear action
\[
  \rho:\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\longrightarrow
       \operatorname{Aut}_{K\text{-semilin}}(J_L)
\]
characterized by invariance of the universal Picard class.

\begin{theorem}[Projectivity of the literal finite-stage carrier]
  \label{route-thm-exact-carrier-projective}
  \notready
  \dcref{custom: the exact IsProjective producer; no accepted source
    specialization yet}
  \uses{route-thm-finite-stage-yoneda}

  Construct the certificate
  \[
    h_{\mathrm{proj}}:
      \operatorname{IsProjective}(P.\mathrm{gluedMap}),
    \tag{Proj}
  \]
  where \(P\) is the same legacy package that carries \(\xi_L\) and \(r_L\).
  This is the sole selected producer for the orbit condition.  A proof by an
  independently chosen projective-space immersion is not an alternate route.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  This target is planned and blocked on (FS-Rep).  A valid proof must construct a relatively ample
  invertible sheaf, or equivalently the finite-type closed immersion furnished
  by the projectivity API, on the literal glued scheme and verify compatibility
  with \(P.\mathrm{gluedMap}\).  Classical projectivity of the Jacobian cannot
  be imported here until its hypotheses and its identification with AJCR's
  degree-zero big-etale functor are proved.  In particular, projectivity of an
  admissible divisor source, a stable-facade carrier, or an object merely
  isomorphic after base change does not inhabit (Proj).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Projectivity implies exact-carrier orbit-affineness]
  \label{route-thm-exact-carrier-orbits}
  \notready
  \dcref{custom: finite hyperplane avoidance on the literal carrier}
  \uses{route-thm-exact-carrier-projective}

  From (Proj), for every scheme point \(x\in|J_L|\), produce an affine open
  \(V\subseteq J_L\) such that
  \[
    \{\rho(\sigma)(x):\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\}\subseteq V.
    \tag{OA}
  \]
  The exact Lean output is
  \[
    (\operatorname{pic0SemilinearGalActionOfRepresentableBy}(C,r_L))
       .\operatorname{OrbitsInAffineOpen}.
  \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  The finite Galois orbit is a finite subset.  Projectivity supplies a closed
  immersion of \(J_L\) into finite relative projective space; choose a
  hyperplane avoiding the orbit, and pull back its affine complement.  The
  source tree contains the exact conditional wrapper
  \texttt{pic0FiniteStageOrbitsInAffineOpen\_of\_isProjective C Ck P repL
  hproj}.  It has the required binders
  \texttt{[FiniteDimensional K P.N.1] [IsGalois K P.N.1]} and returns (OA) on
  \texttt{P.gluedOver}.  Its module is not public-root reachable, so it receives
  no \texttt{\textbackslash leanok} marker; rooting that wrapper is a planned
  integration step after the planned input (Proj).  The immersion entry
  point used internally by the wrapper is quarantined as a second public route.
\end{proof}

\section{The conditional descent theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Galois descent of the represented degree-zero functor]
  \label{route-thm-finite-galois-descent}
  \lean{AlgebraicGeometry.pic0SemilinearGalActionOfRepresentableBy,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0GaloisInvariantEquiv,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0GaloisInvariantEquivGaloisEquivariantOver,
    AlgebraicGeometry.pic0RepresentableBy_finiteGaloisDescent,
    AlgebraicGeometry.locallyOfFiniteType_pic0FiniteGaloisDescent,
    AlgebraicGeometry.quasiCompact_pic0FiniteGaloisDescent}
  \leanok
  \source{stacks-project}
  \dcref{Tags 0246 and 040L, effective fpqc descent and descent of morphisms}
  \uses{route-thm-exact-carrier-orbits}

  Let \(L/K\) be the displayed finite Galois extension, with the Lean
  instances \([\operatorname{Algebra}\ K\ L]\),
  \([\operatorname{FiniteDimensional}\ K\ L]\), and
  \([\operatorname{IsGalois}\ K\ L]\).  Let
  \(C_L=(\operatorname{baseChange}\ K\ L).obj\,C\), let
  \(J_L:\operatorname{Over}(\operatorname{Spec}L)\), and assume the exact
  input
  \[
    r_L:\operatorname{RepresentableBy}
      (\operatorname{pic0TypeFunctor}(C_L),J_L)
  \]
  together with the typeclass \(\operatorname{OrbitsInAffineOpen}\) for
  \(\operatorname{pic0SemilinearGalActionOfRepresentableBy}(C,r_L)\).  The
  stable affine opens glue to
  \[
    J:=\operatorname{StableAffineOpen.gluedQuotientOver}(\rho)
       \longrightarrow\Spec K.
  \]
  For every \(K\)-scheme \(T\), the checked equivalences are
  \[
    \Hom_K(T,J)
      \simeq\Hom_L(T_L,J_L)^{\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)}
      \simeq\operatorname{Pic}^0_{C_L}(T_L)^{\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)}
      \simeq\operatorname{Pic}^0_C(T).
    \tag{G}
  \]
  Hence \(J\) represents \(\operatorname{pic0TypeFunctor}(C)\).  If
  \(J_L\to\Spec L\) is locally of finite type, respectively quasi-compact,
  then so is \(J\to\Spec K\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  Orbit-affineness chooses a Galois-stable affine cover.  Affine fpqc descent
  glues its invariant coordinate rings; Stacks Section 35.38 (Tag 0246)
  records effective descent for quasi-affine morphisms and Tag 040L records
  descent of compatible morphisms.  The first equivalence in (G) is the
  checked quotient universal property.  The second uses \(r_L\) and its
  canonical semilinear action.  The third is the checked equivalence between
  original-field Picard classes and Galois-invariant classes after base change.
  Their naturality in \(T\) is exactly the \texttt{RepresentableBy}
  certificate, and the finiteness properties descend through the same quotient.

  This is a checked \emph{conditional} theorem: the displayed \(r_L\), field
  typeclasses, and \texttt{OrbitsInAffineOpen} remain visible inputs.  Its Lean binder for the
  invariant comparison includes geometric reducedness of \(C\); smoothness of
  the challenge curve derives that instance, so H2 already discharges it.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[No carrier switch]
  \label{route-rem-no-carrier-switch}
  \dcref{custom}

  The selected route finalizes one legacy package with \(L=P.N.1\) before
  descending \(\xi_L\).  It then uses \(P.\mathrm{gluedOver}\) in (FS-Rep),
  (Proj), (OA), and the generic finite-Galois theorem.  The newer
  \texttt{Pic0FiniteStageStableGluePackage} has no checked carrier comparison
  with this object and is never substituted.  Forward representer transport
  along an arbitrary object isomorphism is likewise a consumer of an existing
  representation, not a producer for R6.
\end{remark}
