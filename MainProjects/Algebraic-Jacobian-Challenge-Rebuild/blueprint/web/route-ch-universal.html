5 Universal class and the finite-stage representability gap

Chapter 4 proves the carrier comparison for any legacy \(P_0:\operatorname {Pic0FiniteStageGluePackage}(C_\Omega ,K)\). The route does not freeze that arbitrary stage. Instead, the first missing producer must choose a final legacy package only after all carrier, class, and cocycle coefficients have been enlarged to one finite Galois subextension. This joint choice is what prevents a silent change of carrier.

5.1 What is pinned over the separably closed field

Theorem 15 The universal class and all atlas restrictions over \(\Omega \)
#

Uses

  • Theorem 9
  • Theorem 11

Lean declarations

  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalChartClass
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalOverlapClass
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalChartClass_restrict_left
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalChartClass_restrict_right
  • AlgebraicGeometry.Pic0FiniteStageUniversalAtlasClass
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0FiniteStageUniversalAtlasClass

Let

\[ \xi _\Omega :=r_\Omega (\mathrm{id}_{J_\Omega }) \in \operatorname {Pic}^0_{C_\Omega }(J_\Omega ). \]

For every selected affine chart \(U_i\subset J_\Omega \) and every exact ordered pair-overlap \(U_{ij}=U_i\cap U_j\), the checked classes are exactly the pullbacks of this one \(\xi _\Omega \). If \(q^i_{ij}:U_{ij}\to U_i\) and \(q^j_{ij}:U_{ij}\to U_j\), then

\[ (q^i_{ij})^*(\xi _\Omega |_{U_i}) =\xi _\Omega |_{U_{ij}} =(q^j_{ij})^*(\xi _\Omega |_{U_j}). \]
Proof ▶

Apply the representing equivalence to the identity of \(J_\Omega \). Its naturality says that precomposition by a chart or overlap morphism is pullback of the resulting Picard class, proving both restriction equations. Every class in this theorem still lives over \(\Omega \).

The source tree additionally declares pic0ClassYoneda and pic0SepClosedClassYoneda_app, which express the expected class-to-natural-transformation formula. Their defining module is not reachable from the public AlgebraicJacobian root and has no current verified artifact. They are therefore unverified implementation aids, not attachments or proof credit in this specification.

5.2 The first unmet producer

Theorem 16 Joint Galois-stage package and descended universal class
#

Uses

  • Theorem 15
  • Theorem 14

There must exist, simultaneously:

  1. a legacy package \(P:\operatorname {Pic0FiniteStageGluePackage}(C_\Omega ,K)\);

  2. for \(L:=P.N.1\subset \Omega \), certificates that \(L/K\) is finite Galois, with the displayed inclusion and scalar-tower maps;

  3. on the literal carrier \(J_L:=P.\mathrm{gluedOver}\), a class

    \[ \xi _L\in \operatorname {Pic}^0_{C_L}(J_L) =\operatorname {pic0Subgroup} ((\operatorname {baseChange}_{K,L})C,P.\mathrm{gluedOver}), \qquad C_L=C\times _KL. \]

The symbol \(P\) in this theorem is a new, jointly selected package; it is not the arbitrary comparison witness \(P_0\) from Theorem 14. If the construction starts from \(P_0\), it must additionally provide an explicit refinement map from \(P_0\) to \(P\) together with the induced \(\operatorname {Over}\)-map and its compatibility with every chart and overlap comparison. Without those fields, \(P_0\) is discarded after the comparison theorem and no field enlargement is allowed to silently replace its carrier. All subsequent occurrences of \(J_L\), \(\xi _L\), (FS-Rep), (Proj), and (OA) quantify over this single final \(P\). For the checked comparison \(\beta _P:J_L\times _L\Omega \xrightarrow {\sim }J_\Omega \), this class must satisfy

\[ \operatorname {bc}_{L,\Omega }(\xi _L)=\beta _P^*(\xi _\Omega ) \quad \text{in}\quad \operatorname {Pic}^0_{C_\Omega }(J_L\times _L\Omega ). \tag {UC} \]

Equivalently, its restriction to every descended chart pulls back to \(\xi _\Omega |_{U_i}\), and its two restrictions to every descended overlap agree before gluing.

Proof ▶

The field-theoretic part has no route choice. Every finite coefficient set lies in a finite separable subextension because \(\Omega /K\) is algebraic and separable. Normality of \(\Omega /K\) and Stacks Tag 0BMG place a finite normal enlargement \(L/K\), containing that coefficient field, inside \(\Omega \). Separability inherited from \(\Omega /K\), equivalently Tag 0EXM applied to the normal closure of the finite separable input, makes \(L/K\) finite Galois. Only then re-run the finite-presentation descent of all chart rings, restriction maps, inverse maps, and triple equations at \(L\), and assemble the final legacy package with \(P.N.1=L\). One must not retain an earlier package while changing its field.

The unresolved mathematical step is to descend finite presentations of representatives of the finitely many chart classes, all transition isomorphisms and their cocycle equations at that same \(L\), glue them using the actual etale-sheafified relative Picard equivalence relation, and prove degree zero after every field-valued pullback. Comparison through \(\beta _P\) must then prove (UC). Tag 01ZM supplies finite-presentation, morphism, and equality descent. Section 44.4 (Tag 0B9K) describes the fppf Picard sheaf and invertible-sheaf descent, but is only orientation: it does not identify that object with AJCR’s big-etale degree-zero carrier or construct \(\xi _L\). The planned Lean handoff is one dependent package containing exactly \(P\), the finite-Galois certificates, \(\xi _L\), and equation (UC).

Theorem 17 Yoneda equivalence on the same finite Galois carrier
#

Uses

  • Theorem 16

For the same \((P,L,\xi _L)\), every \(L\)-scheme \(T\) must admit the bijection

\[ \Phi _T:\operatorname{Hom}_L(T,J_L)\xrightarrow {\sim } \operatorname {Pic}^0_{C_L}(T), \qquad f\longmapsto f^*\xi _L. \tag {Y} \]

For every \(q:T'\to T\) and \(f:T\to J_L\), it must satisfy the correctly typed naturality equation

\[ \Phi _{T'}(f\circ q)=q^*\Phi _T(f). \]

The exact public Lean handoff is

\[ \operatorname {RepresentableBy} (\operatorname {pic0TypeFunctor}(C_L),P.\mathrm{gluedOver}). \tag {FS-Rep} \]
Proof ▶

Pullback of \(\xi _L\) defines the transformation in (Y). To prove every component bijective, compare it after the faithfully flat extension \(L\to \Omega \). Equation (UC), \(\beta _P\), and \(r_\Omega \) identify the result with a bijection. Existence and equality of morphisms then descend; Stacks Remark 35.35.12 (Tag 040L) supplies the abstract fpqc morphism-descent principle. The specialization must be performed uniformly for arbitrary \(T\), including the curve scalar-tower and big-etale degree-zero transports.

No public-root declaration proves (Y) or (FS-Rep). In particular, pic0RepresentableBy_finiteStageBaseChange has target over \(\Omega \), not over \(L\), and its own module is unrooted.

Remark 18 Frontier accounting
#

The first unmet mathematical producer is the class \(\xi _L\) and equation (UC) in Theorem 16. Choosing the finite normal coefficient field and rebuilding the finite diagram there is a fixed imported/planned prelude inside that same package, not a competing route. The frontier moves only when a class on the displayed literal carrier exists; further transport or comparison wrappers do not move it.
