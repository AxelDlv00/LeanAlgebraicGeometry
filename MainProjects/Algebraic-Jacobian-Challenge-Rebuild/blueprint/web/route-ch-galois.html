6 Finite-Galois descent to the original field

Fix the single witness \((P,L,\xi _L)\) from Theorem 16, where \(L=P.N.1\) is finite Galois over \(K\), and assume Theorem 17 has supplied

\[ r_L:h_{J_L}\xrightarrow {\sim }\operatorname {Pic}^0_{C_L}, \qquad J_L=P.\mathrm{gluedOver}. \]

No object is base-changed or replaced in this chapter: the Galois action, projectivity input, orbit condition, and quotient all use this literal carrier.

6.1 The one selected exact-carrier geometry route

The equivalence \(r_L\) canonically equips \(J_L\) with a commutative group object and with the semilinear action

\[ \rho :\operatorname {Gal}(L/K)\longrightarrow \operatorname {Aut}_{K\text{-semilin}}(J_L) \]

characterized by invariance of the universal Picard class.

Theorem 19 Projectivity of the literal finite-stage carrier
#

Uses

  • Theorem 17

Construct the certificate

\[ h_{\mathrm{proj}}: \operatorname {IsProjective}(P.\mathrm{gluedMap}), \tag {Proj} \]

where \(P\) is the same legacy package that carries \(\xi _L\) and \(r_L\). This is the sole selected producer for the orbit condition. A proof by an independently chosen projective-space immersion is not an alternate route.

Proof ▶

This target is planned and blocked on (FS-Rep). A valid proof must construct a relatively ample invertible sheaf, or equivalently the finite-type closed immersion furnished by the projectivity API, on the literal glued scheme and verify compatibility with \(P.\mathrm{gluedMap}\). Classical projectivity of the Jacobian cannot be imported here until its hypotheses and its identification with AJCR’s degree-zero big-etale functor are proved. In particular, projectivity of an admissible divisor source, a stable-facade carrier, or an object merely isomorphic after base change does not inhabit (Proj).

Theorem 20 Projectivity implies exact-carrier orbit-affineness
#

Uses

  • Theorem 19

From (Proj), for every scheme point \(x\in |J_L|\), produce an affine open \(V\subseteq J_L\) such that

\[ \{ \rho (\sigma )(x):\sigma \in \operatorname {Gal}(L/K)\} \subseteq V. \tag {OA} \]

The exact Lean output is

\[ (\operatorname {pic0SemilinearGalActionOfRepresentableBy}(C,r_L)) .\operatorname {OrbitsInAffineOpen}. \]
Proof ▶

The finite Galois orbit is a finite subset. Projectivity supplies a closed immersion of \(J_L\) into finite relative projective space; choose a hyperplane avoiding the orbit, and pull back its affine complement. The source tree contains the exact conditional wrapper pic0FiniteStageOrbitsInAffineOpen_of_isProjective C Ck P repL hproj. It has the required binders [FiniteDimensional K P.N.1] [IsGalois K P.N.1] and returns (OA) on P.gluedOver. Its module is not public-root reachable, so it receives no \leanok marker; rooting that wrapper is a planned integration step after the planned input (Proj). The immersion entry point used internally by the wrapper is quarantined as a second public route.

6.2 The conditional descent theorem

Theorem 21 Finite-Galois descent of the represented degree-zero functor
#

Uses

  • Theorem 20

Lean declarations

  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0SemilinearGalActionOfRepresentableBy
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0GaloisInvariantEquiv
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0GaloisInvariantEquivGaloisEquivariantOver
  • AlgebraicGeometry.pic0RepresentableBy_finiteGaloisDescent
  • AlgebraicGeometry.locallyOfFiniteType_pic0FiniteGaloisDescent
  • AlgebraicGeometry.quasiCompact_pic0FiniteGaloisDescent

Let \(L/K\) be the displayed finite Galois extension, with the Lean instances \([\operatorname {Algebra}\ K\ L]\), \([\operatorname {FiniteDimensional}\ K\ L]\), and \([\operatorname {IsGalois}\ K\ L]\). Let \(C_L=(\operatorname {baseChange}\ K\ L).obj\, C\), let \(J_L:\operatorname {Over}(\operatorname {Spec}L)\), and assume the exact input

\[ r_L:\operatorname {RepresentableBy} (\operatorname {pic0TypeFunctor}(C_L),J_L) \]

together with the typeclass \(\operatorname {OrbitsInAffineOpen}\) for \(\operatorname {pic0SemilinearGalActionOfRepresentableBy}(C,r_L)\). The stable affine opens glue to

\[ J:=\operatorname {StableAffineOpen.gluedQuotientOver}(\rho ) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Spec}K. \]

For every \(K\)-scheme \(T\), the checked equivalences are

\[ \operatorname{Hom}_K(T,J) \simeq \operatorname{Hom}_L(T_L,J_L)^{\operatorname {Gal}(L/K)} \simeq \operatorname {Pic}^0_{C_L}(T_L)^{\operatorname {Gal}(L/K)} \simeq \operatorname {Pic}^0_C(T). \tag {G} \]

Hence \(J\) represents \(\operatorname {pic0TypeFunctor}(C)\). If \(J_L\to \operatorname{Spec}L\) is locally of finite type, respectively quasi-compact, then so is \(J\to \operatorname{Spec}K\).

Proof ▶

Orbit-affineness chooses a Galois-stable affine cover. Affine fpqc descent glues its invariant coordinate rings; Stacks Section 35.38 (Tag 0246) records effective descent for quasi-affine morphisms and Tag 040L records descent of compatible morphisms. The first equivalence in (G) is the checked quotient universal property. The second uses \(r_L\) and its canonical semilinear action. The third is the checked equivalence between original-field Picard classes and Galois-invariant classes after base change. Their naturality in \(T\) is exactly the RepresentableBy certificate, and the finiteness properties descend through the same quotient.

This is a checked conditional theorem: the displayed \(r_L\), field typeclasses, and OrbitsInAffineOpen remain visible inputs. Its Lean binder for the invariant comparison includes geometric reducedness of \(C\); smoothness of the challenge curve derives that instance, so H2 already discharges it.

Remark 22 No carrier switch
#

The selected route finalizes one legacy package with \(L=P.N.1\) before descending \(\xi _L\). It then uses \(P.\mathrm{gluedOver}\) in (FS-Rep), (Proj), (OA), and the generic finite-Galois theorem. The newer Pic0FiniteStageStableGluePackage has no checked carrier comparison with this object and is never substituted. Forward representer transport along an arbitrary object isomorphism is likewise a consumer of an existing representation, not a producer for R6.
