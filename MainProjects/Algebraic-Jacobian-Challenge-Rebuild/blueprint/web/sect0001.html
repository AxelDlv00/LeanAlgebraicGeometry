About This Blueprint

This is the authoritative route specification for the Algebraic Jacobian Challenge Rebuild. It fixes one construction: represented degree- \(g\) divisors, the rank-one Abel isomorphism, translated rank-one charts over a separably closed field, finite-stage descent of their glued carrier and universal class, finite-Galois descent, and the same-carrier handoff to the challenge interfaces. A formalizer following this text has no remaining route choice.

Each numbered mathematical package states its objects and quantifiers, records the exact public Lean handoff, and visibly classifies its state as proved, imported, conditional, planned, or unresolved. A \leanok marker means that every attached declaration is checked and reachable from the public AlgebraicJacobian root. It may certify a conditional implication; the displayed antecedents then remain hypotheses, and the marker gives no completion credit to the corresponding route instantiation. Textual declarations outside that root are named only as unverified implementation artifacts and receive no \leanok marker.
