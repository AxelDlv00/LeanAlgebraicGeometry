Every class \(x\) of
  \(B[\varepsilon]^\times / \mathcal B^\times(\rho_1[\varepsilon],
  \rho_2[\varepsilon])\) with \(\pi(x) = 1\) is represented by a truncated exponential:
  \(x = [\mathrm{tExp}(b)]\) for some \(b \in B\).
