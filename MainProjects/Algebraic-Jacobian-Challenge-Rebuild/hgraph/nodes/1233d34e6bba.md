For \(f \colon T' \to T\), \(\lambda \in \mathrm{picEt}(C,T)\) and a field point
  \(t' \colon \Spec K \to T'\),
  \[
    \deg_{t'}\bigl(f^{*}\lambda\bigr) = \deg_{f \circ t'}(\lambda).
  \]
