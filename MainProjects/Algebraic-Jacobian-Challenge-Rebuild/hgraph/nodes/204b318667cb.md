Let \(g \in K(X)^\times\) and \(h \in K(X)\). For any open \(U\), the product \(g h\) has poles
  bounded by \(D - \mathrm{div}(g)\) on \(U\) if and only if \(h\) has poles bounded by \(D\) on
  \(U\).
