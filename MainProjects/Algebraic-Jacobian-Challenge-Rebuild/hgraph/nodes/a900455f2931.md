Let \(h\) be a transport family from \(D\) to \(E\) and \(h'\) a transport family from
  \(E\) to \(D\) such that \(h_B \circ h'_B = \id\) on \(E \otimes \Spec B\) for a test
  algebra \(B\). Then \(h'^{*}_B \circ h_B^{*} = \id\) on
  \(\mathrm{relPic}(E, \Spec B)\).
