  The class map \(D \mapsto \struct X(D)\) is a homomorphism \(\Div(X) \to \Pic(X)\): for all
  \(D, D' \in \Div(X)\),
  \[
    \struct X(D + D') = \struct X(D) \cdot \struct X(D') .
  \]
