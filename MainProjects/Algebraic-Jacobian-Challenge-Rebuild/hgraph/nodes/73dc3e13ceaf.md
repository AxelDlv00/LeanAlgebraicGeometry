\provenancetag{CUSTOM}
  
  
  
  Let \(K\) be separably closed and \(X\) a nonempty scheme smooth of relative dimension
  \(1\) over \(K\). Then \(X\) has a \(K\)-point over \(K\): a morphism
  \(p \colon \Spec K \to X\) with \(p \circ f = \id\).
