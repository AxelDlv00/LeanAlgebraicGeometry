For \(b \in B\), the class \([\mathrm{tExp}(b)]\) lies in \(\ker \pi\); and
  \([\mathrm{tExp}(b)] = 1\) in the thickened \v{C}ech group if and only if
  \(b \in \mathcal B^+(\rho_1, \rho_2)\).
