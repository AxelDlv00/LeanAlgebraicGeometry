Let \(r_0 \in \Gamma(X, V_0)\), \(r_1 \in \Gamma(X, V_1)\) satisfy
  \(r_0|_{\cap} \cdot r_1|_{\cap} = 1\) on the overlap. Then the packaged actions of
  \(r_0\) and \(r_1\) on \(F_g(V_0 \cap V_1)\) are mutually inverse.
