The dévissage twist \(D - x\) (Definition~\ref{def:devissageDivisor}) is the subtraction of the
  one-point divisor \(1 \cdot x\):
  \[
    D - x = D - 1 \cdot x, \qquad (D - x) + 1\cdot x = D .
  \]
