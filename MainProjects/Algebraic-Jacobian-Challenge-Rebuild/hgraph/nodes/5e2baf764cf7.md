Let \(X\) be an integral scheme, \(V \subseteq X\) an affine open whose ring of sections
  \(\Gamma(X, V)\) is a Dedekind domain, and \(x \in V\). Then the stalk
  \(\struct{X,x}\) is a valuation ring.
