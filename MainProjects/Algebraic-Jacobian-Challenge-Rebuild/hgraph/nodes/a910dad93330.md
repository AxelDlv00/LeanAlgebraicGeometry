On every field fibre, divisors of degree at least \(n_{\mathrm{adm}}\) have vanishing
  \(H^1\). At exact admissible degree their section rank is
  \(n_{\mathrm{adm}}+1-g\), so the numerical projective dimension of the complete linear
  system is \(n_{\mathrm{adm}}-g\). This is only the fibre calculation: a universal divisor
  line bundle, locally free pushforward with base change, and a relative projective bundle
  remain to be constructed.
