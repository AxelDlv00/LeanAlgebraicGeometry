For a Weil divisor \(D\) with \(H^1(\struct Y(D)) = 0\),
  \[
    h^0\bigl(\struct Y(D)\bigr) \;=\; \deg D + \chi(\struct Y) .
  \]
  (Riemann--Roch with vanishing \(h^1\); with \(\chi(\struct Y) = 1 - g\) this is the
  classical \(h^0(D) = \deg D + 1 - g\).)
