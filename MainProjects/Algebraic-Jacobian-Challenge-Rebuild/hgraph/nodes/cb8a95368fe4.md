The meromorphic presentation (\ref{lem:localEquations_presentation}) of the pulled
  tracked point system has trivialising elements
  \[
    f_z \;=\;
    \begin{cases}
      \pi^{\sharp}(t), & \pi(z) = x', \\
      1, & \pi(z) \neq x',
    \end{cases}
  \]
  at each point \(z\) of \(C_{K_2}\).
