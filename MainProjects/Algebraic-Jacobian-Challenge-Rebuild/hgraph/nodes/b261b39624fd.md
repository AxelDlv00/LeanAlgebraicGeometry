The direct endpoint is
  \[
    \operatorname{pic0AdmissibleAbelQuotientRepresenter}(C)
      : \sum_{Q : \Over(\Spec k)}
          \mathrm{RepresentableBy}\bigl(\mathrm{pic0TypeFunctor}(C),Q\bigr).
  \]
  Its body chooses the admissible datum internally, directly consumes
  \(\operatorname{divFunctorAff\_admissible\_representableBy}\), constructs the effective
  quotient above, and returns its Yoneda comparison. It has no representability,
  local-surjectivity, kernel-pair, rational-point, or quotient-data binder.
