\provenancetag{CUSTOM}
  
  
  For a test object \(T\), the subgroup
  \[
    \mathrm{pic}^0(C,T) \;:=\;
    \bigl\{\, \lambda \in \mathrm{picEt}(C,T) \;:\; \deg_t(\lambda) = 0
      \text{ for every field point } t \text{ of } T \,\bigr\}
  \]
  of classes of degree zero at \emph{every} field point of \(T\).
