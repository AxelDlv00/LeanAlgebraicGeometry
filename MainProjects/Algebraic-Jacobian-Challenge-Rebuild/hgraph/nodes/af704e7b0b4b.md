\provenancetag{CUSTOM}
  
  
  
  Let \(C\) be a scheme, proper over \(k\). If there exists a locally quasi-finite morphism
  \(C \to \PP^1\) over \(k\), then there exists a finite morphism \(C \to \PP^1\) over \(k\).
