Let \(Y\) be a scheme. An \emph{affine two-chart cover} of \(Y\) is a pair of affine open
  subschemes \(V_0, V_1 \subseteq Y\) with \(V_0 \cup V_1 = Y\), whose overlap \(V_0 \cap V_1\)
  is again affine.
