  Given a representation of \(\mathrm{pic}^0(C_L)\) by \(J_L\), Galois-invariant Picard-zero
  classes on \(T_L\) are naturally equivalent to Galois-equivariant morphisms
  \(T_L\to J_L\) for the canonical semilinear action.
