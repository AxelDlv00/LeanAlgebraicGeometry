Let \(x\) be a closed point and \(B\) a Weil divisor with
  \(H^1(\struct Y(B)) = 0\) (i.e.\ a subsingleton). Then
  \(H^1(\struct Y(B + x)) = 0\), and by iteration
  \(H^1(\struct Y(B + n\,x)) = 0\) for all \(n \in \N\).
