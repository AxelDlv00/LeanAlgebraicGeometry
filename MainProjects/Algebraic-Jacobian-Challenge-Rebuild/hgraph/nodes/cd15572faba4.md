For a finite projective \(R\)-module \(M\) and every \(R\)-module \(P\), there is a
  natural equivalence
  \[
    M \otimes_R P \;\cong\; \Hom_R(M^{\vee}, P) :
  \]
  the functor \(P \mapsto M \otimes P\) is represented, in dual-Hom form, by
  \(M^{\vee}\).
