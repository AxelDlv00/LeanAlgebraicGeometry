On the overlap sections \(F_g(V_0 \cap V_1)\),
  \[
    \mathrm{triv}_0(p) \;=\; g \cdot \mathrm{triv}_1(p), \qquad
    \mathrm{triv}_1(p) \;=\; g^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{triv}_0(p).
  \]
