The dévissage sequence
  \[
    0 \longrightarrow \mathcal O(D-x) \longrightarrow \mathcal O(D)
      \xrightarrow{\ \pi\ } \mathrm{sky}_x(J) \longrightarrow 0
  \]
  is a short exact sequence of sheaves of \(K\)-modules.
