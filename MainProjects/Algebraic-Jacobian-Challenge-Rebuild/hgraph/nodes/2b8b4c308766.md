\provenancetag{CUSTOM}
  
  For a scheme \(X\) and an open \(O \subseteq X\) with \(O \subseteq \emptyset\) (that is,
  \(O = \emptyset\)), the unit group \(\Gamma(X, O)^{\times}\) is trivial (a subsingleton).
