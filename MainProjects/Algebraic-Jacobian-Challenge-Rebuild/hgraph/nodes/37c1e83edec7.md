Let \(A \to B\) be a module-finite homomorphism of \(k\)-algebras and \(V \subseteq X_A\) an affine
  open. Then the cover-piece ring \(\Gamma(X_B, \mathrm{cg}^{-1}V)\) is a module-finite \(\Gamma(X_A,
  V)\)-algebra.
