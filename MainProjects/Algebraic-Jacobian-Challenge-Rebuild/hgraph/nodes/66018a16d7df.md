Let \(A\) be a Noetherian domain, \(b, y \in A\), and let
  \(Q = \bigl((b) : y\bigr)\) be a prime ideal. If the localisation \(A_Q\) is a
  regular local ring, then \(\mathrm{ht}(Q) \leq 1\).
