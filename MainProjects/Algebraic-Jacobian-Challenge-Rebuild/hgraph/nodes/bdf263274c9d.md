Let \(S\) be an \(R\)-algebra with \(\Omega_{S/R}\) a free \(S\)-module, and
  let \(S_M\) be a localisation of \(S\) at a multiplicative set \(M\). Then
  \(\Omega_{S_M/R}\) is a free \(S_M\)-module.
