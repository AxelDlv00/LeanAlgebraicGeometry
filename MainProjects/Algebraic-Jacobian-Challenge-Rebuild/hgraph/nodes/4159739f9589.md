Let \((R, \mathfrak m)\) be a Noetherian local ring with residue field \(\kappa\) and
  \(M\) a nonzero finite \(R\)-module with \(\mathrm{depth}(M) = D \in \mathbb{N}\).
  Then \(\Ext^D_R(\kappa, M) \neq 0\).
