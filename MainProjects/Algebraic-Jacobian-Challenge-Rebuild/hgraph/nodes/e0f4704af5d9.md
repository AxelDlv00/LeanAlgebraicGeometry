  `divFunctor C π 0` is singleton-valued, and the slice''s terminal object `Over.mk

  `abelSigmaChart`, `IsChartUniv` and the rest of the `Pic0Chart*` cluster all take

  `rep : (divFunctor C π n).RepresentableBy D` as a hypothesis, and until now nothing
  produced

  "rep :

  differ by

  about a third, and the task brief that prompted this work quoted a third figure
  again.  The

  claim that matters is a zero, not a large number: **the producer side was empty**,
  verified by

  reading the three existing `…representableBy` declarations

  (`DivRepGlobalData.representableBy`, `divFunctor_representableBy_of_chartClause`,

  `DivRepAffinePullback.representableBy`), each of which takes an unwitnessed structure
  whose only

  producer is itself gated on U2.


  **What it does not do.**  It does not give the seam an atlas at this parameter:
  a chart at

  `nn i = 0` still needs a legal index `Z i` of degree `m·d₁ − 0`, and the coverage
  antecedent is

  untouched.

  `not_mem_chartLocus_of_two_le_genus_zero_param`
