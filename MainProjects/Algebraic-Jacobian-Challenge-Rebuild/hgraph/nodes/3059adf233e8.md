Apply the flat-proper equivalence-relation theorem to construct a scheme
  \(Q = D_{\mathrm{adm}}/R\) over \(\Spec k\), with quotient map
  \(D_{\mathrm{adm}} \to Q\), and identify \(R\) with
  \(D_{\mathrm{adm}} \times_Q D_{\mathrm{adm}}\). This is a construction of the concrete
  quotient, not a theorem with quotient data as a hypothesis.
