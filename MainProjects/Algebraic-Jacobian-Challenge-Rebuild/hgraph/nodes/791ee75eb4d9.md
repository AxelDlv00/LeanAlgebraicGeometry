  §1.6, in the

  For `f : T'' ⟶ T` and `t : T''.left`, membership of `t` in the pulled-back locus

  in the original are the *same* condition on the *same* twisted class, read at two

  plainly so that no lane cites it as if transport (i) were discharged.  What it *does*
  is fix

  `f`,

  `hinv` when

  needs, and is

  *not* automatic for an arbitrary morphism of tests.  The residue between the two
  is why

  `hinv` is a hypothesis and not a `rw`.'
