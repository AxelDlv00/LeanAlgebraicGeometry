Under the standing finiteness hypotheses, subtracting a closed point \(x\) from a Weil divisor
  \(D\) drops the Euler characteristic by the residue degree:
  \[
    \chi(\mathcal O(D)) \;=\; \chi(\mathcal O(D - x)) + [\kappa(x):K].
  \]
