Let \(X\) be a scheme whose underlying space is irreducible, \(V \subseteq X\) an affine
  open with \(\Gamma(X, V)\) a Dedekind domain, and \(x \in V\). Then every generalization
  of \(x\) is the generic point of \(X\) or \(x\) itself.
