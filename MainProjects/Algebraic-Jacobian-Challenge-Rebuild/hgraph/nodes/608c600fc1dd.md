A ring homomorphism \(f : R \to S\) induces the ring homomorphism
  \[
    f[\varepsilon] : R[\varepsilon] \to S[\varepsilon], \qquad
    a + b\varepsilon \longmapsto f(a) + f(b)\varepsilon,
  \]
  applying \(f\) to both components.
