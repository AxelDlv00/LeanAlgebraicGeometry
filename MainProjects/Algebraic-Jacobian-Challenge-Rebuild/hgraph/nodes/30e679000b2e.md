Every unit \(u \in R[\varepsilon]^\times\) factors as a constant unit times a
  truncated exponential:
  \[
    u \;=\; \mathrm{inl}^\times\bigl(\mathrm{fst}^\times(u)\bigr)
      \cdot \mathrm{tExp}\bigl(\mathrm{fst}(u^{-1}) \cdot \mathrm{snd}(u)\bigr).
  \]
