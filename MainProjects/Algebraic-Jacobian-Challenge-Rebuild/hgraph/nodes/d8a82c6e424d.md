\provenancetag{REFERENCE}
  
  
  
  
  Let \(k\) be a commutative ring and \(X\) an affine scheme over \(\Spec k\). Then
  \[
    H^1\bigl(X, \struct{X/k}\bigr) \;=\; 0 .
  \]
