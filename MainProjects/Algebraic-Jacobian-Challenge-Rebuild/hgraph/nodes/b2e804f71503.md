\provenancetag{ADAPTED}
  For \(g \in K(X)^\times\) and any Weil divisor \(D\), multiplication by \(g\) is an isomorphism of
  sheaves of \(K\)-modules
  \[
    \mathcal O(D) \;\cong\; \mathcal O\bigl(D - \mathrm{div}\, g\bigr).
  \]
