  Suppose \(k\) is separably closed.  For every field extension \(K/k\) and every
  \(\lambda\in\mathrm{pic}^0(C,\Spec K)\), there is an invertible sheaf
  \(\mathcal M\) of degree \(g(C)\) on \(C\) such that
  \(\lambda\in U_{\mathcal M}(\Spec K)\).
