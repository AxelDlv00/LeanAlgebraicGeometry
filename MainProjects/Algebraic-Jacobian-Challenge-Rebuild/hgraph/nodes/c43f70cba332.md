\provenancetag{ADAPTED}
  
  
  
  
  Let \(R\) be Noetherian and \(\delta : A \to B\) surjective with \(A\) flat, \(B\)
  projective, and \(K = \ker \delta\) finitely generated. Then \(K\) is a finite
  projective \(R\)-module.
