Let \(\iota : F \to G\) be a monomorphism of sheaves of \(R\)-modules on a site and let
  \(U\) be an object of the site. If \(c \in G(U)\) maps to zero in \((\coker \iota)(U)\),
  then \(c = \iota_U(a)\) for a (unique) \(a \in F(U)\).
