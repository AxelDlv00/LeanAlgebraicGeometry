Under the standing hypotheses of \ref{def:classDeg}, for every class \(L\) and every
  integer \(n \in \Z\),
  \[
    \deg_{\Pic}\bigl(L^{\,n}\bigr) \;=\; n \cdot \deg_{\Pic}(L).
  \]
