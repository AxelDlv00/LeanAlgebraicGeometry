Let \(X\) be integral and reduced, \(Y\), \(Z\), \(S\) separated, \(s_Y\),
  \(s_Z\) locally of finite type, and \(a\), \(b\) as in
  \ref{def:ratmap_pairPartialMap}. Then the class of the pairing representative
  equals \((a, b)\).
