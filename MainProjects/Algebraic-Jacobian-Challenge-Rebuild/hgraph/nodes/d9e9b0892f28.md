The map \(\mathrm{tExp} : (R, +) \to R[\varepsilon]^\times\) is a group homomorphism,
  \[
    \mathrm{tExp}(b + c) = \mathrm{tExp}(b) \cdot \mathrm{tExp}(c),
  \]
  and it is injective.
