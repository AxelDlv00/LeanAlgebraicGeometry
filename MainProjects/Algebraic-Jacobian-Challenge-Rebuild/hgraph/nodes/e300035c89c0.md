Let \(U \subseteq X_A\) be an affine open. A unit \(t \in \Gamma(X_B,
  \mathrm{cg}^{-1}U)^{\times}\) whose two coprojection pullbacks agree, \(u_1^{*} t = u_2^{*}
  t\), descends to a unit: there is \(s \in \Gamma(X_A, U)^{\times}\) with \(\mathrm{cg}^{*} s =
  t\).
