Let \(V \subseteq Y\) be an open with \(V \subseteq \operatorname{range}(w)\). Then pullback of
  sections along \(w\) is an isomorphism of rings
  \(\Gamma(Y, V) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \Gamma(Z, w^{-1}V)\).
