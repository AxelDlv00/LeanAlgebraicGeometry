Let \(E, F\) be presented \'etale covers of \(A\) and \(h \colon B_E \to B_F\) a
  homomorphism of \(A\)-algebras. If \(\lambda \in \mathrm{Desc}(C,E)\), then
  \(h^{*}\lambda \in \mathrm{Desc}(C,F)\).
