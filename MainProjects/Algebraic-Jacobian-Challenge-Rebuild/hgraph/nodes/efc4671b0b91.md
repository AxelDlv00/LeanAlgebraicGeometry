Let \(R\) be a Noetherian domain, \(t \in R\) nonzero, and \(\mathfrak p\) a
  prime containing \(t\). Then there is a prime
  \(\mathfrak q \subseteq \mathfrak p\) of height exactly \(1\) with
  \(t \in \mathfrak q\).
