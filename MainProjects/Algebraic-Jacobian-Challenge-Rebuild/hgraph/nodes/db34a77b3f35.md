For a ring homomorphism \(f : R \to S\):
  \(f[\varepsilon]^\times(\mathrm{tExp}(b)) = \mathrm{tExp}(f(b))\) for all
  \(b \in R\), and
  \(\mathrm{fst}^\times \circ f[\varepsilon]^\times = f^\times \circ
  \mathrm{fst}^\times\) on \(R[\varepsilon]^\times\).
