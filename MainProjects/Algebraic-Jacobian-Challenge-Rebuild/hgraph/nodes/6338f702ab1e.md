For points \(x, y : 1 \to G\) of a group object \(G\), the
  \emph{point translation}
  \[
    \tau_{x,y} \;=\; \rho_y \circ \rho_x^{-1} \;:\; G \cong G
  \]
  is the automorphism ``multiply on the right by \(x^{-1} y\)''.
