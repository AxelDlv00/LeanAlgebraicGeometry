Equality under the sheafified Abel map is equivalent to the raw equalizer sieve being
  covering for the big-etale topology. Membership of an arrow in that sieve is equivalent
  to the pulled-back divisor classes differing by \(\mathrm{picFromBase}\) on every affine
  open. Thus the sheafified kernel is locally the same concrete relative-linear-equivalence
  relation, including the quotient by line bundles from the base.
