In the situation of \ref{lem:slice_factorisation}, let moreover \(f : X \times Y \to Z\) be
  a morphism which is constant on the slice over \(y\), with value a point \(c\) of \(Z\):
  \(f \circ \sigma_y = c \circ t_X\). Then
  \[
    f \circ p \;=\; c \circ t_T .
  \]
