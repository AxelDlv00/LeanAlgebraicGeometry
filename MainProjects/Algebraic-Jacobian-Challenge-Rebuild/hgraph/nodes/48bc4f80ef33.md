For a partial map \(g : Y \dashrightarrow Z\) out of an integral scheme,
  defined at the generic point \(\eta_Y\), the generic morphism of
  \(g\)'s rational-map class sends the closed point of \(\Spec K(Y)\) to the
  value \(g(\eta_Y)\).
