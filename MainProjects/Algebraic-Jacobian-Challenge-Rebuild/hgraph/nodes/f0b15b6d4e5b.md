  Let \(\varphi : B \twoheadrightarrow A\) be a surjection of Noetherian local
  rings whose target has strictly smaller embedding dimension than the source.
  Then some \(x \in \ker\varphi\) does not lie in \(\mathfrak m_B^2\).
