The Yoneda sheaf of \(Q\) and the degree-zero Picard Sigma functor are coequalizers of
  the same represented kernel pair. The induced isomorphism is compatible with the
  structure maps to \(\Spec k\), and therefore descends from the Sigma-extension to
  \(\mathrm{pic}^0_{\mathrm{Set}}(C)\) on the slice.
