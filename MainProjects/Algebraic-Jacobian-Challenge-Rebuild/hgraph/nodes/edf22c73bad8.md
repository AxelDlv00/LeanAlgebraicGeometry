\provenancetag{CUSTOM}
  
  
  Let \((A, \mathfrak m)\) be a Noetherian local ring with
  \(\dim_\kappa \mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2 \leq \dim A\). Then \(A\) is a regular local
  ring.
