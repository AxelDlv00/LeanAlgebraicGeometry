Let \(S\) be a nonzero standard-smooth \(R\)-algebra of relative dimension
  \(n\), and \(S_M\) a nonzero localisation of \(S\). Then \(\Omega_{S_M/R}\)
  is free of rank \(n\) over \(S_M\).
