Let \((w_i)_{i \in \iota}\) be a clopen partition of \(Y\). Two \v Cech Picard classes
  \(L, L' \in \Pic(Y)\) with \(w_i^{*} L = w_i^{*} L'\) for every \(i\) are equal.
