If \(D_0, D_1 \in \Div^{\,g}_{\mathrm{Zar}}(S)\) are classified by the \emph{same}
  morphism \(v \colon \Spec S \to \mathbf{Div}\), then \(D_0 = D_1\). Equivalently,
  \(D \mapsto \gamma_S(D)\) is injective.
