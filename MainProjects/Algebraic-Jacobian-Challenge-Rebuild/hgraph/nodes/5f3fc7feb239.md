For \(T\)-points \(a, b : T \to G\) of a group object \(G\),
  \[
    \mu \circ \bigl\langle\, \delta \circ \langle a, b\rangle,\ b \,\bigr\rangle
    \;=\; a .
  \]
  In pointwise notation: \((a b^{-1})\, b = a\).
