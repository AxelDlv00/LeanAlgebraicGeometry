\provenancetag{CUSTOM}
  
  
  
  Let \((A, \mathfrak m)\) be a regular local (Noetherian) ring of Krull
  dimension \(\geq 2\). Then there are \(u, v \in \mathfrak m\) with
  \(u \neq 0\) and \(v\) regular modulo \((u)\).
