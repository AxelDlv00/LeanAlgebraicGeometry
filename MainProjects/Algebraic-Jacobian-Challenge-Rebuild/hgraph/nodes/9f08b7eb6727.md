\(\mu_a \circ \mu_b = \mu_{ab}\); \(\mu_1 = \mathrm{id}\); on truncated exponentials
  \(\mu_a^\times(\mathrm{tExp}(b)) = \mathrm{tExp}(ab)\); and for a ring homomorphism
