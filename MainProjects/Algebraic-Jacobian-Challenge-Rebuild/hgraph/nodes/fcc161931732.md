For an open \(V \subseteq Y\) with \(V \subseteq \operatorname{range}(w)\), the isomorphism of
  unit groups
  \[
    \Gamma(Y, V)^{\times} \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \Gamma(Z, w^{-1}V)^{\times}
  \]
  induced by pullback of sections along \(w\).
