  `free_of_cyclic_mod_eps` that a consumer holding only "the restriction along `ε

  `Submodule.exists_sub_smul_mem_of_quotient_cyclic` supplies `m`, and `free_of_cyclic_mod_eps`

  of the

  reduction is exactly the input it needs.


  **One deviation from the sibling''s statement, and it is not cosmetic.** AJC states
  this at

theorem free_of_quotient_eps_cyclic
    (M : Type u) [AddCommGroup M] [Module (DualNumber A) M]
    [Module.Invertible (DualNumber A) M]
    (h : ∃ y : M ⧸ (Ideal.span {(ε : DualNumber A)} • (⊤ : Submodule (DualNumber A) M)),
      ∀ z, ∃ r : DualNumber A, z = r • y) :
    Module.Free (DualNumber A) M := by
  obtain ⟨m, hm⟩ := Submodule.exists_sub_smul_mem_of_quotient_cyclic
    (Ideal.span {(ε : DualNumber A)} • (⊤ : Submodule (DualNumber A) M)) h
  exact free_of_cyclic_mod_eps A M m hm
