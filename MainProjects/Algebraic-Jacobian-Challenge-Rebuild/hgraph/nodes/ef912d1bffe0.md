Let \(k\) be a commutative ring, \(R\) a commutative \(k\)-algebra, \(M\) a
  \(k\)-module and \(e\) a \(k\)-linear endomorphism of \(M\). If \(M_e\) is a finite
  \(k[t]\)-module, then \((R \otimes_k M)_{\mathrm{id} \otimes e}\) is a finite
  \(R[t]\)-module.
