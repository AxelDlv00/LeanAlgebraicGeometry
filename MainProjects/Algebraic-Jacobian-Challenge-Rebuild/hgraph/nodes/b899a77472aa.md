Let \(g : X \to Y\) be a morphism, regarded as a rational map, and let
  \(p : W \to X\) have open underlying map. Then the precomposition of the
  rational map \(g\) with \(p\) is the rational map of the composite morphism
  \(g \circ p : W \to Y\).
