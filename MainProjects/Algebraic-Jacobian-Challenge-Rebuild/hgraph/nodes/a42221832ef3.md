  Let \(B\) be a Dedekind domain, \(P\) a nonzero prime, \(f \neq 0\) and \(n \ge 0\).
  Then
  \[
    f \in P^n \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad n \le \mathrm{ord}_P(f) .
  \]
