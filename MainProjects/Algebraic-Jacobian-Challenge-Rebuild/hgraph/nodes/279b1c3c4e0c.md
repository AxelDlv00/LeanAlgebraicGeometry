For \(X\) reduced and \(Y\) separated, the \emph{maximal representative} of a
  rational map \(f : X \dashrightarrow Y\) is the partial map with domain
  \(\operatorname{Dom}(f)\) obtained by gluing all representatives; its class
  is \(f\).
