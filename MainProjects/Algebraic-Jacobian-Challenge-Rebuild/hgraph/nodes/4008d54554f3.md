Let \(\sigma : k \to A\) and let \(u \in A\) be a unit. Then the morphism
  \([1 : u] : \Spec A \to \PP^1\) through the chart \(U_0\) equals the morphism
  \([u^{-1} : 1]\) through the chart \(U_1\) (both as in \ref{def:P1_fromSpecChart}).
