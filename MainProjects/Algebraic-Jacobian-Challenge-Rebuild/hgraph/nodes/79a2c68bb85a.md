lemma pushFieldPoint_left [Algebra k K] [Algebra L K] [IsScalarTower k L K]
    (s : overSpec L K ⟶ T) :
    (pushFieldPoint k s).left = s.left :=
  Category.id_comp s.left

`t : overSpec k K ⟶ (Over.map σ).obj T` makes `K` an `L`-algebra, via `Spec.preimage` of
