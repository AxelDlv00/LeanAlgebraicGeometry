Let \((R, \mathfrak m)\) be a Cohen--Macaulay Noetherian local ring and
  \(\mathfrak p \in \operatorname{Ass}(R)\). Then
  \(\operatorname{coheight}(\mathfrak p) = \dim R\); equivalently
  \(\dim(R/\mathfrak p) = \dim R\).
