Let \(A\) be a domain, and let \(u, v, b, x \in A\) with \(u \neq 0\) and
  \(v\) regular modulo \((u)\) (i.e.\ \(v s \in (u)\) implies \(s \in (u)\)).
  If \(u x \in (b)\) and \(v x \in (b)\), then \(x \in (b)\).
