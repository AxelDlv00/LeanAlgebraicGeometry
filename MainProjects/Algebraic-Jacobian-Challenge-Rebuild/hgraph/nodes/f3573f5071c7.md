For every Weil divisor \(D\) and every \(n \in \N\), multiplication by \(u^{-n}\) carries
  \(\struct Y(D)(V_0)\) isomorphically onto \(\struct Y(D + nF)(V_0)\); that is,
  \[
    \struct Y(D + nF)(V_0) \;=\; u^{-n}\cdot \struct Y(D)(V_0)
  \]
  as \(K\)-submodules of \(K(Y)\).
