For a rational map \(f : X \dashrightarrow Y\), a morphism \(p : W \to X\) with
  open underlying map, and a morphism \(g : Y \to Z\),
  \[
    g \circ (f \circ p) \;=\; (g \circ f) \circ p
    \qquad\text{as rational maps } W \dashrightarrow Z .
  \]
