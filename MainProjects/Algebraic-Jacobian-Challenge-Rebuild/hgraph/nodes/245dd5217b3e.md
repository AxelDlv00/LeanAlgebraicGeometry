Two admissible divisor families have the same Abel value exactly when, on every affine
  open of the test scheme, the ratio of their Picard classes lies in
  \(\mathrm{picFromBase}\). Thus the concrete kernel is precisely relative linear
  equivalence, including the quotient by classes pulled back from the base.
