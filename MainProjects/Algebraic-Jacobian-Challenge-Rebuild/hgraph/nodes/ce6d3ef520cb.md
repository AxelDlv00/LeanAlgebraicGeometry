  For every \(k\)-test scheme \(T\), restriction along a finite Galois extension \(L/k\)
  identifies \(\mathrm{pic}^0(C,T)\) with the Galois-invariant degree-zero Picard classes on
  \(T_L\), naturally in \(T\).
