The structure morphism \(\PP^1 \to \Spec k\) is the canonical morphism
  \(\Proj A \to \Spec A_0\) followed by the isomorphism \(\Spec A_0 \cong \Spec k\) induced by
  \ref{def:P1_gradeZero}.
