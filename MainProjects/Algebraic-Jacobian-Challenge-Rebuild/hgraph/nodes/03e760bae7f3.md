If \(U_0\) and \(U_1\) are affine opens of \(X\) (still with \(U_0 \cup U_1 = X\)), then
  \(\mathrm{delta} : \Gamma(X, U_0 \cap U_1) \to H^1(X,\struct{X/k})\) is surjective.
