Let \(x'\) be a non-generic point of \(X\), with tracked point equations at \(x'\)
  (\ref{def:pointEquations}, from the tracked uniformizer data of
  \ref{lem:pointUniformizerData}), and let \(W\) be an integral scheme over \(\Spec K\)
  and \(f : W \to X\) a morphism with \(f(\eta_W) = \eta_X\). The \emph{pulled tracked
  point system} is the pullback of this local-equation system along \(f\)
  (\ref{def:localEquations_pullback}), with regularity discharged by integrality of
  both schemes (\ref{lem:pullbackEqn_regular}). Its \emph{trivialising unit} is the
  pullback \(f^{\sharp}(t) \in K(W)^{\times}\) of the chosen uniformizer \(t\) at
  \(x'\) (\ref{lem:uniformizer}, \ref{def:functionFieldMap}). This is the construction
  of the pulled tracked point system of the base-field transition
  (\ref{def:pointTransitionEquations}), for an arbitrary morphism mapping generic
  point to generic point.
