Let \(K\) be a commutative ring and \(X\) a scheme locally of finite type over
  \(\Spec K\). For every affine open \(V\) of \(X\), the algebra map
  \(K \to \Gamma(X, V)\) of \ref{def:overAlgebraMap} is of finite type.
