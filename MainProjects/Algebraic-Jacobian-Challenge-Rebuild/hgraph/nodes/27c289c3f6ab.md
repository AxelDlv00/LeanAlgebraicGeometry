Let \(k\) be a field, \(\iota\) a finite index type, and \(P\) a prime ideal of
  \(k[x_i : i \in \iota]\). Then there is a natural number \(d\) with
  \[
    d \leq \mathrm{trdeg}_k\bigl(k[x_i : i \in \iota]/P\bigr)
    \quad\text{and}\quad
    \#\iota \leq \mathrm{ht}(P) + d.
  \]
