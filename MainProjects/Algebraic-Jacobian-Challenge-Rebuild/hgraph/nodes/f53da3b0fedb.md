Let \(f \in k[X_0, X_1]\) be homogeneous of positive degree. The inclusion
  \(D_+(f) \to \PP^1\) is the composite of the canonical identification
  \(D_+(f) \cong \Spec A_{(f)}\) with the affine model \(\Spec A_{(f)} \to \PP^1\).
