The class map is a group homomorphism \(\Div(X) \to \Pic(X)\), trivial on principal divisors:
  \[
    \struct X(0) = 1, \quad \struct X(D + D') = \struct X(D) \cdot \struct X(D'), \quad \struct
    X(\mathrm{div}\, g) = 1 \ (g \in K(X)^{\times}).
  \]
