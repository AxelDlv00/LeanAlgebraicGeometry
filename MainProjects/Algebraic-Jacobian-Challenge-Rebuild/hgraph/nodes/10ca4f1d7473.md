  Define \(\mathrm{PicRankOneOpen}(C,T)\) to be the set of
  \(\lambda\in\mathrm{pic}^{g(C)}(C,T)\) admitting a rank-one local presentation.

  \[
    \mathrm{PicRankOneOpen}(C)\subseteq\mathrm{pic}^{g(C)}(C).
