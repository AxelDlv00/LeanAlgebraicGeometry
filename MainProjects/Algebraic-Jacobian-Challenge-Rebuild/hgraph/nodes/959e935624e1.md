Granting \ref{thm:divRepUniversalFamily}, and separately supplying a chart family of
  relatively representable open immersions together with the required locally-surjective
  \(\Sigma\)-map, the Abel map \(D \mapsto \OO(D)\) (\ref{def:divFamZar}) can be used as
  the conditional input to \ref{def:pic0RepresentableByOfCharts} after twisting by a fixed
  divisor of degree \(g\).  Only after those antecedents are inhabited does the
  degree-zero Picard functor become representable and yield a Jacobian datum
  (\ref{def:jacobian_data}) --- hence, through the frozen definition
  (\ref{def:jacobian}), the Jacobian of \(C\).

  The universal family, the open-immersion chart certificates, and local surjectivity are
  distinct unproved inputs here.  The passage from a representation over a separably closed
  base to one over \(k\) is a further descent step, not addressed here.
