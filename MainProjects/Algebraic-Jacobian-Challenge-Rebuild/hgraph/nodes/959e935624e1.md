Granting \ref{thm:divRepUniversalFamily}, the Abel map \(D \mapsto \OO(D)\)
  (\ref{def:divFamZar}) becomes a morphism of functors from the representable functor
  \(\Div^{\,g}_{\mathrm{Zar}}\) to the degree-zero Picard functor after twisting by a fixed
  divisor of degree \(g\), and the loci over which it is representable by open immersions
  supply the chart family required by \ref{def:pic0RepresentableByOfCharts}. That
  hypothesis being met, the degree-zero Picard functor of \(C\) is representable, and the
  representing object with its universal element is a Jacobian datum
  (\ref{def:jacobian_data}) --- hence, through the frozen definition
  (\ref{def:jacobian}), the Jacobian of \(C\).

  Neither the chart family nor the local surjectivity of the resulting maps is proved; both
  rest on \ref{thm:divRepUniversalFamily}. The passage from a representation over a
  separably closed base to one over \(k\) is a further descent step, not addressed here.
