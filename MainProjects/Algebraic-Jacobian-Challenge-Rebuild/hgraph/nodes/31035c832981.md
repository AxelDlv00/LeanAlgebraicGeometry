The source \(D_{\mathrm{adm}}\) admits a closed immersion into a concrete pair of
  Grassmannians. Pulling back the finite affine Grassmannian cover gives a finite affine
  cover of the source; in particular the source is quasi-compact, locally of finite type,
  and separated. This is the concrete project-local input used in the quotient argument.
