\paragraph{5. Cohomology and base change.}
At this point, we have to digress to prove a theorem of Grothendieck on the
behavior of cohomology groups of a family of vector bundles $E_y$ on a family
of varieties $X_y$, parametrized by points $y \in Y$ where $X_y$ is assumed
to be a flat family of varieties. An important consequence of this result is
the semicontinuity of the dimensions of the cohomology groups of the $E_y$.

We assume the following basic result.

\paragraph{Theorem.}
\textit{If $f : X \to Y$ is a proper morphism of locally noetherian
preschemes and $\mathcal{F}$ a coherent sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules on
$X$, for all $p \geq 0$ the direct image sheaves $R^p f_*(\mathcal{F})$ are
coherent sheaves of $\mathcal{O}_Y$-modules.}

We recall the following definition. If $f : X \to Y$ is a morphism of
preschemes and $\mathcal{F}$ a quasicoherent sheaf on $X$, $\mathcal{F}$ is
said to be \textit{flat over $Y$} or \textit{$f$-flat} if for each
$x \in X$, $\mathcal{F}_x$ (for its natural structure of
$\mathcal{O}_{Y, f(x)}$-module) is $\mathcal{O}_{Y, f(x)}$-flat. It is easily
shown that this condition is equivalent to requiring that for $U \subset X$,
$V \subset Y$ with $U$ and $V$ affine open, and $f(U) \subset V$,
$\mathcal{F}(U)$ is a flat $\mathcal{O}_Y(V)$-module.

The main result of this section is the following

\paragraph{Theorem.}
\textit{Let $f : X \to Y$ be a proper morphism of noetherian schemes with
$Y = \Spec A$ affine, and $\mathcal{F}$ a coherent sheaf on $X$, flat over
$Y$. There is a finite complex
$K^{\cdot} : 0 \to K^0 \to K^1 \to \cdots \to K^n \to 0$
of finitely generated projective $A$-modules and an isomorphism of functors}
\[
  H^p(X \times_Y \Spec B, \mathcal{F} \otimes_A B)
    \cong H^p(K^{\cdot} \otimes_A B), \quad (p \geq 0)
\]
\textit{on the category of $A$-algebras $B$.}

\paragraph{Proof.}
Choose a finite affine covering $\mathfrak{A} = \{U_i\}_{i \in I}$ of $X$ by
affine open subsets. Then the \v{C}ech complex
$C^{\cdot} = C^{\cdot}(\mathfrak{A}, \mathcal{F})
  = \oplus\, C^p(\mathfrak{A}, \mathcal{F})$
of alternating \v{C}ech cochains on $\mathfrak{A}$ with coefficients in
$\mathcal{F}$ is a finite complex of $A$-flat modules, whose cohomologies are
isomorphic to the cohomology groups $H^p(X, \mathcal{F})$.
