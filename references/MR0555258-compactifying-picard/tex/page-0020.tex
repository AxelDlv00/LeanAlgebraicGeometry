projection. Since $p$ is proper, the subset
\[
Q=\overline Q-p\bigl([\,(\overline X-X)\times\overline Q\,]

\]

\[
(1\times g)^{-1}(\Supp(G))\cap[(\overline X-X)\times T]=\varnothing,

$\Quot^{\phi}_{(\overline F/\overline X/S)}(T)$ lies in
$\Quot^{\phi}_{(F/X/S)}(T)$. Thus $Q$ represents
$\Quot^{\phi}_{(F/X/S)}$.

\paragraph{(2.7) Corollary.}
\textit{Let $f:X\to S$ be a finitely presented, locally projective (resp.
locally quasi-projective) morphism of schemes, and let $F$ be a locally
finitely presented $\mathcal O_X$-Module. Then $\Quot_{(F/X/S)}$ is
representable by a disjoint union of locally finitely presented, locally
projective (resp. locally quasi-projective) $S$-schemes.}

\paragraph{Proof.}
This assertion is local on $S$ [EGA $0_{\mathrm I}$, 4.5.5], so we may
assume $S$ is affine and $f$ is projective. The assertion now follows easily
from (2.6), from Example (2.2(i)), and from [EGA $0_{\mathrm I}$, 4.5.4].

\paragraph{(2.8) Corollary.}
\textit{Let $f:X\to S$ be a strongly projective (resp. strongly
quasi-projective) morphism of schemes. Then for any polynomial
$\phi\in\mathbb Q[T]$, the functor $\Hilb^{\phi}_{(X/S)}$ is representable
by a strongly projective (resp. strongly quasi-projective) $S$-scheme.}

\paragraph{Proof.}
The assertion follows immediately from (2.6) with $F=\mathcal O_X$, with
$B=\mathcal O_S$, and with $\nu=0$.
