\textbf{Purpose.} This packet is a compact, auditable briefing for a human mathematician. It is not a completion claim. The question is whether the current finite-descent route can produce an unconditional original-field Picard-zero representer, or whether the route must change.

\section*{Executive diagnosis}
The rank-one and separably-closed portions are genuine and kernel-clean. The arbitrary-field capstone is absent. The two decisive missing bridges are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a binder-free finite-stage \code{RepresentableBy}/Yoneda equivalence for the glued carrier;
\item exact-carrier projectivity or \code{FiniteInAffine} control of the finite Galois orbit.
\end{enumerate}
The compile chain needed to test these bridges is also uncertified, so many sessions have produced prerequisites and conditional consumers without moving the headline theorem.
