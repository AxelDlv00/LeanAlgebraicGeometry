STATUS active -> blocked, 2026-07-26 (run 0048 round 4). Not a demotion of the lane's importance: an honest label. Nothing in this subtree can be costed, let alone worked, until one question is answered, and it is a question about mathematics rather than about Lean.

THE QUESTION. The chart-confinement no-go is proved structurally and degree-agnostically, but the only witness ever exhibited (F = tX^2 + XY + tY^2 over k[t], C = P^1, pi = id) has degree 2 on a curve of GENUS 0, while the functor pins the degree to the genus (hchi, DivRepAffKit.lean:62; IsCertified clause (c2), DivisorFamily.lean:426-437). On the campaign's own stratum: at g = 0 the no-go is vacuous (degree-0 relative divisors have empty support); at g = 1 base shrink DOES evade it (a section's support locus is Spec R, so removing one base point confines it, and the gate is pointwise on the base after I-0320); at g >= 2 NO WITNESS HAS EVER BEEN EXHIBITED. See I-0356.

WHY THE ANSWER DECIDES THE WHOLE SUBTREE.
 * If a g >= 2 witness exists, the certificate route as designed is dead over an arbitrary field, and exactly one of R1 (p1-aut -> fibre-avoid -> cert-relocalize), R2 (generalise FinCoverData's piece type) or descent from a large field (dat-g) must be chosen — and R1 alone provably cannot suffice, because the pointwise gate then needs two kappa(p)-rational points of P^1 off the fibre's support image at every prime, which fails over small finite residue fields. That is I-0346's field-size question, and it is THIS obstruction seen pointwise, not a separate one. So R1 would have to be R1-plus-descent.
 * If no witness exists — i.e. on-stratum seed divisors are always confinable after a base shrink — the certificate route REOPENS and p1-aut, fibre-avoid, cert-relocalize and field-size can all be deleted.
Costing p1-aut or R2 before that answer is spending a session on a coin flip.
