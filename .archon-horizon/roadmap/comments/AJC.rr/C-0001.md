The mathematically interesting finding is that the ADELIC form of chi class-invariance is cheaper than the sheaf-theoretic one. Multiplication by g in K(X)^* is a k-linear automorphism carrying Gamma(U, O(D)) ONTO Gamma(U, O(D - div g)), and it carries the coboundary onto the shifted coboundary, so it descends to H1(D) = H1(D - div g). That makes ell, h1 and chi linear-equivalence invariants with NO finiteness and NO chi-additivity input -- whereas the sibling project's sheaf-level mulEquivDivisorSheaf route needs the chi machinery even to state the transport. Worth remembering when weighing 'port from AJCR' against 'rederive adelically'.
