1. NO-LEAK IS THE Z-CLOPEN CONDITION, proved both ways (isClopen_trace_of_supportLeak_eq_empty,
supportLeak_eq_empty_of_isClopen_trace, Picard/DivSchemeCertZarLeak.lean). The piece trace supp cap U is
ALWAYS open in the subspace supp (U is open), so it is clopen there exactly when it is closed in the
ambient space, i.e. exactly when the leak locus is empty. So the assembler's per-piece no-leak clause and
the recorded Z-clopen certificate principle of I-0209 are literally the same statement. This explains
retrospectively why successive fibre analyses never closed the clause: it is not a fibrewise condition
at all, it asks that the piece trace be a clopen part of the divisor scheme.

2. THE MISSING BRICK I-0209 NAMED IS NOW HALF-BUILT. Its algebraic core is elementary and landed:
Module.Projective.quotient_span_singleton_one_sub_of_isIdempotentElem and its flat companion
Module.Flat.quotient_span_singleton_one_sub_of_isIdempotentElem -- cutting by an idempotent e makes
multiplication by e a well-defined R-linear section of B -> B/(1-e), so the quotient is a RETRACT of B
and inherits projectivity/flatness with no finiteness or flatness input. The flat version is the
applicable one: piece section rings are known flat over R (FinCoverData.flat_sections_pieces, from the
FREE pinned-chart rings), not projective.

3. WHAT IS STILL MISSING is the geometric half: produce the packet idempotent. Mathlib supplies the

existsUnique form) turns a clopen subset of a spectrum into an idempotent whose basic open it is. So the
remaining chain is: divisor scheme finite over Spec R => its clopen packets => idempotent =>
basic-open piece swallowing the packet => clopen trace => no-leak => (c1). Every link except the first
now exists. The first is exactly I-0209's 'W-as-algebra extraction'.
