WARNING FOR THE NEXT SESSION: step (ii) is the trap. Deriving the packet idempotents FROM finite
projectivity of W(d) and then using them to prove the certificate (which contains that very finite
projectivity as clause (c2)) is circular. The non-circular version must get the packet decomposition
from the geometry -- the divisor scheme Z = V(d) being finite over Spec R by properness plus the
degree-g fibre structure -- NOT from W(d)'s own certificate clauses. Concretely: build the idempotents
in Gamma(Z) from finiteness of Z over the base, then transport to the chart product via the piece
traces. Two no-gos already bound the alternatives (I-0323): no cleverer submodule, no cleverer pieces.
