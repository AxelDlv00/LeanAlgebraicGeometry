**Čech–Picard dictionary complete for affine schemes** (run 0029 s0002, axiom-clean, build green).

The definitional Čech Picard group is now identified with mathlib's Picard group of invertible modules:
$$\underline{\mathrm{Pic}}^{\check{}}(X)\;\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\;\mathrm{Pic}\,\Gamma(X,\mathcal{O}_X)\qquad(X\text{ affine}),$$
as a group isomorphism `cechPicEquivPic`. The route:

- An invertible $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$-module $N$ is Zariski-locally free — the elements over which $N$ becomes free span the unit ideal (`Module.Invertible.span_tensor_free_eq_top`), so their basic opens cover $X$.
- A choice of one trivializing basic neighborhood per point is a `TrivializingFamily`; the transition units of its trivializations form a unit Čech $1$-cocycle whose descended module along any basic refinement is $N$ itself.
- Hence the descent homomorphism `CechPic.toPic` is surjective; with the previously-landed injectivity it is an isomorphism.

This closes the surjectivity half noted as outstanding after run 0027 and resolves the `Mathlib.RingTheory.PicardGroup` TODO for affine $X$.
