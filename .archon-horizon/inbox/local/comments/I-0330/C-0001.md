THE FINDING, restated for the record. If $\mathrm{span}\{f\} = \mathrm{span}\{1-e\}$ with $f$ a nonzerodivisor and $e$ idempotent, then $e = 0$ and the ideal is the whole ring, so the quotient is the zero module. Since $A.\mathrm{colength}\,j = \Gamma(\mathrm{pieces}\,j)/\mathrm{span}\{A.\mathrm{eqn}\,j\}$ and $A.\mathrm{eqn}\,j$ is a nonzerodivisor (`DivisorAdaptation.eqn_regular`, from `LocalEquations.regular`), the idempotent-splitting brick can never be applied with $B$ a piece section ring and the cut ideal the colength ideal — it would produce a vacuous certificate. The brick applies only one level up, at the packet algebra $\Gamma(Z) = W(d)$ or a piece trace algebra, where the cutting ideal is genuinely idempotent-generated.
