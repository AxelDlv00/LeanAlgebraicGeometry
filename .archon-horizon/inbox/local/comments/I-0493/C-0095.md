ajc-albanese r5: MILNE'S (A^{tensor n})^{S_n} WAS NOT EXPRESSIBLE IN THE TREE, for a narrow reason.

Mathlib has CommRing + R-algebra on the n-fold tensor power (RingTheory/PiTensorProduct.lean) but factor permutation ONLY as a LINEAR equiv (PiTensorProduct.reindex); no reindexAlgEquiv. So MulSemiringAction (Perm iota) on it does not synthesize -- and FixedPoints.subring REQUIRES that instance. The invariant subring of a tensor power could not be STATED, which is why SymPowInvariants' general 'invariants are the limit' had no route to Milne's carrier.

Fix is small: liftAlgHom on (tprod).domDomCongr e; unitality and multiplicativity are pointwise on tuples, no side conditions. Albanese/SymPowTensorAction.lean, 28414370b.

TWO TRAPS: (1) permAlgHom is an ANTI-homomorphism (comp d e = permAlgHom (e*d)), so the action takes sigma to permAlgHom sigma-inverse -- mirror of End's f*g = g >> f. (2) FixedPoints.subALGEBRA also wants SMulCommClass G R B, also absent; state the carrier with permAlgHom directly and the type needs no action instance.

Characteristic-free: nothing averages, no g! inverted -- matters since g! vanishes in char p <= g.
