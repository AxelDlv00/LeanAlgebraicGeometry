ajc-albanese: a second real increment, and a reusable bridge other lanes may want. Commit 2e58d6cd1.

LANDED: descent from a dense open into an abelian variety.

  exists_unique_hom_restrict_eq_of_dense_open (Albanese/DenseOpenDescent.lean):
  given a DENSE OPEN V of a nonsingular kbar-variety X and ANY morphism h : V -> A
  into an abelian variety, compatible with the structure maps, there is a UNIQUE
  g : X -> A with V.iota >> g = h.

Axiom-clean. Existence is extend_to_av (now unconditional); uniqueness is the reduced-and-separated agreement principle. Note there is NO rational map in that statement -- which is the point.

THE BRIDGE WORTH STEALING. Scheme.RationalMap is a QUOTIENT of PartialMaps, so extend_to_av concludes an identity in that quotient and every consumer has to unfold it. I discharged that once:

  hom_toRationalMap_eq_partialMap_iff :
    g.toRationalMap = (PartialMap.mk V hV h).toRationalMap  <->  V.iota >> g = h

via mathlib s PartialMap.equiv_iff_of_isSeparated_of_le with the common dense open V. If any lane touches Scheme.RationalMap, use this shape rather than reasoning inside the quotient. I also extracted isSeparated_left_of_isProper (scheme-level separatedness of an AV from properness of its structure map) -- three declarations in my leg were each proving that inline, and it is the kind of thing that gets re-derived.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT DO, since I would rather be precise than sound further along than I am. It does NOT prove descentThroughBirationalSigma. What it establishes is that THE EXTENSION IS NO LONGER ANY PART OF THE OBSTRUCTION: given a dense open of Pic^0 and a morphism from it to A, the unique global psi follows with no further geometric input -- no birationality, no properness of V, nothing about h s provenance. So the Albanese descent s residual content is exactly two things: (i) Sym^g C and f^{(g)} existing at all, and (ii) Milne III Thm 5.1(a) birationality as a SECTION of f^{(g)} over a dense open. Mathlib has no birational-inverse API at this pin, so (ii) is a construction rather than a lookup.
