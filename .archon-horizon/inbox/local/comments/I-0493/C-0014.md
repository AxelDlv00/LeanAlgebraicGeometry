1. TANGENT (a). Clause (i) of the geometric middle is CLOSED, sorry-free and axiom-clean.
An invertible module over a ring with a NILPOTENT ideal, cyclic modulo the ideal, is free
(Picard/NilpotentThickeningFree.lean); specialised to the dual numbers, an invertible
A[eps]-module trivial along eps -> 0 is trivial (Picard/DualNumberChartTriviality.lean).
Two things worth stealing:
 * For INVERTIBLE modules a surjection from the ring is automatically bijective
   (mathlib Module.Invertible.bijective_of_surjective), so 'generated by one element'
   gives FREE outright. False for general modules; it is what makes the argument short.
 * I did NOT use mathlib's Nakayama (Submodule.le_of_le_smul_of_le_jacobson_bot): it needs
   N.FG and I <= jacobson bot. For a nilpotent I the iteration N + I^n.M = M terminates on
   its own, three lines, NO finiteness. That matters here specifically: affine charts of a
   curve do not have finite-dimensional section spaces over k -- the same binder
   ChiUnconditional.ell_le_finrank_chart_along_tower calls 'a substantive geometric
   restriction' -- so a finitely-generated version would have been unusable.
Clause (ii) turned out to be ALREADY LANDED (DualNumber.baseChangeAlgEquiv). Residue of
the tangent lane is clause (iii) alone, the transition-unit identification.

2. PROPERNESS (b). Pic0.universallyClosed_of_ambient: UniversallyClosed (PicScheme C).hom
implies UniversallyClosed (Pic0Scheme C).hom, because Pic^0 -> Pic is a CLOSED immersion
(clopen-ness is load-bearing -- an open immersion is not universally closed). With
proper_of_universallyClosed this gives proper_of_ambient_universallyClosed: Pic^0 is proper
as soon as PIC is universally closed. The identity component is now out of the problem,
which matters because Kleiman 5 th:qpp&p is a theorem about Pic, not about Pic^0 -- the old
reduction would have forced a re-descent to the component.
