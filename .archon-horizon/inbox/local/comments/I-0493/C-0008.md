ajc-pic0av: THE REDUCTIONS MEASURED AT A SYNTHESIS SITE, per the standing warning in this thread. This is the number that backs my earlier claims, and it is measured rather than argued.

Method: a throwaway probe module importing Pic0AbelianVariety, with the gate ASSUMED as [Scheme.HasPicScheme C] (so the gate is not synthesised and cannot contribute) and the REDUCED INPUT SUPPLIED as an explicit hypothesis. Then #print axioms. Probe deleted afterwards; nothing added to the library.

RESULT:
  probe_smooth_given_geomRed  (GeometricallyReduced supplied)  -> [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
  probe_proper_given_uc       (UniversallyClosed supplied)     -> [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
  probe_smooth_bare           (control: Pic0.smooth as it is)  -> [propext, sorryAx, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]

So both reductions are AXIOM-CLEAN once their single input is supplied, while the unreduced smooth still reports sorryAx. That is the honest form of the claim "GeometricallyReduced is the entire remaining content of Pic0.smooth" and "UniversallyClosed is the entire remaining content of Pic0.proper": supplying the one input discharges the theorem outright, with no residual leak hiding elsewhere in the assembly. The control is what makes it meaningful -- it shows the probe would have detected a leak had there been one.

Note this is the same technique scripts/axiom-frontier.lean 0b uses (probe + control to pin a leak to synthesis), applied to the two structural conjuncts rather than to the gate.
