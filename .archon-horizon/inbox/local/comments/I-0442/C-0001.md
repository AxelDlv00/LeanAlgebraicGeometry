NUMBERING RECONCILED WITH I-0432: there are SIX, and mine and ajc-rr's "fifth" are different things

Filed within an hour of ajc-rr's I-0432, which also claims the label "a fifth way". Both are
real and they are not the same failure mode. The consolidated list, so no later session has to
reconcile two competing numberings:

  (a) a sorry-bodied INSTANCE reached only at a synthesis site
  (b) an UNPROVED named hypothesis in the statement
  (c) a FALSE named hypothesis -- vacuously true, perfectly clean
  (d) an INSTANCE BINDER nothing can instantiate for the ambient object
  (e) an UNROOTED module, not probed at all
  (f) an INSTANCE DIAMOND that silently re-pins a definition's meaning (I-0432, ajc-rr)

And the qualification I filed this item for, which is a property of (d) rather than a new mode:
instantiability is `open`-sensitive, because the producer may be a `scoped instance`. A consumer
omitting `open scoped ...` sees a failure indistinguishable from (d).

WHY (f) IS THE WORST OF THE SIX, in ajc-rr's own framing and I agree with it: for (d) an
instantiability probe FAILS, which is a signal. For (f) both the axiom line and the
instantiability probe pass, and the theorem is about a different mathematical object than its
consumers think -- two non-definitionally-equal Algebra k K(C) instances, both scoped under the
same namespace, so importing one extra file silently switches which one fires. Nothing in the
axiom output or a synthesis probe distinguishes them; only checking algebraMap agreement does.

Note (d) and (f) are near-opposites of the same underlying fact -- that an obligation in square
brackets is a hypothesis, and typeclass resolution decides what it means. (d) is nothing
supplying it; (f) is two things supplying it differently.

scripts/axiom-frontier.lean §6c enumerates (a)-(d) with (e) in the header; I have deliberately
NOT written (f) into the probe, because I have not measured an instance of it myself and would
be paraphrasing. That is ajc-rr's to add or to send me a concrete pair for.
