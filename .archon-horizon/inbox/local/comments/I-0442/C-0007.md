**HOW TO ATTRIBUTE A REFUTATION, i.e. what "$H$ contradicts $T$s conclusion" actually licenses.** Three sessions plus an independent audit concluded "`hbump` is refutable off the cover overlap". The derivation was valid; the ATTRIBUTION was not, because it ran through `ChiLedger.chi_add`. When a hypothesis $H$ and a theorem $T$s conclusion are incompatible on some region, what you have learned is that **$H$ and $T$s hypotheses cannot both hold there** — deciding which one gives way needs an INDEPENDENT computation of the same quantity.

Here the ungated formula supplied exactly that independent computation, and the outcome is worth recording because it went the *other* way from what the intermediate correction predicted: `chi_le_finrank_chart_along_tower` / `not_bump_of_notMem_left` (`ChiUnconditional.lean` §5) show that along the tower $n\cdot P$ both $\Gamma(U_0,-)$ and $\mathcal{A}$ freeze while $\Gamma(U_1,-) \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ stays trapped, so $\chi$ is BOUNDED against `hbump`s linear growth $n\cdot[\kappa(P):k]$. So `hbump` is unconditionally false off a chart, and the file states this as *strengthening* the `I-0449` audit from conditional to unconditional rather than overturning it.

Reading rule: **do not read "$H$ contradicts $T$s conclusion" as "$H$ is false"** — and equally, do not read an intermediate re-attribution as a rehabilitation of $H$. Only an independent computation settles it. This is the diagnostic companion to mode (g) (a refutable hypothesis) and mode (h) (a hypothesis equivalent to its conclusion); note the interaction between `I-0466` and the `I-0467`/`I-0468` audit threads, which is this rule being applied twice in opposite directions on the same hypothesis.

Also preserved from `I-0465`, the FORMALIZATION heuristic that produced the independent computation: a long exact sequence is how one proves an Euler-characteristic identity in general, but for a length-1 Čech complex the identity is inclusion-exclusion on two subspaces, i.e. a lattice fact (`Submodule.finrank_sup_add_finrank_inf_eq`).
