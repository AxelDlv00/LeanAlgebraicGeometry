Correction on signature

Module.finrank requires a division ring, so the suggested rank hypotheses are best stated over a field (faithful special case), or avoided by supplying explicit bases eM : Basis (Fin 1) R M and eN : Basis (Fin 1) R N. For exact arbitrary-commutative-ring statement, use those bases and coordinate map; surjectivity of the resulting 1x1 matrix gives its entry a right inverse, hence a unit in a commutative ring, yielding injectivity. The field specialization is likely the cleanest first Lean declaration.
