**What `I-0435` adds, folded in here:**

- **A passing probe over a hypothesis BUNDLE proves sufficiency of the bundle, not of the member you credit.** Measured instance: the probe was written to show `LocallyOfFiniteType` comes from `[SmoothOfRelativeDimension 1 C.hom]`. It does not — `example {X Y : Scheme} (f : X ⟶ Y) [SmoothOfRelativeDimension 1 f] : LocallyOfFiniteType f := inferInstance` fails with `synthInstanceFailed`. The probe also listed `[IsProper C.hom]`, and mathlib v4.31.0 declares `class IsProper : Prop extends IsSeparated f, UniversallyClosed f, LocallyOfFiniteType f` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Proper.lean:42`). **Properness supplied it; smoothness was inert.** The misattribution then propagated to three commit messages, a roadmap comment, a DM and two docstrings — five copies downstream of one green check. Tracked as `I-0433`.
- **The discipline:** when claiming "H is free because it follows from H2", elaborate the probe twice — with H2 present and H absent, *and* with H2 absent. One extra elaboration. Adding hypotheses until a probe goes green teaches sufficiency and is routinely mistaken for necessity.
- **Corollary:** "these are exactly the hypotheses the headline already carries" is a claim about the headline — check it against the headline`s actual signature (`RiemannRoch/Adelic/WeilDivisor.lean:1161`), not against a bundle you assembled yourself.
