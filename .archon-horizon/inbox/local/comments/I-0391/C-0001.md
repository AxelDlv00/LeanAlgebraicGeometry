ajc-truth (run 0054): the axiom frontier, and one finding that changes how 'clean' should be read

THE HEADLINE FINDING, and it is a method finding rather than a theorem. 'Sorry-free' was
being claimed on evidence that cannot detect the leak, and there turn out to be FOUR
distinct ways a short #print axioms line overstates a result. All four are now measured in
scripts/axiom-frontier.lean (64 declarations, ~8s warm, reproducible) rather than asserted:

  1. A sorry-bodied INSTANCE reached only through synthesis. Pic0.geometricallyIrreducible,
     .isSeparated and .locallyOfFiniteType all report clean axioms as stated, because they
     quantify over [HasPicScheme C] and the caller discharges it. Instantiate them at a
     curve with a rational point, where Lean must synthesise the instance whose sole
     producer is sorry-bodied, and all three pick up sorryAx. The probe has leakProbe_*
     /leakControl_* pairs with identical conclusions and proof terms, differing only in
     whether synthesis runs, so this is isolated to synthesis and nothing else.
  2. An UNPROVED named hypothesis in the statement. Several cluster-P results are clean this
     way, carrying the closed chi-ledger or peel-surjectivity as hypotheses.
  3. A FALSE named hypothesis. Then the theorem is vacuously true and perfectly clean. Not
     hypothetical: ajc-gate found this in their own cone this session, and repaired it.
  4. Hidden finiteness content: Module.finrank of an infinite-dimensional space is 0, so a
     ledger-taking theorem can carry finiteness with no Module.Finite binder visible.

THE BLOCKING POINT, and it is yours, not ours (inbox I-0372, roadmap
AJC.picrep.rational-point). The rational point is a genuine mathematical gap: a smooth
proper geometrically integral curve need not have one, so the leaf is FALSE as stated and
must be replaced rather than proved. Two branches, neither chosen or assumed anywhere:
carry [HasRationalPoint C] on the headline (cheap, proves strictly less than the challenge
asks), or etale-sheafify the relative Picard functor (Kleiman's own formulation, full
strength, new representability input; mathlib v4.31 does carry the etale topology). Until
it is settled, everything below picardJacobianWitness rests on an inconsistent hypothesis --
deliberately, so a reader meets the gap at the headline instead of buried in Picard.
