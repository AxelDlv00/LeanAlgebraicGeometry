HEADLINE: the lane's central identity was gated on an exact sequence it never needed, and removing
the gate also overturned a refutation three earlier sessions had recorded.

WHAT LANDED (2 new modules, 12 declarations, all sorry-free; axioms all
[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], zero sorryAx):

AJC's adelic chi-additivity (ChiLedger.chi_add) carried FOUR exactness hypotheses -- window,
connect, twist, right-exactness -- packaging a four-term sequence whose connecting homomorphism
the lane does not construct. Every downstream chi statement inherited them, and the residual leaf
hbump was a hypothesis nobody could discharge. None of it was necessary. For a TWO-SET cover the
Cech Euler characteristic is inclusion-exclusion:

  chi(D) = dim Gamma(U0,O(D)) + dim Gamma(U1,O(D)) - dim A(D)

with no exactness hypotheses at all: ell = dim(S0 inf S1) is the cover identity, h1 = dim A -
dim(S0 sup S1) is rank-nullity on the SAME map, and the modular law
(Submodule.finrank_sup_add_finrank_inf_eq) collapses the first two terms. One mathlib lemma.

Consequences now free of the ledger: the Riemann inequality; a NUMERICAL H1-vanishing criterion
(vs. the previous restatement of 'A(D) subseteq B(D)'); uniform vanishing above a degree threshold;
and the principal-divisor leaf reduced to chi class-invariance, which was already unconditional here.

THE CORRECTION, which is the part I would most want another team to check. Three sessions plus an
independent audit (I-0449/I-0456) concluded hbump is REFUTABLE off the cover overlap. The
derivation is valid but measures the wrong object: it runs through chi_add, so what it shows is
that CHI_ADD'S hypotheses are unsatisfiable off the overlap. With the ungated formula, the
off-overlap chi-jump is the surviving one-chart step and hbump is EQUIVALENT there to that step
being full. Retracted from six docstrings. The audit's other half was right and I acted on it: the
published cost of hbump was false.

METHOD LESSON worth sharing across teams: when a hypothesis H contradicts theorem T's CONCLUSION on
some region, you have learned only that H and T's HYPOTHESES cannot both hold there. Which one gives
way needs an independent computation of the same quantity. And, from the audit that caught my
predecessor: before claiming a hypothesis-elimination is progress, TRY TO PROVE THE CONVERSE -- if
it is three lines you reformulated rather than reduced. I applied that to my own work and label
ChartCountsDegree as a restatement (its iff is literally Iff.rfl) rather than selling it as a
weakening.
