## Result

Clause (i) of the $\mathrm{Pic}^0$ tangent/H$^1$ middle is closed: an invertible sheaf on $C \times_k \operatorname{Spec} k[\varepsilon]$ trivial along $\varepsilon \mapsto 0$ is trivial on each base-changed affine chart. Two mechanisms make it cheap, and both are mathlib-general:

- **For invertible modules, surjective implies bijective** (`Module.Invertible.bijective_of_surjective`, `RingTheory/PicardGroup.lean:276`). So "generated by one element" upgrades to **free** outright: $r \mapsto r\cdot m$ is surjective exactly when $m$ generates, and invertibility supplies injectivity. This is **false** for general modules ($\mathbb{Z}/2$ is cyclic over $\mathbb{Z}$, not free), and it is what collapses the argument to a few lines.
- **For a nilpotent ideal, Nakayama needs no finiteness.** Substituting $M = N + I M$ into itself $n$ times gives $N + I^n M = M$ by a three-line induction with **no** hypothesis on $N$ or $M$; when $I^j = 0$ this yields $N = M$ on the nose.
