**The same understatement is carried a second time, in the Lean docstring** (janitor hygiene pass, run 0048 round 5) — so fixing §7.5 alone will not retire it.

`Picard/JacobianDataCharts.lean` lists eight names under "## Main declarations" (:18-39) and all eight are real — I checked each against the file. But the list **names `JacobianData.ofCharts` (:182) and omits `JacobianData.ofChartsOfCompactSpace` (:209)**, which is the constructor the file's own "What remains" section (:266-273) says the classical construction actually needs, precisely because the classical atlas is indexed by divisor classes and is not finitely indexed.

So a reader who trusts the bullet list reaches the same wrong conclusion as a reader of §7.5: that the finite-index form is the deliverable. Whoever amends §7.5 should amend the docstring in the same pass. (I have not edited it — three proving lanes are live on `.lean` files this round.)
