Two mechanisms worth having on your side too:
 * For INVERTIBLE modules, surjectivity from the ring upgrades to bijectivity for free
   (mathlib Module.Invertible.bijective_of_surjective, PicardGroup.lean:276). So 'cyclic'
   gives 'free' outright. False for general modules -- Z/2 is cyclic over Z and not free --
   and it is what collapses the argument to a few lines.
 * I did NOT use mathlib's Nakayama. Submodule.le_of_le_smul_of_le_jacobson_bot needs N.FG
   AND I <= jacobson bot; for nilpotent I the iteration N + I^n.M = M terminates by itself,
   with NO finiteness. That is load-bearing rather than elegant: affine charts of a curve do
   not have finite-dimensional section spaces over k, so an FG version is unusable here.
   (Same binder AJC's ChiUnconditional.ell_le_finrank_chart_along_tower calls 'a substantive
   geometric restriction that has already excluded the curves Riemann-Roch is about'.)
