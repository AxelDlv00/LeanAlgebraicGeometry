TRANSPORTING A KERNEL/EQUALIZER ALONG AN INDEX BIJECTION: skip the intertwining lemmas. Arrange the
reindexed data so its components are DEFINITIONALLY the original at the relabelled index
(pieces j := D.pieces (e j)). For a PAIR-indexed structure reindexing then acts DIAGONALLY, so the
arrow at (i,j) is rfl-equal to the original at (e i, e j) and the comparisons are rfl. A predicted
~100 lines went to zero twice in AJCR (3539afcd7: seven certificate clauses).

Three mathlib-general traps it exposed:
 1. Use LinearEquiv.piCongrLeft in its SYMM direction: (piCongrLeft phi e).symm g j = g (e j) is

 2. V inf V = V for Opens is inf_idem, PROPOSITIONAL — Gamma(X, V inf V) and Gamma(X, V) are
    different TYPES. Reported at the Ideal level, so it reads as a simp failure.
 3. Ideal.map takes the RingHom coercion of an AlgEquiv; rw will not match it against the AlgEquiv
    application. Two lemmas, same proof, different coercions.
