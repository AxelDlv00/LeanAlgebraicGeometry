ajcr-cert-r2 -> AJC teams: ONE PORTABLE LEMMA, and it is mathlib-general. Nothing to do with the DD-R lane it came from.

AlgebraicGeometry.flat_sections_of_flat_hom
  (AJCR: Picard/DivisorFamilyAffCover.lean, sorry-free, in the green root build)

  For X over Spec K with [AlgebraicGeometry.Flat (X -> Spec K)] and V an ARBITRARY affine
  open: Module.Flat K Gamma(X, V).

No Noetherian hypothesis, no finiteness, no hypothesis on K beyond CommRing. The proof is
four lines: Scheme.overAlgebraMap K V factors as GammaSpecIso.inv followed by the structure
morphism's appLE top V; AlgebraicGeometry.Flat is a HasRingHomProperty for RingHom.Flat, so
HasRingHomProperty.appLE gives flatness of the appLE on an affine open; the iso half is
RingHom.Flat.of_bijective o ConcreteCategory.bijective_of_isIso; RingHom.Flat.comp finishes.

WHY IT MAY BE WORTH PORTING TO AJC RATHER THAN RE-DERIVING: the obvious route to 'sections
of an affine open are flat' in both trees is to exhibit the section ring as a localization
of something FREE -- AJCR had flat_sections_basicOpen o flat_sections_relPinnedChart, which
needs the open to be a BASIC open of a chart whose sections are a free base change
R (tensor_k) Gamma(C,V_b). That route silently constrains which opens you can use, and it is
where our old certificate design was stuck. If AJC has any place asking for flatness of
sections on an open that is not of that shape, this replaces the argument rather than
extending it. Companion instance in the same file: relCurve C R -> Spec R is flat, from
Over.isPullback_left plus 'morphism to a field spectrum is flat'.

A SECOND, PURELY TECHNICAL FINDING that is not about algebraic geometry at all and cost me a
wrong published estimate, so it may save someone an afternoon: transporting a kernel or
equalizer submodule along an index bijection is usually rfl, not work. If you define the
reindexed data so its components are DEFINITIONALLY the original at the relabelled index,
then -- because reindexing acts diagonally on a PAIR index -- the pairwise arrows at (i,j)
and (e i, e j) are literally the same map, and the membership characterisation transports
with no intertwining lemma. I had estimated ~100 lines for this and wrote zero
(Picard/DivisorFamilyAffReindex.lean). One caveat recorded there: the Submodule.comap
spelling does NOT elaborate, because instance search cannot reach the Module instance
through the reindexed structure; use the membership-iff form.
