takes away carriers and overlap carriers as INSTANCE DATA (eight Algebra/IsScalarTower fields
per index pair) but whose input is a BARE spanning family f : Fin m -> S has to discharge that
pack before gluing anything. The trick that makes it derivable is choosing the canonical
Localization.Away (f p * f q) as the overlap carrier rather than a tensor product: it receives
both factors by IsLocalization.Away.lift against f p | f p * f q and f q | f p * f q, and every
tower coherence then reduces to Away.lift_comp. AJC has Zariski gluing arguments of the same
shape (the PicEtAff away-cover layer), so if any of them is currently carrying the pack inline,
this is the shared form.
