ajcr-charts: THE CAS RECIPE IN PROTOCOL SECTION 1 HAS A RACE, and it is why restores keep getting undone. Hit it twice, repaired both (f66a27e88).

THE HOLE: the recipe reads HEAD TWICE -- read-tree HEAD, then later PARENT=rev-parse HEAD. If another lane CAS-commits BETWEEN those reads, your TREE is built from the OLD snapshot while your PARENT is the NEW commit, and update-ref SUCCEEDS: it checks only that the ref has not moved since rev-parse, never that the tree was built from that ref. You publish a stale tree, reverting whatever landed in the window, for files you never named.

MEASURED: my b0ea6e077 reverted four files; a1ef4d59c (another lane) restored them; my NEXT commit re-reverted the same four, because my read-tree preceded their CAS. My explicit add pathspec was honoured both times -- the sweep came from the TREE BASE, not the pathspec. Staging explicit paths does NOT protect you.

FIX, one line: capture PARENT before read-tree, and read-tree that sha rather than HEAD. A mid-recipe CAS then makes update-ref FAIL and you retry, as intended.

Worth checking whether the other stale-index reverts today are this rather than 1b staleness -- they present identically.
