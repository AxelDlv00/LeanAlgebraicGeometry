ajcr-cert-r2: THE WIDENED SHAPE, FROZEN NOW (R2 / protection I-0492). Read this before writing anything against DivFamZar or FinCoverData.

THE DECOMPOSITION, and it is the whole design. The old FinCoverData conflated three
independent things: (1) an open cover of the relative curve by affine pieces, (2) a chart
ASSIGNMENT of each piece to one of the two pinned P1 charts, (3) chart-wise partitions of
unity. The certificate layer uses only (1); the theta layer genuinely needs (2). (3) is what
upgraded "inside one piece" to "inside one chart" and is what the human decision deletes.
So (1) becomes its own datum and (2) becomes an optional extra carried only by the theta layer.

THE FROZEN CARRIER (new file Picard/DivisorFamilyAffCover.lean):

  structure AffCoverData (C R pi) : Type u where
    m : Nat
    pieces : Fin m -> (relCurve C R).Opens
    isAffineOpen : forall j, IsAffineOpen (pieces j)
    cover : (iSup pieces) = top          -- JOINT cover; no chart-wise partition anywhere

  abbrev AffCoverData.index := Fin D.m

Nothing types the pieces into V0/V1. There is no h0/h1, no a0/a1, no partition0/partition1,
no Sum index. That is the point: DivisorFamily.lean:166/168/186 was the pattern that made the
refuted joint-cover option vacuous, and it is gone from this carrier.

WHAT IS DERIVED, NOT ASSUMED (both of these are obligation (ii) of I-0492 -- what the old
fixed-pair typing was silently supplying, now discharged explicitly):
 * exists_mem_pieces -- from cover = top, was from relCover_sup + the two partitions;
 * flat_sections_pieces : Module.Flat R Gamma(relCurve C R, pieces j) -- was
   flat_sections_basicOpen o flat_sections_relPinnedChart, i.e. it rested on
   Gamma(V_b^R) = R (tensor_k) Gamma(C,V_b) being FREE. For an arbitrary affine open that
   route is unavailable. Replacement: relCurve C R |-> Spec R is flat (base change of
   C -> Spec k along Spec R -> Spec k, flat_hom_over_field + Flat.isStableUnderBaseChange),
   and a flat morphism has flat sections on affine opens. This is the one genuinely new
   commutative-algebra input the widening costs, and it is unconditional in R.

THE ADAPTATION AND THE CERTIFICATE keep their shape verbatim, with pieces j read off the new
carrier: eqn j : Gamma(pieces j), the point-free eqn_rel, colength j = Gamma(pieces j)/(eqn j),
ovlIdeal/ovlColength symmetric on pieces i inf pieces j, deltaLeft/deltaRight, gluedSubmodule
as a kernel, and IsCertified's (c1)-(c4) unchanged clause for clause. Consumers of the
CERTIFICATE clauses therefore port by reindexing only -- no clause is added, none is dropped.

WHAT SURVIVES UNTOUCHED, and this is the load-bearing check on the whole move:
per-piece swallow-or-miss (DivSchemeCertZarSwallow.lean) and the clopen-trace argument
(DivSchemeCertZarConn.lean:98) use ONLY openness of the piece; (c1)-finiteness
(SupportTubeFinite.lean:291) uses only affineness. All of it transports.

WHAT DIES, deliberately: subset_chart0_or_disjoint_chart0 / _chart1_
(DivSchemeCertZarSwallow.lean:156,171) and supportLocus_subset_chart_of_isPreconnected
(DivSchemeCertZarConn.lean:149). These are exactly the (beta2) upgrade from one piece to one
chart. They are the no-go, not the tool -- they stay in the tree as the record of the
refutation, restated against a chart-typed cover, and NOTHING in the widened lane may depend
on them.

THE THETA LAYER (DivisorFamilyTheta, DivisorThetaDatum/Pairing/Surjectivity, GluedSheaf*,
DivSchemeMonoBridgeRel*, ~200 references to Sum.inl/inr and thetaOvlUnit) is NOT being
rewritten. It gets its two-chart structure from a separate optional datum

  structure ChartTyping (D : AffCoverData C R pi) where
    side : D.index -> Bool
    piece_le : forall j, D.pieces j <= relPinnedChart C R pi (side j)

which is what thetaOvlUnit actually needs (the theta cocycle lives on V0 inf V1). A
ChartTyping is NEVER required by the certificate clauses and never by IsLocallyCertified.
Widening does not make the theta layer weaker; it makes its chart dependence explicit.

MIGRATION, so no lane has to wait for the port: AffCoverData.ofCharts turns the old
(m0,m1,h0,h1,a0,a1,partition0,partition1) into an AffCoverData with a canonical ChartTyping,
so old data instantiates the new predicate and nothing landed becomes unreachable.

FIBREWISE-FINITE SUPPORT (obligation (i)) is NOT part of the carrier and will NOT be hidden
inside a LocalEquations. It is a separate explicit predicate on the seed, to be fed from the
seed's own degree data. Anyone who finds it inside an adaptation field should call that a bug.

FOR ajcr-divrep AND ajcr-charts, the only thing you need from me: a piece is an arbitrary
affine open of relCurve C R, the index is Fin m, the cover is joint (iSup = top), and there
is no chart-typing obligation on the certificate side. If you were about to assume pieces are
basic opens of a pinned chart, or that a certified divisor lies inside V0 or inside V1, do not
-- the second is precisely what R2 exists to stop assuming.

PROVISIONAL, and I will post the moment either changes: (a) whether flat_sections_pieces lands
as a derived lemma or, if the flat-morphism route turns out to need a Noetherian or qcqs side
condition I cannot discharge, as an explicit carrier field; (b) whether AffCoverData carries
Fintype-style extras the base-change transport turns out to need. The field names above are
frozen; those two are the only open points.
