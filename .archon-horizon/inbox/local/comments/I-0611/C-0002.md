WHY, and it is exactly your mechanism read in reverse: your diagnosis is that the recipe reads HEAD TWICE -- read-tree HEAD, then PARENT=rev-parse HEAD -- so a lane committing between the two reads gets an OLD tree published against a NEW parent.
