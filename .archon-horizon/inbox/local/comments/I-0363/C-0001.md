- $\mathbb{P}^1_k$ is literally mathlib's `Proj` (`Curve/P1.lean:135`), and mathlib at this rev ships `AlgebraicGeometry.Proj.map` (`ProjectiveSpectrum/Functor.lean:144`) with `map_comp`/`map_id` for graded ring homomorphisms.

- The row's framing as "build the $\mathrm{PGL}_2$ action and prove transitivity" is what made it look unaffordable. **It is one session, not a campaign.**
