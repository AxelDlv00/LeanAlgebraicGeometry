Cluster-P substrate landed in three new modules under AlgebraicJacobian/RiemannRoch/Adelic/, all sorry-free, all kernel-checked (lake build, 8571 jobs), every keystone #print axioms = [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]. Commits c5e72b89b, e56633817, beb6c583a, 7053bdd51, 1f2d5a1fe.

PROVENANCE, theorem by theorem, as the task demands. Nothing was mechanically ported. AJCR's RiemannRoch development is built on CurveDivisor / divisorSheaf / Sheaf.chi / CechPic, none of which exist as names in AJC; its sorry-free 17.8k lines are therefore not importable or transcribable here. What I reused is ARGUMENTS:
  * chi-transport along multiplication by g: argument adapted from AJCR mulEquivDivisorSheaf + chi_divisorSheaf_eq_of_picClass_eq / deg_divOf. Rederived in the adelic function-field abstractions, where it becomes elementary subspace linear algebra and needs NO finiteness input -- strictly cheaper than the sheaf-theoretic form, which needs chi-additivity even to state the transport.
  * bounded-vanishing skeleton (fix a base twist, effective witness of the residual class, peel, transport along the class): same skeleton as AJCR exists_bound_subsingleton_hModule_one_of_isFinite_toP1. NOT a port: AJCR gets its base vanishing from the FLV-class machine via a finite dominant pi : Y -> P1 and the fiber divisor, and its peel from peel_effective on divisorSheaf. AJC has neither, so those two become named hypotheses here and the file says so.
  * effective-witness dictionary: AJCR's exists_effective_of_picClass phrased on classes; here proved from chi <= ell plus the order arithmetic.
  * residue-degree positivity: AJCR has CurveDivisor.deg_pos_of_nonneg_of_ne_zero; the positivity input here is derived from this project's own orderGe filtration (1 is in ord>=0 but not ord>=1), not from a residue-field API.
  * REUSED AJC CODE, not reproved: localStep_finrank_le (node N14), finrank_localStepDom, chi_le_ell, riemann_inequality, exists_order_eq, the pointDivisor calculus, IsConstantField and its GateInstances discharge.

THE THREE GAPS, kept precisely apart in every docstring:
  (i) single-field bounded vanishing -- ASSEMBLED, conditional on base vanishing + peel-surjectivity + closed ledger. None is a gate class; both vanishing hypotheses verified load-bearing (a first draft's hpeel silently subsumed hbase, corrected before commit).
  (ii) extension-uniformity -- NOT proved, and not merely unproved: even stating it needs the 2-affine cover transported to C_kappa, which CurveBaseChange.lean does not supply. The invariants here are pinned on a chosen cover.
  (iii) global generation -- NOT proved, not addressed at all; needs H1(D-x) = 0 at every closed point plus evaluation-surjectivity.

No new sorry, axiom, admit or native_decide. The single pre-existing sorry, WeilDivisor.lean:1161 principal_degree_zero, is REDUCED (see the AJC.rr.principal roadmap comment) but deliberately not substituted.
