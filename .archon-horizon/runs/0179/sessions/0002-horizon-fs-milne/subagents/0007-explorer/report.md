Findings for `/root`:

Mathlib has no native `EffectiveCartierDivisor`, `RelativeEffectiveDivisor`, Cartier divisor, or “sum of Cartier divisors” API. Relevant available primitives are:

- `AlgebraicGeometry.Flat (f : X ⟶ Y)` in [`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Flat.lean`](/home/axel/LeanAlgebraicGeometry-Horizon/.lake-packages/mathlib/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Flat.lean:42), with `Flat.iff_flat_stalkMap`, `Flat.stalkMap`, and base-change/composition instances.
- `Scheme.IdealSheafData`, `IdealSheafData.subscheme`, and `IdealSheafData.subschemeι` in Mathlib’s `AlgebraicGeometry/IdealSheaf/Subscheme.lean`.
- No Mathlib predicate saying an ideal sheaf is locally principal/generated by a non-zero-divisor.

The closest existing encoding is AJC’s `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.DivFamily` in [`Picard/DivFunctorDef.lean`](/home/axel/LeanAlgebraicGeometry-Horizon/MainProjects/Algebraic-Jacobian-Challenge/AlgebraicJacobian/Picard/DivFunctorDef.lean:768). For `π : X ⟶ S`, `T : Over S`, it packages a divisor on `X ×_S T` as:

```lean
structure DivFamily (π : X ⟶ S) (T : Over S) where
  F : (pullback π T.hom).Modules
  isFinitePresentation : F.IsFinitePresentation
  flat : CoherentSheafFlat (pullback.snd π T.hom) F
  properSupport : Modules.HasProperSupport (pullback.snd π T.hom) F
  q : pullback-unit ⟶ F
  epi : Epi q
  kerLocallyTrivial : LineBundle.IsLocallyTrivial (kernel q)
```

Here `flat` is the project-local affine-local predicate `Scheme.CoherentSheafFlat`, defined in [`Picard/FlatteningStratification.lean`](/home/axel/LeanAlgebraicGeometry-Horizon/MainProjects/Algebraic-Jacobian-Challenge/AlgebraicJacobian/Picard/FlatteningStratification.lean:1431), not Mathlib’s morphism `Flat`. `HasProperSupport` and `schematicSupportι` are project-local in [`Picard/QuotScheme.lean`](/home/axel/LeanAlgebraicGeometry-Horizon/MainProjects/Algebraic-Jacobian-Challenge/AlgebraicJacobian/Picard/QuotScheme.lean:545).

Suggested Milne implementation:

1. Reuse the quotient encoding if the subsequent Jacobian formalization needs families over arbitrary `T`: a relative effective divisor is a quotient `q : O_{X_T} ⟶ F`, with `Epi q`, `CoherentSheafFlat (pr₂) F`, and `LineBundle.IsLocallyTrivial (kernel q)`. Proper-support/finitely-presented fields are optional for Milne’s stated definition.
2. If only a local placeholder is needed, define a predicate over an ideal sheaf/closed immersion requiring “locally principal generated by a non-zero-divisor” plus flatness of the associated closed subscheme. This will require introducing the missing Cartier predicate locally; Mathlib cannot supply it directly.
3. The sum lemma is currently not directly feasible from existing APIs. It needs a construction of the product/local-equation ideal (or an equivalent quotient/tensor construction) and a proof that the resulting quotient is base-flat. AJC’s `DivFamily` has no general addition operation; its existing `DivFamily.zero` is only the zero-family special case.

The blueprint statements are exactly at [`ch03-jacobian-varieties.tex`](/home/axel/LeanAlgebraicGeometry-Horizon/FormalizedSources/AbelianVarieties/Milne/blueprint/src/ch03-jacobian-varieties.tex:159): definition, and line 162: sum lemma.
