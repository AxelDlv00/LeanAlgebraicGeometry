Let \(T\) be an object of \(\Over(\Spec k)\) and \(V \subseteq T\) an affine open. Then
  pullback along the second projection is a bijection
  \(\Gamma(T, V)^{\times} \to \Gamma(C \otimes T, \mathrm{pr}^{-1}V)^{\times}\) of unit
  groups.
