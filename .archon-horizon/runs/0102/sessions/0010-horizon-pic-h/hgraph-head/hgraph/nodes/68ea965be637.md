For any open \(W \ni x_t\), the kernel of \(\varepsilon_t\) is a maximal ideal and
  \[
    \dim_K \bigl(\Gamma(C_K, W) \,/\, \ker\varepsilon_t\bigr) \;=\; 1 .
  \]
