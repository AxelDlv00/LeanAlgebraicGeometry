\(\mathrm{tCl}(b + c) = \mathrm{tCl}(b) + \mathrm{tCl}(c)\);
  \(\mathrm{tCl}(b) = 0\) iff \(b\) is an additive coboundary
  \(\rho_1(a_1) + \rho_2(a_2)\) of the two charts; and every element of \(\ker \pi\) is
  \(\mathrm{tCl}(b)\) for some overlap section \(b\).
