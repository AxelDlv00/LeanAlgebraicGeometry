For every \(z \in A_{(X_1)}\) one has \(\theta(\rho(z)) = q(T^{-1})\), where
  \(q = \alpha_1(z) \in k[t]\). In other words, under the identifications \(\alpha_1\) and
  \(\theta\), the restriction \(\rho\) is the homomorphism \(k[t] \to k[T, T^{-1}]\),
  \(t \mapsto T^{-1}\).
