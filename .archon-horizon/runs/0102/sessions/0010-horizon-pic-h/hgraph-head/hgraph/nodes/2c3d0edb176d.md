Let \((R, \mathfrak m)\) be a Noetherian local ring and \(M\) a nonzero finite
  \(R\)-module of finite projective dimension \(\mathrm{pd}_R(M) = n \in \mathbb{N}\).
  Then
  \[
    \mathrm{pd}_R(M) + \mathrm{depth}(M) = \mathrm{depth}(R).
  \]
