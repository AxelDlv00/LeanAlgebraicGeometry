For every Weil divisor \(D\) on the challenge curve \(C\),
  \[
    \chi(\mathcal O(D)) = 1 - g(C) + \deg_{\Pic}\struct X(D) .
  \]
  The standing finiteness hypotheses are discharged on the curve, so there is no side condition.
