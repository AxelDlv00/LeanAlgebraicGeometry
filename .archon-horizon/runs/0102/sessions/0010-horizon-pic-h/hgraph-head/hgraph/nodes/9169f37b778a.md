For a commutative ring \(R\) and a global section \(g \in \Gamma(\Spec R, \top)\), the
  section ring on the basic open \(D(g)\) is the localization of \(R\) itself away from the
  element \(\Gamma\Spec\text{-}\mathrm{iso}(g) \in R\).
