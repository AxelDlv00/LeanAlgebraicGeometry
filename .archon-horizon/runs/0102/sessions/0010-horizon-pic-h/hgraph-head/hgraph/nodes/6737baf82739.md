Let \(I\) be a nonzero principal ideal of \(B\) and \(n \ge 0\), with \(B/I^{n+1}\) and
  \(B/I^n\) finite-dimensional over \(k\). Then
  \[
    \dim_k\bigl(B/I^{n+1}\bigr) \;=\; \dim_k(B/I) + \dim_k\bigl(B/I^n\bigr) .
  \]
