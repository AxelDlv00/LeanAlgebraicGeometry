On an \emph{affine} open \(W \ni x_t\), the kernel of the evaluation at the graph section
  is the prime ideal of the graph point:
  \[
    \ker \varepsilon_t \;=\; \mathfrak p_{x_t} \subseteq \Gamma(C_K, W).
  \]
