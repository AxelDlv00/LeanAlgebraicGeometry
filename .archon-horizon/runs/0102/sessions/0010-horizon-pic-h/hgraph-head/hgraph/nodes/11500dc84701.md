The degree-zero cohomology of the pair \(P\) is the kernel of the difference map,
  \[
    H^0(P) \;=\; \ker \delta_P
    \;=\; \{\, (x, y) \in M_0 \times M_1 : \iota_0 x = \iota_1 y \,\},
  \]
  an \(R\)-submodule of \(M_0 \times M_1\): the pairs of chart sections agreeing on the
  overlap.
