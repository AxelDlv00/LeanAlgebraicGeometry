For every Mayer--Vietoris square \(S\), the complex of \ref{def:moduleShortComplex} is a
  short exact sequence of sheaves of \(R\)-modules:
  \[
    0 \to R[X_1] \to R[X_2] \oplus R[X_3] \to R[X_4] \to 0 .
  \]
