Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, \(f : R^n \to M\) a surjective \(R\)-linear map, and
  \(k \in \mathbb{N}\). If \(M\) has projective dimension \(< k + 2\), then
  \(K = \ker f\) has projective dimension \(< k + 1\).
