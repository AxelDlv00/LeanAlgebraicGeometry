Let \(C\) be the challenge curve over \(k\) and \(K/k\) a field extension. The
  base-changed curve \(C_K\), a legal challenge curve over \(K\)
  (\ref{thm:baseChangeCurve_bundle}), satisfies
  \[
    \genus(C_K) = \genus(C).
  \]
