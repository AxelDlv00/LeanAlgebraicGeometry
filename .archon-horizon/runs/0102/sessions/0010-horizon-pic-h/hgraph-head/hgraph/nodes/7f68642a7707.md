Let \(D\) be an affine two-cover of \(C\) and \(R\) a commutative \(k\)-algebra.
  There is an \(R\)-linear equivalence
  \[
    R \otimes_k H^1\bigl(C, \struct{C}\bigr) \;\cong\;
      H^1\bigl(C_R, \struct{C_R}\bigr)
  \]
  on the cohomology carriers.
