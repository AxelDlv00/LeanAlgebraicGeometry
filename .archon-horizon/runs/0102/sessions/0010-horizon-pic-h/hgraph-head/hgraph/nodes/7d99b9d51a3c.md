For every section \(b \in \Gamma(\PP^1, U_1)\),
  \[
    \gamma_{01}\bigl(b|_{U_0 \cap U_1}\bigr) = q(T^{-1}), \qquad q = \gamma_1(b);
  \]
  under the identifications, restriction from the right chart to the overlap is
  \(t \mapsto T^{-1}\).
