Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and \(a, b \in R\) with \(\mathrm{span}\{a\} = \mathrm{span}\{b\}\).
  If \(a\) is a nonzerodivisor, then \(a\) and \(b\) differ by a unit: there is a unit \(u \in
  R^{\times}\) with \(a = u\,b\).
