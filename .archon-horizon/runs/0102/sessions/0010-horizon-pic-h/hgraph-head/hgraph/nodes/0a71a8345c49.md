For a closed point \(x\), the Picard class of the point-divisor local equations at \(x\) equals
  the anchored class of the one-point divisor \(1 \cdot x\):
  \[
    \mathrm{picClass}(1 \cdot x) \;=\; \struct X(1 \cdot x).
  \]
