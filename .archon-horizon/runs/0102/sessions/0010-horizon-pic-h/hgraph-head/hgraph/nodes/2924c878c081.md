Let \(M\) be a finitely generated \(R\)-module and \(\mathfrak p\) a prime. Then
  \(M \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak p) = 0\) if and only if \(M_{\mathfrak p} = 0\), i.e.\
  \(\mathfrak p \notin \operatorname{Supp} M\).
