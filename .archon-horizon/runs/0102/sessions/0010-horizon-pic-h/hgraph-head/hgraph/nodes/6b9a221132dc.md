For an object \(U\) of \(\mathcal C\), a sheaf \(F\) of \(R\)-modules and \(n \ge 0\), the
  degree-\(n\) cohomology of \(U\) with coefficients in \(F\) is the \(R\)-module
  \[
    H'^n(U, F) \;=\; \Ext^n\bigl(R[U],\, F\bigr).
  \]
