Let \(X\) be a scheme over \(\Spec k\) whose structure morphism is bijective on global
  sections. Then \(H^0(X, \struct{X/k})\) is one-dimensional over \(k\); in particular it is
  finite-dimensional and \(h^0(\struct{X/k}) = 1\).
