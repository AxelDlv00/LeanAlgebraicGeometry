Let \(R\) be a ring, \(M\) an \(R\)-module and \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) with
  \(\mathrm{pd}_R(M) = n\). Then \(M\) has projective dimension \(< n + 1\), i.e.\
  \(\Ext^i_R(M, -) = 0\) for all \(i \geq n + 1\).
