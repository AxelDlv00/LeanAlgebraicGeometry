For \(V \subseteq C\) open, pullback of sections along \(\rho\) (allowed since
  \(\rho^{-1}(V_R) = V_{R'}\), \ref{lem:relCurveMap_proj}) is a ring homomorphism
  \[
    \rho^{\sharp} \;:\; \Gamma(C_R, V_R) \longrightarrow \Gamma(C_{R'}, V_{R'}) .
  \]
