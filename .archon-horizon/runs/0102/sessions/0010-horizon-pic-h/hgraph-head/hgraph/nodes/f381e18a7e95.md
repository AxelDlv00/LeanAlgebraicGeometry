Let \(D, D'\) be Weil divisors on \(X\) with \([\struct X(D)] = [\struct X(D')]\). If
  \(H^0(\struct X(D))\) (respectively \(H^1(\struct X(D))\)) is a finite-dimensional
  \(K\)-module, then so is \(H^0(\struct X(D'))\) (respectively \(H^1(\struct X(D'))\)).
