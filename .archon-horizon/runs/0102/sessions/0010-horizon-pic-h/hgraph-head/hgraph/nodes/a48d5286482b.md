With the structure of Definition~\ref{def:tensorAwayAlgebra}, \(S_i \otimes_A S_j\) is the
  localization of \(B_1 \otimes_A B_2\) away from the element
  \((r \otimes 1)(1 \otimes s) = r \otimes s\).
