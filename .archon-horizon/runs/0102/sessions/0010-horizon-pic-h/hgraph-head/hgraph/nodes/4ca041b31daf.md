Let \(W\) be an affine open of a scheme \(X\) and \(s \in \Gamma(X, W)\) a nonzerodivisor. Then
  for every point \(y \in W\) the germ of \(s\) at \(y\) is a nonzerodivisor of the stalk
  \(\struct{X, y}\).
