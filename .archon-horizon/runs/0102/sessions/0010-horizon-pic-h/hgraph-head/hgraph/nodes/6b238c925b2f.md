For \(i : U \to V\) in \(\mathcal C\), restriction along \(i\) is the \(R\)-linear map
  \(H'^n(V, F) \to H'^n(U, F)\), \(x \mapsto x \circ R[i]\): precomposition with
  \(R[i] : R[U] \to R[V]\) (contravariant functoriality of \(\Ext^n(-, F)\)).
