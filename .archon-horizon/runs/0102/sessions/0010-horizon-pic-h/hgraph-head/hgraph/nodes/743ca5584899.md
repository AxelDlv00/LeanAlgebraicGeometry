Write \(g \colon k[X] \to B\) for the coordinate map, so \(g(p) = p(u)\). For every polynomial
  \(p \in k[X]\),
  \[
    g(p) \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes g(p) \;\in\; (\delta),
  \]
  the ideal generated by the diagonal equation.
