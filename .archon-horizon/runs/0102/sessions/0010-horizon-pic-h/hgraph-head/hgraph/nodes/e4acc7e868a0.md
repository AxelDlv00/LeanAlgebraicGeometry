title: K\"ahler rank equals transcendence degree over a perfect field

Let \(k\) be a perfect field and \(K\) a field extension of \(k\) that is essentially
  of finite type. Then
  \[
    \dim_K \Omega_{K/k} = \mathrm{trdeg}_k(K).
  \]
