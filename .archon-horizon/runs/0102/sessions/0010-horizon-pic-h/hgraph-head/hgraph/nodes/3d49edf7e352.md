The comparison morphism satisfies
  \(\mathrm{pr}_1 \circ \rho = \mathrm{pr}_1\) and
  \(\mathrm{pr}_2 \circ \rho = \Spec(\varphi) \circ \mathrm{pr}_2\); consequently
  \(\rho^{-1}(V_R) = V_{R'}\) for every open \(V \subseteq C\).
