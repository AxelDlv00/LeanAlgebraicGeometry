Let \(R\) be a ring and \((S, \mathfrak m, \kappa)\) a local \(R\)-algebra,
  formally smooth over \(R\), with \(\Omega_{S/R}\) free of rank
  \(n \in \mathbb{N}\). If the composite \(R \to S \to \kappa\) is
  \emph{bijective}, then
  \[
    \dim_\kappa \mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2 = n .
  \]
