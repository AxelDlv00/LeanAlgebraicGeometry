\([E,\lambda] = [F,\mu]\) in \(\mathrm{PicEtAff}(C,A)\) if and only if there exist a presented
  \'etale cover \(G\) of \(A\) and homomorphisms of \(A\)-algebras \(f \colon B_E \to B_G\),
  \(g \colon B_F \to B_G\) with \(f_{*}\lambda = g_{*}\mu\).
