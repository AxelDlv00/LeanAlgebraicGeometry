In the situation of \ref{def:pointPullbackEquations}, the meromorphic presentation
  (\ref{lem:localEquations_presentation}) of the pulled tracked point system has
  trivialising elements
  \[
    f_z \;=\;
    \begin{cases}
      f^{\sharp}(t), & f(z) = x', \\
      1, & f(z) \neq x',
    \end{cases}
  \]
  at each point \(z\) of \(W\).
