Let \(\delta \colon Z \to Y\) and \(\delta_r \colon Y \to Y\) satisfy \(\delta \circ
  \delta_r = \delta\), and suppose the \(\delta_r\)-pullback of \(c\) is trivial. Then the
  \(\delta\)-pullback of \(c\) is trivial: \(\delta^{\sharp} c = 1\).
