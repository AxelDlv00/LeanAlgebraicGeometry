\(\id_X^{*} = \id_{J_X}\) (both slots taken by the same datum \(d_X\)), and
  for \(g \colon X \to Y\) and \(g' \colon Y \to Z\),
  \[
    (g' \circ g)^{*} \;=\; g^{*} \circ g'^{*} \colon J_Z \longrightarrow J_X .
  \]
