The Jacobian is a contravariant functor
  \[
    \Jac : \mathrm{Curve}(k)^{\op} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Grp}\bigl(\Over(\Spec k)\bigr),
  \]
  sending a curve to its Jacobian group scheme and a morphism of curves to the induced pullback of
  line bundles. In particular an isomorphism of curves induces an isomorphism of Jacobian group
  schemes.
