For a sheaf \(F\) of \(R\)-modules on \((\mathcal C, J)\) and \(n \ge 0\), the degree-\(n\)
  cohomology of \(F\) is the \(R\)-module
  \[
    H^n(F) \;=\; \Ext^n\bigl(R_J,\, F\bigr),
  \]
  the \(\Ext\)-group computed in the category of sheaves of \(R\)-modules.
