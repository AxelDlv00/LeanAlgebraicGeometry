Let \(C\) be the challenge curve and \(R\) a commutative \(k\)-algebra. There is an
  \(R\)-linear equivalence
  \[
    R \otimes_k H^0\bigl(C, \struct C\bigr) \;\cong\; H^0\bigl(C_R, \struct{C_R}\bigr).
  \]
