Let \(D, D'\) be Weil divisors on \(X\) with \([\struct X(D)] = [\struct X(D')]\). If the
  degree-one cohomology \(H^1(\struct X(D))\) vanishes (in the strong sense of being a
  subsingleton), then so does \(H^1(\struct X(D'))\).
