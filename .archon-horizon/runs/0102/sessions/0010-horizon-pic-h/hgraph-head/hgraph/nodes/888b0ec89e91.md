Let \(I = (\pi)\) be a nonzero principal ideal of \(B\) and \(e \ge 0\), with \(B/I^e\)
  finite-dimensional over \(k\). Then
  \[
    \dim_k\bigl(B/I^e\bigr) \;=\; e \cdot \dim_k(B/I) .
  \]
