For every \(f \colon T' \to T\), \(s \in \mathrm{picEt}(C, T)\) and affine open \(W\) of
  \(T'_{\mathrm{left}}\), there exists a unique plus class \(v \in \mathrm{PicEtAff}(C, \Gamma(T',
  W))\) with the pullback-value property for \((f, s, W)\).
