The degree map \(\deg_K : \Div(X) \to \Z\) is a homomorphism of abelian groups: it satisfies
  \(\deg_K 0 = 0\), \(\deg_K(D + D') = \deg_K D + \deg_K D'\) and \(\deg_K(-D) = -\deg_K D\).
