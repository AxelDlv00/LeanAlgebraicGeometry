Let \((w_i)_{i \in \iota}\) be a \emph{finite} clopen partition of \(Y\). Every family
  \((L_i)_{i \in \iota}\) with \(L_i \in \Pic(Z_i)\) is the family of restrictions of a single
  class \(M \in \Pic(Y)\): \(w_i^{*} M = L_i\) for all \(i\).
