Let \(B\) be a Dedekind domain, \(P\) a nonzero prime of \(B\), and \(f \neq 0\).
  Then the \(P\)-adic valuation of \(f\) equals the multiplicity of \(P\) in the prime
  factorization of the principal ideal \((f)\):
  \[
    v_P(f) \;=\; \mathrm{ord}_P(f) .
  \]
