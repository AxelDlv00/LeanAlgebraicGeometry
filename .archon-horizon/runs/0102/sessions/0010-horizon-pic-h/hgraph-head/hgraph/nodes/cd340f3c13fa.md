For \(i \in \{0,1\}\), the dehomogenization at \(X_i\) is the \(k\)-algebra homomorphism
  \(\delta_i : k[X_0, X_1] \to k[t]\) with \(\delta_i(X_i) = 1\) and \(\delta_i(X_j) = t\) for
  \(j \neq i\).
