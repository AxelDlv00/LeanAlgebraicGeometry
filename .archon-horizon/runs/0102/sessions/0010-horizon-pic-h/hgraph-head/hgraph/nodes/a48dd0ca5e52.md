\(\deg^{+}_K\) is a homomorphism: \(\deg^{+}_K(1) = 0\),
  \(\deg^{+}_K(a b) = \deg^{+}_K(a) + \deg^{+}_K(b)\) and
  \(\deg^{+}_K(a^{-1}) = -\deg^{+}_K(a)\).
