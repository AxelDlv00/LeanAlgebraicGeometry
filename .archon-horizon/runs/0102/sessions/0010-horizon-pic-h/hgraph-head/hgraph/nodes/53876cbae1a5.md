Two meromorphic presentations with equal divisors present equal \v Cech Picard classes: if
  \(\mathrm{presentationDivisor}(P) = \mathrm{presentationDivisor}(Q)\) then \(\mathrm{picClass}(P)
  = \mathrm{picClass}(Q)\).
