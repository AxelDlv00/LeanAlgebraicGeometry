For \(W' \le W\), componentwise restriction carries \(F_g(W)\) into \(F_g(W')\), and
  the resulting \(k\)-linear maps are functorial in the inclusion; so \(W \mapsto F_g(W)\)
  is a presheaf of \(k\)-modules.
