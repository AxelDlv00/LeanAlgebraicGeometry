For a \(k\)-algebra homomorphism \(\varphi \colon R \to R'\), descending a global unit on
  \(\Spec R\) and pulling it back along \(C \lhd \Spec\varphi\) equals first pulling back along
  \(\Spec\varphi\) and then descending on \(\Spec R'\).
