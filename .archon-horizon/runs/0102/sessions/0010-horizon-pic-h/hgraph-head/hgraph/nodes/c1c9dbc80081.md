Let \(v \in (P \otimes_A P)^{\times}\) be an \(A\)-descent \(1\)-cocycle relative to \(A \to
  P\).  Then its collapse \(\pi(v)\) is a \(B\)-descent \(1\)-cocycle relative to \(B \to P\).
