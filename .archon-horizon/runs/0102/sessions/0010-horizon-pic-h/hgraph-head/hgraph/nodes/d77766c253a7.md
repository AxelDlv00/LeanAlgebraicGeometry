Let \(W, X\) be schemes over \(\Spec K\) and \(f : W \to X\) an isomorphism of
  schemes over \(\Spec K\). Then for every point \(z\) of \(W\),
  \[
    [\kappa(z) : K] \;=\; [\kappa(f(z)) : K].
  \]
