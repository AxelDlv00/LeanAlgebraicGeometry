Let \(x'\) be a non-generic point of \(C_{K_1}\), with tracked point equations at
  \(x'\) (\ref{def:pointEquations}, from the tracked uniformizer data of
  \ref{lem:pointUniformizerData}). The \emph{pulled tracked point system} is the
  pullback of this local-equation system along the transition \(\pi\)
  (\ref{def:localEquations_pullback}), with regularity discharged by integrality of
  both curves (\ref{lem:pullbackEqn_regular}, \ref{thm:baseChangeCurve_integral},
  generic points matching by \ref{lem:baseFieldTransition_genericPoint}). Its
  \emph{trivialising unit} is the pullback
  \(\pi^{\sharp}(t) \in K(C_{K_2})^{\times}\) of the chosen uniformizer \(t\) at
  \(x'\) (\ref{lem:uniformizer}, \ref{def:functionFieldMap}).
