For \(i, j \in \{0,1\}\), the canonical degree-zero fraction
  \(X_j^{\deg X_i} / X_i^{\deg X_j}\) attached to the pair \((X_i, X_j)\) of homogeneous
  elements equals the chart coordinate \(t_{ij} = X_j / X_i \in A_{(X_i)}\).
