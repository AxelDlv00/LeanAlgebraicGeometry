The transition units of the pulled-back system \(f^{*}E\) are the \(f\)-pullbacks of those of
  \(E\): for all \(y, y'\),
  \[
    g^{f^{*}E}_{y y'} = f^{*}\bigl(g_{f(y)f(y')}\bigr).
  \]
  Consequently the unit \v Cech \(1\)-cocycle of \(f^{*}E\) is the \(f\)-pullback of the cocycle of
  \(E\).
