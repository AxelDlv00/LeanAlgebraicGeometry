Let \(T\) be an object of \(\Over(\Spec k)\) and \(W \subseteq T\) any open. Then pullback
  of sections along the second projection,
  \(\Gamma(T, W) \to \Gamma(C \otimes T, \mathrm{pr}^{-1}W)\), is injective.
