Let \(x \in U\) and let \(t\) be a section of \(\mathrm{sky}_x(J)\) over \(U\), i.e.\ a class in
  \(J\). There is an open \(W\) with \(x \in W \subseteq U\) and a section of \(\mathcal O(D)\) over
  \(W\) whose jump class is \(t|_W\).
