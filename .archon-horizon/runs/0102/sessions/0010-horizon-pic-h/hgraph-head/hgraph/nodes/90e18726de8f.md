Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, \(I \subseteq R\) an ideal, \(M\) an \(R\)-module and
  \(\iota\) a finite index type. Inside the product module \(\iota \to M\),
  \[
    I \cdot (\iota \to M) \;=\; \{\, f : \iota \to M \mid f(i) \in IM
      \text{ for all } i \,\}.
  \]
