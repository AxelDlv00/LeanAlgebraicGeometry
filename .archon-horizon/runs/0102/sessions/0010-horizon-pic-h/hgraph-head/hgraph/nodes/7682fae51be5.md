Theorems~\ref{thm:baseChangeCurve_bundle}, \ref{thm:baseChangeCurve_integral} and
  \ref{thm:baseChangeCurve_cohomFinite} discharge every hypothesis of the degree stack for \(C_K\)
  over \(K\). Hence the degree homomorphism \(\deg_{\Pic} \colon \Pic(C_K) \to \Z\)
  (Definition~\ref{def:classDeg}) is defined on the base-changed curve, for every field extension
  \(K/k\).
