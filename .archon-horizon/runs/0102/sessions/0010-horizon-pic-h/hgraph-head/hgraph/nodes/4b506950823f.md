For a short exact sequence \(0 \to X_1 \xrightarrow{f} X_2 \to X_3 \to 0\) of sheaves of
  \(R\)-modules and a degree \(n\) with \(H^n(X_3) = 0\), the map
  \(H^n(f) : H^n(X_1) \to H^n(X_2)\) is surjective.
