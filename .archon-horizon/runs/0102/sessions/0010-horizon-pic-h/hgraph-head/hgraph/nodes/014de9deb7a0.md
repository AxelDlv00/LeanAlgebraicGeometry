For a field \(K\) and a value \(x \in K\), the \(K\)-rational point of
  \(\mathbb{A}^1 = \Spec K[X]\) at \(x\): the maximal ideal \(\ker(\mathrm{ev}_x) = (X - x)\). It
  lies in a basic open \(D(h)\) iff \(h(x) \neq 0\).
