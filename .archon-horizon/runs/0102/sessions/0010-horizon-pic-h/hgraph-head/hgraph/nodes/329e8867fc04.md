Let \(M\) be a finitely generated \(R\)-module with
  \(M \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak p) = 0\) for every prime \(\mathfrak p\). Then
  \(M = 0\): fibrewise vanishing over the whole base propagates to global vanishing.
