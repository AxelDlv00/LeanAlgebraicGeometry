For a section \(s \in \Gamma(X, U)\) over a nonempty open \(U\), its germ at the generic point
  \(\mathrm{germ}_\eta(s) \in K(X)\) has poles bounded by the zero divisor on \(U\), i.e.\ lies in
  \(\mathcal O(0)(U)\).
