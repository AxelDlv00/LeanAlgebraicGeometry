Granting a representation
  \(\operatorname{divFunctorAff}(C,n)\text{ is represented by }D\), and separately supplying a
  chart family of relatively representable open immersions together with the required
  locally-surjective \(\Sigma\)-map, the widened Abel chart
  \(\operatorname{abelSigmaChartAff}\) (the Abel class of the arbitrary-affine carrier) is the conditional
  input to \ref{def:pic0RepresentableByOfCharts}. The degree \(n\) is a window parameter; the
  formal degree-window lemmas show that a reference divisor of degree at least \(g\) is
  available, while any consumer that insists on degree exactly \(g\) has an additional
  arithmetic/degree-pinning obligation. Only after the representation and the two seam
  antecedents are inhabited does the degree-zero Picard functor become representable and yield
  a Jacobian datum (\ref{def:jacobian_data}) --- hence, through the frozen definition
  (\ref{def:jacobian}), the Jacobian of \(C\).

  The universal family, the open-immersion chart certificates, and local surjectivity are
  distinct unproved inputs here.  The passage from a representation over a separably closed
  base to one over \(k\) is a further descent step, not addressed here.
