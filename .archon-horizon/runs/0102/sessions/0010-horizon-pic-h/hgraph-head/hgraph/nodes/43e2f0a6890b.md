Let \(\bar k\) be an algebraically closed field and \(S\) a nonzero
  standard-smooth \(\bar k\)-algebra. For every maximal ideal
  \(\mathfrak m \subseteq S\), the localisation \(S_{\mathfrak m}\) is a
  regular local ring.
