Let \(X\) be a scheme, \(V \subseteq X\) open, and \((W_i)_{i \in I}\) a family of opens
  with \(W_i \subseteq V\) and \(V \subseteq \bigcup_i W_i\). If two units
  \(s, t \in \struct X(V)^{\times}\) satisfy \(s|_{W_i} = t|_{W_i}\) for all \(i\), then
  \(s = t\).
