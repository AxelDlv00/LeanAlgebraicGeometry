Let \(k\) be a field and \(S\) a standard-smooth \(k\)-algebra of relative dimension
  \(n\). Then every maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m \subseteq S\) has
  \(\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak m) \geq n\).
