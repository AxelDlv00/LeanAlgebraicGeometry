Let \(B\) be a Dedekind domain over \(k\) and \(f \neq 0\) an element such that the
  residue \(B/P\) is finite-dimensional over \(k\) for every prime factor \(P\) of
  \((f)\). Then \(B/(f)\) is finite-dimensional over \(k\).
