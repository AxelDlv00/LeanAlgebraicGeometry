For a point \(x \in X\) and a finite-dimensional \(K\)-module \(M\), the cohomology of the
  skyscraper \(\mathrm{sky}_x(M)\) is finite-dimensional in degrees \(0\) and \(1\):
  \(H^0\) is \(M\) and \(H^1\) vanishes.
