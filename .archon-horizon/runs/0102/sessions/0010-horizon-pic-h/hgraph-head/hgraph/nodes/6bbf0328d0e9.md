title: Restriction of unit sections is transitive

For opens \(W \subseteq V \subseteq U\) of a scheme \(X\) and
  \(u \in \struct X(U)^{\times}\), restricting \(u\) to \(V\) and then to \(W\) is
  restricting \(u\) to \(W\).
