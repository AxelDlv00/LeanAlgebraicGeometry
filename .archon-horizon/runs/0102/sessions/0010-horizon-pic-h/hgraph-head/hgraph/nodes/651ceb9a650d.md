For a finitely generated \(R\)-module \(M\),
  \[
    \{\mathfrak p \in \Spec R : M \otimes_R \kappa(\mathfrak p) = 0\}
    \;=\; \Spec R \smallsetminus \operatorname{Supp} M .
  \]
