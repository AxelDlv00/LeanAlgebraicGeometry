With no Noetherian hypothesis on \(R\), the fibrewise-vanishing locus
  \(\{\mathfrak p \in \Spec R : H^1(\text{pair of } F_g) \otimes_R
  \kappa(\mathfrak p) = 0\}\) is open in \(\Spec R\) --- the strata over which the
  engine fires.
