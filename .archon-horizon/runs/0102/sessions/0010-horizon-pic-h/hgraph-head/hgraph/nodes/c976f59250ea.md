For classes \(\lambda, \mu \in \mathrm{picEt}(C,T)\) and a field point \(t\),
  \[
    \deg_t(1) = 0, \qquad
    \deg_t(\lambda\mu) = \deg_t(\lambda) + \deg_t(\mu), \qquad
    \deg_t(\lambda^{-1}) = -\deg_t(\lambda).
  \]
