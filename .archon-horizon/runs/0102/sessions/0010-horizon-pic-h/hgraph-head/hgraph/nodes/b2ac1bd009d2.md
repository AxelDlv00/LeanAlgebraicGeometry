Let \((R, \mathfrak m)\) be a Noetherian local ring and \(M\) a nonzero finite
  \(R\)-module with \(\mathrm{pd}_R(M) = k + 1\) for some \(k \in \mathbb{N}\). Then
  \[
    (k + 1) + \mathrm{depth}(M) = \mathrm{depth}(R).
  \]
