Let \((R, \mathfrak m)\) be a Noetherian local ring, \(M\) a finite nontrivial
  \(R\)-module, and \(x \in \mathfrak m\) an \(M\)-regular element. Then
  \[
    \operatorname{depth} M \;\le\; \operatorname{depth}(M/xM) + 1 .
  \]
