For a closed point \(x\), the divisor \(D - x\) is obtained by decreasing the coefficient of \(D\)
  at \(x\) by one and leaving every other coefficient unchanged. Thus \((D-x)_x = D_x - 1\) and
  \(D - x \le D\).
