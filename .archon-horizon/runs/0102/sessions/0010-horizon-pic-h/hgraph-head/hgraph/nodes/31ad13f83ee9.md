A class \(\lambda \in \mathrm{picEt}(C, \sigma_{!}T)\) is of degree zero at every
  field point if and only if its componentwise lift
  \(\mathrm{lift}(\lambda) \in \mathrm{picEt}(C_L, T)\) is.
