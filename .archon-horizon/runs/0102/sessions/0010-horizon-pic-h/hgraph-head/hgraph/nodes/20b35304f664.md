Let \((R, \mathfrak m)\) be a Cohen--Macaulay Noetherian local ring, \(J\) a
  prime of height \(1\), and \(x_1, \dots, x_r\) a list such that the only prime
  containing \((x_1, \dots, x_r) + J\) is \(\mathfrak m\). Then
  \(\dim R \le r + 1\).
