Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, \(I \subseteq R\) an ideal, and \(e : M \cong M'\) an
  \(R\)-linear equivalence of \(R\)-modules. Then
  \(\mathrm{depth}_I(M) = \mathrm{depth}_I(M')\).
