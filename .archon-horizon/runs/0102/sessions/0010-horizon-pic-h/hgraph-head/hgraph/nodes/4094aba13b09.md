Let \(B\) be a domain over \(k\) and \(I = (\pi)\) a nonzero principal ideal with
  \(B/I\) finite-dimensional over \(k\). Then \(B/I^n\) is finite-dimensional over
  \(k\) for every \(n \ge 0\).
