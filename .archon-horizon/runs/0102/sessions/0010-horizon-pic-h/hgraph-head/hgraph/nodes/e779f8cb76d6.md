Precomposed with the base inclusion \(A \to B\), the two coprojections agree:
  \(\mathrm{tensorInl} \circ (A \to B) = \mathrm{tensorInr} \circ (A \to B)\) as \(k\)-algebra
  homomorphisms \(A \to B \otimes_A B\).
