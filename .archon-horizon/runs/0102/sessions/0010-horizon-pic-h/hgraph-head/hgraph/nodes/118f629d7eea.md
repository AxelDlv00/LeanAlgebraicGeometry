For \(i \neq j\), the isomorphism of \(k\)-algebras
  \(\alpha_i : A_{(X_i)} \cong k[t]\) given by the mutually inverse homomorphisms of
  \ref{def:P1_awayToPoly} and \ref{def:P1_polyToAway}; the chart coordinate \(t_{ij}\)
  corresponds to \(t\).
