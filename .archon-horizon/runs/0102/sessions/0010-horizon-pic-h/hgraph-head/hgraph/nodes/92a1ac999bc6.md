Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, \(N, M\) two \(R\)-modules, \(i \geq 0\), and
  \(x \in \mathrm{Ann}_R(N)\). Then \(x\) annihilates \(\Ext^i_R(N, M)\): for every
  class \(e \in \Ext^i_R(N, M)\) we have \(x \cdot e = 0\).
