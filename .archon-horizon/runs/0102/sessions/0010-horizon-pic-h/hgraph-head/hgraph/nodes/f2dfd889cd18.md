The point-product class map (\ref{def:divisorClass}) and the anchored meromorphic class map
  (\ref{def:curveDivisor_picClass}) agree on every Weil divisor:
  \[
    \mathrm{divisorClass}(D) \;=\; \struct X(D), \qquad D \in \Div(X).
  \]
