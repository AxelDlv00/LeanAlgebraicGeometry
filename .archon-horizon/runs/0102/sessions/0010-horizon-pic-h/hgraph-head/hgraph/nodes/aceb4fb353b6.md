Let \(K\) be a field extension of \(k\) and \(a \in \mathrm{PicEtAff}(E, K)\) a plus
  class with \(\deg^{+}_K(a) = 0\). Then \(\deg^{+}_K\bigl(g^{*}a\bigr) = 0\).
