For an open \(U \ni \eta\), a unit section \(u \in \Gamma(X,U)^{\times}\), and a closed point
  \(x \in U\), the germ at \(\eta\) of \(u\) has trivial order at \(x\):
  \[
    \mathrm{ord}_x\bigl(\mathrm{germ}_\eta(u)\bigr) = 0.
  \]
