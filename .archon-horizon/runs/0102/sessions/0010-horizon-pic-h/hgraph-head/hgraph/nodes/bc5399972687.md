For every section \(a \in \Gamma(\PP^1, U_0)\),
  \[
    \gamma_{01}\bigl(a|_{U_0 \cap U_1}\bigr) = p(T), \qquad p = \gamma_0(a);
  \]
  under the identifications, restriction from the left chart to the overlap is
  \(t \mapsto T\).
