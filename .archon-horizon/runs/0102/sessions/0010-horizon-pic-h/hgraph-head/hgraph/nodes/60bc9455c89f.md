Let \(L\) be a field extension of \(k\) and \(y \in \mathrm{relPic}(E, \Spec L)\) a
  relative Picard class with \(\mathrm{relPicDeg}_L(y) = 0\). Then the curve transport
  satisfies \(\mathrm{relPicDeg}_L\bigl(g^{*}y\bigr) = 0\).
