The kernel of the collapse \(\pi \colon B \otimes_k B \to B \otimes_{k[X]} B\) is exactly the
  principal ideal generated by the diagonal equation:
  \[
    \ker \pi \;=\; (\delta) \;=\; (u \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes u).
  \]
