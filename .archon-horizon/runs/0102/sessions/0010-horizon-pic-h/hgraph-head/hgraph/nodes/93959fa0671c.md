Let \(g : A \to A'\) be a homomorphism of rings under \(k\) (so \(A'\) is under \(k\)
  via \(g \circ \sigma\)), \(i \in \{0,1\}\) and \(a \in A\). Then the composite
  \(\Spec A' \xrightarrow{\Spec g} \Spec A \to \PP^1\) of \ref{def:P1_fromSpecChart} at
  \(a\) is the morphism of \ref{def:P1_fromSpecChart} at \(g(a) \in A'\).
