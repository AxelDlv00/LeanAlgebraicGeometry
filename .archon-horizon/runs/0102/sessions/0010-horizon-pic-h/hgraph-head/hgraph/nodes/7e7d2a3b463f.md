Let \(i \neq j\) and let \(e_0, e_1 \ge 0\) with \(e_0 + e_1 = a\). Then in \(A_{(X_i)}\)
  \[
    \frac{X_0^{e_0} X_1^{e_1}}{X_i^{a}} \;=\; t_{ij}^{\,e_j}.
  \]
