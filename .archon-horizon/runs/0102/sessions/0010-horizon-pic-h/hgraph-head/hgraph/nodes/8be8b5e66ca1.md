The principal divisor is a homomorphism from \(K(X)^\times\) to \(\Div(X)\): for all
  \(g, g' \in K(X)^\times\),
  \[
    \mathrm{div}(g g') = \mathrm{div}(g) + \mathrm{div}(g'), \qquad \mathrm{div}(1) = 0 .
  \]
