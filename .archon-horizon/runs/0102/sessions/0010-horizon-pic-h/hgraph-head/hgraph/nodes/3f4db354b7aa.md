The \emph{image lattice} of the pair \(P\) is the \(R\)-submodule
  \(Q(P) = \iota_0(M_0) + \iota_1(M_1) \subseteq N\), and the degree-one cohomology of
  \(P\) is the quotient
  \[
    H^1(P) \;=\; N \big/ Q(P),
  \]
  with quotient projection \(N \twoheadrightarrow H^1(P)\), \(n \mapsto [n]\).
