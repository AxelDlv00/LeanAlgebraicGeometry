The equivalence of \ref{def:constHomEquiv} is natural in the sheaf: for
  \(\varphi : R_J \to F\) and \(f : F \to G\), the section corresponding to
  \(f \circ \varphi\) is \(f_\top\) applied to the section corresponding to \(\varphi\).
