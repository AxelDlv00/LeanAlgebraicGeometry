For the challenge curve \(C\) over \(k\) and any field extension \(K/k\),
  \[
    \dim_K H^1\bigl(C_K, \struct{C_K}\bigr) = \dim_k H^1\bigl(C, \struct C\bigr)
      = \genus(C),
    \qquad
    \dim_K H^0\bigl(C_K, \struct{C_K}\bigr) = \dim_k H^0\bigl(C, \struct C\bigr).
  \]
