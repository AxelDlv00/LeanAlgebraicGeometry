Let \(C\) be smooth of relative dimension one and geometrically irreducible over the
  field \(k\), and let \(K_1, K_2\) be field extensions of \(k\). Then the base-field
  transition satisfies \(\pi(\eta_{C_{K_2}}) = \eta_{C_{K_1}}\).
