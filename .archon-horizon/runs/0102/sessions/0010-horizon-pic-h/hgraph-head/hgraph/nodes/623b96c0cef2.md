Let \(t\) be a uniformizer at \(x\). Multiplication by \(t^a\) is a \(K\)-linear isomorphism
  \(L_a \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } L_0\), carrying \(L_{a-1}\) onto \(L_{-1}\).
