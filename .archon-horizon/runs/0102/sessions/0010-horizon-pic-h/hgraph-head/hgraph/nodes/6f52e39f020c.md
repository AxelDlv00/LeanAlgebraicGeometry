For every test \(T\), every field point \(t \colon \Spec K \to T\) and every \(m \in \N\),
  \[
    \deg_t(\theta_T) = \deg_{\Pic}(L_0) =: d_1,
    \qquad
    \deg_t\bigl(\theta_T^{\,m}\bigr) = m \, d_1 ,
  \]
  a base-field-invariant constant.
