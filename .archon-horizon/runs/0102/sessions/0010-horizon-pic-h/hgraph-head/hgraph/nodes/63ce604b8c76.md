For \(i \in \{0,1\}\), the \(k\)-algebra homomorphism \(A_{(X_i)} \to k[t]\) sending a
  degree-zero fraction \(a / X_i^n\) (with \(a\) homogeneous of degree \(n\)) to
  \(\delta_i(a)\).
