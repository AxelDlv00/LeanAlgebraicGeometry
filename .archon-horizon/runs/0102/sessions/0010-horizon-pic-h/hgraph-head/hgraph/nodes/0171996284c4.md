Let \(X\) be a scheme, proper over \(k\), and let \(\pi : X \to \PP^1\) be a locally
  quasi-finite morphism over \(k\) (i.e.\ its composite with the structure morphism of
  \(\PP^1\) is the structure morphism of \(X\)). Then \(\pi\) is finite.
