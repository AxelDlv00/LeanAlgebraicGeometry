Let \(\tau \colon A \to A'\) be a homomorphism of test algebras linear over both base
  fields. Then \(\tau^{*} \circ h^{*} = h^{*} \circ \tau^{*}\) as maps
  \(\mathrm{PicEtAff}(E, A) \to \mathrm{PicEtAff}(D, A')\).
