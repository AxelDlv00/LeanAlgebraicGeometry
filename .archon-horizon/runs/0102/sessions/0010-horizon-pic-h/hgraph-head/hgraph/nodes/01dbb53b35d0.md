Let \(k\) be a field and \(X\) an integral scheme, smooth of relative dimension one over
  \(\Spec k\). Then every point of \(X\) lies in an affine open \(V\) whose ring of
  sections \(\Gamma(X, V)\) is a Dedekind domain.
