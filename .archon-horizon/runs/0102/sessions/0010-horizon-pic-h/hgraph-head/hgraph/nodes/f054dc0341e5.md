On the vanishing locus, for every commutative \(R\)-algebra \(R'\) there is an
  \(R'\)-linear equivalence
  \[
    R' \otimes_R H^0\bigl(C_R, F_g\bigr) \;\cong\;
      \ker\bigl(\delta_{F_g} \otimes_R R'\bigr).
  \]
