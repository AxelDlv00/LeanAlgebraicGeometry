Assume $f_{T, *}\mathcal{O}_{X_T} \cong \mathcal{O}_T$ for all
schemes $T$ over $B$. Then the inertia stack of $\Picardstack_{X/B}$
is equal to $\mathbf{G}_m \times \Picardstack_{X/B}$.
