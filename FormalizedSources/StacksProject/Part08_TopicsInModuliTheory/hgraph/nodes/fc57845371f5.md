Let $g \geq 2$. The algebraic stack $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_g$ is a
Deligne-Mumford stack, proper and smooth over $\Spec(\mathbf{Z})$.
Moreover, the locus $\mathcal{M}_g$ parametrizing smooth curves
is a dense open substack.
