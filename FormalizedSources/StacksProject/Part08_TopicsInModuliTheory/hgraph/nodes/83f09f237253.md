Let $B$ be an algebraic space. Let $\pi : X \to Y$ be a quasi-finite
morphism of algebraic spaces which are separated and of finite presentation
over $B$. Then $\pi_*$ induces a morphism
$\Cohstack_{X/B} \to \Cohstack_{Y/B}$.
