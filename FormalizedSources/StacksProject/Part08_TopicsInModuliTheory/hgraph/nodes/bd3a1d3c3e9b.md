With $B, X, Y$ as in the introduction of this section, in addition
assume $X \to B$ is proper. Then the
subfunctor $\mathit{Isom}_B(Y, X) \subset \mathit{Mor}_B(Y, X)$
of isomorphisms is an open subspace.
