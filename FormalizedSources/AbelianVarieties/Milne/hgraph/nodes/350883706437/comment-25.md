Commit fe9186f2f1 proves, over an algebraically closed field, that a homomorphism of abelian varieties with finite scheme-theoretic kernel and equal topological Krull dimensions is an isogeny. The proof factors finite maps through their scheme-theoretic image and uses strict dimension drop. This is only the (c)-to-(a) direction in that specialization; the arbitrary-field four-way characterization, intrinsic flatness, and projectivity remain open, so this node stays empty.
