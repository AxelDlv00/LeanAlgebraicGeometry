\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves-about}

This project follows \emph{Universal secant bundles and syzygies of canonical curves} by Michael Kemeny (2020). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves/}{\texttt{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05849}{arXiv:2003.05849}.

For a polarized K3 surface or a canonical curve, Koszul cohomology is the middle cohomology of the usual three-term exterior-multiplication complex. Lazarsfeld--Mukai bundles and their universal secant constructions provide the geometric input. The argument uses kernel bundles, Hilbert schemes, and cohomology of tautological bundles. A universal secant bundle turns the required syzygy vanishing into an injectivity statement for a natural cohomology map.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}

\begin{theorem}[Generic Green vanishing]
\label{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves-main}
\source{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves}
\uses{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves-setup}
If $C$ is a general complex curve of genus $g=2k$ or $g=2k+1$, then $K_{k,1}(C,\omega_C)=0$.
\notready
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The extremal syzygy space on a K3 surface]
\label{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves-secondary}
\source{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves}
\uses{universal-secant-bundles-syzygies-canonical-curves-main}
Let $(X,L)$ be a Picard-rank-one polarized K3 surface of even genus $2k$. The extremal syzygy space is identified with a symmetric power of the space of sections of the Lazarsfeld--Mukai bundle, via the Veronese map supplied by universal secant bundles.
\notready
\end{theorem}
