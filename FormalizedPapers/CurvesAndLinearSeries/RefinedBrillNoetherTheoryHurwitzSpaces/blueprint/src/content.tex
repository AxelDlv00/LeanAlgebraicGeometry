\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-about}

This project follows \emph{A refined Brill--Noether theory over Hurwitz spaces} by Hannah K. Larson (2020). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces/}{\texttt{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.08597}{arXiv:1907.08597}.

Fix a general degree-$k$ cover $f:C\to\mathbb P^1$ and a degree-$d$ line bundle on $C$. Splitting loci record the possible degrees of the summands of the pushforward bundle $f_*L$. Degeneration to balanced covers and determinantal calculations give expected codimension, smoothness, and universal cycle classes for refined splitting loci over Hurwitz spaces.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}
\label{sec:refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-results}

\begin{theorem}[Smoothness and dimension of refined splitting loci]
\label{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-main}
\source{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces}
\uses{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-setup}
For a general degree-$k$, genus-$g$ cover and splitting vector $\vec e$, the refined splitting locus is smooth of pure dimension $g-u(\vec e)$ when $u(\vec e)\leq g$, and is empty when $u(\vec e)>g$.
\notready
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Universal class of a splitting locus]
\label{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-secondary}
\source{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces}
\uses{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-main}
When the expected codimension is attained, the class of the universal refined splitting locus is given by a universal polynomial in the Chern classes of the relevant pushforwards.
\notready
\end{proposition}
