\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{pixtons-formula-abel-jacobi-picard-stack-about}

This project follows \emph{Pixton's formula and Abel--Jacobi theory on the Picard stack} by Younghan Bae; David Holmes; Rahul Pandharipande; Johannes Schmitt; Rosa Schwarz (2023). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/pixtons-formula-abel-jacobi-picard-stack/}{\texttt{pixtons-formula-abel-jacobi-picard-stack}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08676}{arXiv:2004.08676}.

For pointed prestable curves and an integer weighting $A$ with total degree $d$, the Abel--Jacobi construction defines twisted double ramification cycles on the universal Picard stack. Prestable graphs, tautological psi classes, mod-$r$ weightings, and compactified Abel--Jacobi maps yield a graph-sum expression and its vanishing and closure properties.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\begin{theorem}[Universal twisted double ramification cycle]
\label{pixtons-formula-abel-jacobi-picard-stack-main}
\source{pixtons-formula-abel-jacobi-picard-stack}
\uses{pixtons-formula-abel-jacobi-picard-stack-setup}
Logarithmic compactification of the Abel--Jacobi map produces a universal twisted double ramification cycle on the universal Picard stack, compatible with the usual cycle on the moduli space of pointed curves.
\notready
\end{theorem}
