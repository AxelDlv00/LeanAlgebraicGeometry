\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{no-enriques-surface-over-the-integers-about}

This project follows \emph{There is no Enriques surface over the integers} by Stefan Schröer (2023). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/no-enriques-surface-over-the-integers/}{\texttt{no-enriques-surface-over-the-integers}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07025}{arXiv:2004.07025}.

An Enriques surface is studied through its numerical-class local system, elliptic and Jacobian fibrations, and contractions of surface families over arithmetic bases. The proof combines the classification of singular fibers, monodromy of numerical classes, finite-field point counts, and a systematic elimination of all possible fiber configurations.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.
