\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{motivic-invariants-birational-maps-about}

This project follows \emph{Motivic invariants of birational maps} by Hsueh-Yung Lin; Evgeny Shinder (2024). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/motivic-invariants-birational-maps/}{\texttt{motivic-invariants-birational-maps}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07389}{arXiv:2207.07389}.

Birational maps of smooth projective varieties are measured in truncated Grothendieck groups of varieties and by the defect contributed by their blow-up centers. The paper studies $L$-equivalence, links and strong birational maps, motivic invariants of Cremona transformations, and applications to K3 and K-trivial varieties.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.
