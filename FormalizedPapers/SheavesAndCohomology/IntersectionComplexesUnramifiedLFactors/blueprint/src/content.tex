\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors-about}

This project follows \emph{Intersection complexes and unramified L-factors} by Yiannis Sakellaridis; Jonathan Wang (2020). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors/}{\texttt{intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03943}{arXiv:2009.03943}.

Let $X$ be a spherical variety over a non-archimedean local field, with its arc space, invariant-theoretic Cartan cone, and intersection-complex function. The paper studies arc-space asymptotics, semi-infinite orbit dimensions, canonical affine closures, and Springer-theoretic perverse sheaves to compare IC pushforwards with unramified local factors.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\begin{conjecture}[IC pushforward and the L-group]
\label{intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors-main}
\source{intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors}
\uses{intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors-setup}
The normalized pushforward of the intersection-complex function is described by the character of a symplectic $L$-group representation whose weights are determined by the spherical variety.
