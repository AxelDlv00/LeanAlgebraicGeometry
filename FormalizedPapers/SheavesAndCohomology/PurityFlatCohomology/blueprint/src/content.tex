\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{purity-flat-cohomology-about}

This project follows \emph{Purity for flat cohomology} by Kęstutis Česnavičius; Peter Scholze (2024). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/purity-flat-cohomology/}{\texttt{purity-flat-cohomology}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.10932}{arXiv:1912.10932}.

The paper studies flat and étale cohomology with finite locally free coefficients on schemes and rings, including the characteristic-primary and prime-to-characteristic cases. Arc and $I$-complete arc hyperdescent, perfectoid geometry, crystalline and prismatic Dieudonné theory, and devissage reduce purity to local algebraic and cohomological inputs.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.
