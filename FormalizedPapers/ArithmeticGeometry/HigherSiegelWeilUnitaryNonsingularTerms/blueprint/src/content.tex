\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms-about}

This project follows \emph{Higher Siegel--Weil formula for unitary groups: the non-singular terms} by Tony Feng; Zhiwei Yun; Wei Zhang (2023). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms/}{\texttt{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11514}{arXiv:2103.11514}.

Let $X'/X$ be the fixed double cover of curves, let $\mathcal E$ be a rank-$n$ vector bundle on $X'$, and let $a:\mathcal E\to\sigma^*\mathcal E^\vee$ be an injective Hermitian map. Unitary shtuka stacks, special cycles, virtual fundamental classes, Hermitian Springer theory, and local density formulas identify geometric cycle degrees with Fourier coefficients of Eisenstein series.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}

\begin{theorem}[Higher Siegel--Weil identity]
\label{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms-main}
\source{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms}
\uses{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms-setup}
For every $r\geq0$, the $r$-th derivative at the central point of the normalized non-singular Fourier coefficient of the Siegel--Eisenstein series equals the degree of the corresponding special cycle.
\notready
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Virtual special-cycle class]
\label{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms-secondary}
\source{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms}
\uses{higher-siegel-weil-unitary-nonsingular-terms-main}
For an injective Hermitian map of equal-rank bundles, the special-cycle stack carries a proper virtual zero-cycle whose degree enters the higher derivative formula.

\end{proposition}
