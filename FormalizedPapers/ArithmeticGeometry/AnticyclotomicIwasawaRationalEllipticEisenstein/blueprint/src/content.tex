\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{anticyclotomic-iwasawa-rational-elliptic-eisenstein-about}

This project follows \emph{On the anticyclotomic Iwasawa theory of rational elliptic curves at Eisenstein primes} by Francesc Castella; Giada Grossi; Jaehoon Lee; Christopher Skinner (2021). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/anticyclotomic-iwasawa-rational-elliptic-eisenstein/}{\texttt{anticyclotomic-iwasawa-rational-elliptic-eisenstein}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02571}{arXiv:2008.02571}.

Let $E/\mathbb Q$ be an elliptic curve, let $p>2$ be an Eisenstein prime, and let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field satisfying the Heegner, splitting, discriminant, and Selmer hypotheses of the paper. The algebraic side is expressed through Selmer groups and local cohomology, while the analytic side uses Katz $p$-adic $L$-functions. Comparison of their Iwasawa invariants links Heegner classes to anticyclotomic $p$-adic $L$-values.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.
