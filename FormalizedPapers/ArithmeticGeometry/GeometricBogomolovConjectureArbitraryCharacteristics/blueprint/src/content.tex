\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-about}

This project follows \emph{Geometric Bogomolov conjecture in arbitrary characteristics} by Junyi Xie; Xinyi Yuan (2022). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics/}{\texttt{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.09722}{arXiv:2108.09722}.

Let $K/k$ be a finitely generated extension of algebraically closed fields and let $A/K$ be an abelian variety equipped with its canonical height. A subvariety is special when it is built from the trace and a torsion subvariety. Integral models, canonical heights, small points, Bertini arguments, and reductions in transcendence degree establish the function-field analogue of Bogomolov in arbitrary characteristic.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\begin{definition}[Special subvariety and small-point locus]
\label{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-central-object}
\source{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics}
A subvariety of $A_{\overline K}$ is central when it is special, meaning obtained from the $\overline K/k$-trace and a torsion translate; the opposing condition is density of points of arbitrarily small canonical height.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}
\label{sec:geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-results}

\begin{theorem}[Geometric Bogomolov]
\label{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-main}
\source{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics}
\uses{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-setup}
If a closed subvariety of $A_{\overline K}$ contains a dense set of small points, then it is special.
\notready
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Special-subvariety criterion]
\label{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-secondary}
\source{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics}
\uses{geometric-bogomolov-conjecture-arbitrary-characteristics-main}
The canonical-height and trace constructions identify the special subvarieties as precisely the loci for which arbitrarily small points can be Zariski dense.
\notready
\end{theorem}
