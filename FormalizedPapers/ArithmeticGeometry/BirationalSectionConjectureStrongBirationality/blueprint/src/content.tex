\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{birational-section-conjecture-strong-birationality-about}

This project follows \emph{On the birational section conjecture with strong birationality assumptions} by Giulio Bresciani (2023). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/birational-section-conjecture-strong-birationality/}{\texttt{birational-section-conjecture-strong-birationality}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13397}{arXiv:2108.13397}.

For a family of curves, étale fundamental gerbes and their relative sections encode geometric and arithmetic points. A Galois section is called $t$-birationally liftable when it admits the required gerbe-theoretic specialization diagrams. The argument controls specialization of ramified sections, lifting of $t$-birational sections, reduction to number fields, and the comparison of fundamental gerbes with fundamental groups.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.
