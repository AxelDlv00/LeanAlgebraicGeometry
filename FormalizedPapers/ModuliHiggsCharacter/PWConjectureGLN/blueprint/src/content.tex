\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{pw-conjecture-gl-n-about}

This project follows \emph{The P=W conjecture for GL_n} by Davesh Maulik; Junliang Shen (2024). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/pw-conjecture-gl-n/}{\texttt{pw-conjecture-gl-n}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02568}{arXiv:2209.02568}.

For a smooth curve and coprime degree/rank, the Dolbeault moduli space of Higgs bundles and the Betti character variety have identified cohomology. The Hitchin map defines the perverse filtration and the character variety carries a weight filtration. Strong perversity of Chern classes, vanishing cycles, twisted Hitchin systems, support theorems, and global Springer theory reduce the comparison to a finite sequence of geometric steps.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}
\label{sec:pw-conjecture-gl-n-results}

\begin{conjecture}[P=W for $\mathrm{GL}_n$]
\label{pw-conjecture-gl-n-main}
\source{pw-conjecture-gl-n}
\uses{pw-conjecture-gl-n-setup}
For all cohomological degrees and perversity indices, the perverse filtration on the Dolbeault side equals the even and odd weight filtrations on the Betti side: $P_kH^m=W_{2k}H^m=W_{2k+1}H^m$.
\notready
\end{conjecture}

\begin{theorem}[Proof of P=W]
\label{pw-conjecture-gl-n-secondary}
\source{pw-conjecture-gl-n}
\uses{pw-conjecture-gl-n-main}
The P=W conjecture for the twisted $\mathrm{GL}_n$ Hitchin system holds.
\notready
\end{theorem}
