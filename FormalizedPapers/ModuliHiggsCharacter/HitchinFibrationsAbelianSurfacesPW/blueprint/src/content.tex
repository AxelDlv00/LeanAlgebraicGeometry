\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{hitchin-fibrations-abelian-surfaces-pw-about}

This project follows \emph{Hitchin fibrations, abelian surfaces, and the P=W conjecture} by Mark de Cataldo; Davesh Maulik; Junliang Shen (2021). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/hitchin-fibrations-abelian-surfaces-pw/}{\texttt{hitchin-fibrations-abelian-surfaces-pw}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11885}{arXiv:1909.11885}.

The Dolbeault and Betti moduli spaces of Higgs bundles and local systems have canonically identified cohomology. The Hitchin map supplies a perverse filtration, while the Betti side has a mixed-Hodge weight filtration. Perverse sheaves, decomposition-theorem splittings, tautological classes, and the geometry of Hilbert schemes on abelian surfaces organize the comparison.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.
