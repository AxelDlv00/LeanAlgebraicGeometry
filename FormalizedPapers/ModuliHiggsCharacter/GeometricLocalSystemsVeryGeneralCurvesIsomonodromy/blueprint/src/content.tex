\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-about}

This project follows \emph{Geometric local systems on very general curves and isomonodromy} by Aaron Landesman; Daniel Litt (2023). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy/}{\texttt{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00039}{arXiv:2202.00039}.

A pointed hyperbolic curve carries parabolic bundles and regular-singular flat connections. Very general curves and integral local systems are considered together with their Hodge-theoretic realizations. Parabolic stability, Atiyah and parabolic Atiyah bundles, deformation cohomology, Harder--Narasimhan estimates, and isomonodromy constrain geometric local systems.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\chapter{Principal results}

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}
\label{sec:geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-results}

\begin{theorem}[Rank lower bound for geometric local systems]
\label{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-main}
\source{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy}
\uses{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-setup}
An infinite-monodromy integral local system underlying a polarizable variation of Hodge structure on an analytically very general genus-$g$ pointed curve has rank at least $2\sqrt{g+1}$.
\notready
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Small-rank isomonodromy]
\label{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-secondary}
\source{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy}
\uses{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-main}
A regular-singular flat bundle of rank less than $2\sqrt{g+1}$ whose isomonodromic deformation underlies a polarizable variation of Hodge structure has unitary monodromy.
\notready
\end{theorem}
