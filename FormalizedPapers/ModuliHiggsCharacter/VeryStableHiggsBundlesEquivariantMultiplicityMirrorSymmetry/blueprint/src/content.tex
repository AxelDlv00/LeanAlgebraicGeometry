\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry-about}

This project follows \emph{Very stable Higgs bundles, equivariant multiplicity and mirror symmetry} by Tamás Hausel; Nigel Hitchin (2022). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry/}{\texttt{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08583}{arXiv:2101.08583}.

Let $\mathcal M$ be the moduli space of stable Higgs bundles on a smooth curve with its scaling action and Hitchin fibration. The upward flow from a fixed point and the nilpotent cone encode the notion of very stability. Białynicki--Birula decompositions, Hecke transformations, and equivariant intersection multiplicities describe upward and downward flows and their mirror-symmetry interpretation.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}

\begin{theorem}[Closed upward flows]
\label{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry-main}
\source{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry}
\uses{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry-setup}
A stable fixed Higgs bundle is very stable if and only if its upward Białynicki--Birula flow is closed in the Higgs moduli space.
\notready
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Ranked chain criterion]
\label{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry-secondary}
\source{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry}
\uses{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry-main}
For a stable Higgs bundle whose underlying bundle is a chain of line bundles, very stability is equivalent to the composite Higgs section having no repeated zero.
\notready
\end{theorem}
