\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras-about}

This project follows \emph{Virasoro constraints for moduli of sheaves and vertex algebras} by Arkadij Bojko; Woonam Lim; Miguel Moreira (2024). The
retrieved source is archived under
\href{../../../../../references/virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras/}{\texttt{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras}} and is indexed in
the workspace reference manifest. The corresponding preprint is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05266}{arXiv:2210.05266}.

Let $M$ be a moduli space of sheaves on a smooth projective surface and $P$ a moduli space of stable pairs, with their virtual classes and descendent algebras. A Joyce vertex algebra, universal sheaves, descendent operators, and conformal/Virasoro elements organize the tautological insertions and reduce constraints to rank-reduction identities.

This blueprint is an independently worded, source-faithful mathematical
outline of the paper's definitions, constructions, and principal results. It
should be read alongside the original source; the source anchor on each node
points to the complete extracted TeX. Statements are intentionally marked
\notready until a matching Lean declaration and proof have been added.

\section{Main theorem or conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Virasoro constraints for sheaves]
\label{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras-main}
\source{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras}
\uses{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras-setup}
For every descendent $D$ in the sheaf descendent algebra, integration of the Virasoro transform over the virtual class of $M$ vanishes.
\notready
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Virasoro constraints for pairs]
\label{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras-secondary}
\source{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras}
\uses{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras-main}
For every nonnegative Virasoro index and every descendent $D$, the corresponding Virasoro insertion integrates to zero over the virtual class of the stable-pair moduli space.
\notready
\end{conjecture}
